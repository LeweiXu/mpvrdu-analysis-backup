Graduate students can apply to go for an exchange programme at a partner university for three
months or more as part of their graduate research programme. These exchange programmes
enable students to receive supervision and guidance from professors in our partner universities,
gain access to data that may not be readily available in NUS, as well as be exposed to graduate
training beyond NUS.
Furthermore, they allow students to expand their networks for future academic collaboration
and career development.
