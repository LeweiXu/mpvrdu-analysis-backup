Getting Started
Buttons and screen control
The watch is equipped with a color touchscreen that is highly responsive to your touches and can be swiped in different directions.
Up button
<table><tr><td>Operation</td><td>Function</td><td>Remarks</td></tr><tr><td>Press once</td><td>Wake watch screen when the screen is off.Access the app list screen from the home screen.Return to the home screen.</td><td rowspan="2">-</td></tr><tr><td>Press and hold</td><td>Power on the watch when the watch is off.Access the restart/power off screen when the watch is on.</td></tr></table>
Down button
<table><tr><td>Operation</td><td>Function</td><td>Remarks</td></tr><tr><td>Press once</td><td>The Blood pressure app is enabled by default.Access the set customized feature.</td><td>There are certain exceptions such as for calls and workouts.</td></tr><tr><td>Press and hold</td><td>Wake up the voice assistant.</td><td>Voice assistant is only supported in some countries/regions.</td></tr></table>
