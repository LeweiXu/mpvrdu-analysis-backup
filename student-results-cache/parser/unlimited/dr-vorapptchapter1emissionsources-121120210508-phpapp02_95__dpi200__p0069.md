The End Vision for ROMI
- The end vision for ROMI is that your organisation has greater visibility into the effectiveness of its marketing investment.
- The organisation must enable marketing to be more accountable through objective-based measurement.
- The organisation must ensure that its marketing disciplines, processes, metrics, tools and organizational structure are better aligned so that it has the infrastructure and capability in place to deliver great marketing.
• Underlying this entire vision must be a commitment from the organisation to improve customer and partner satisfaction.
