## TUNNEL FORMWORK SYSTEM

•Tunnel formwork system allows the contractor to cast walls and slabs in one operation in a daily cycle.

• It combines the speed, quality and accuracy of factory/off-site production with the flexibility and economy of in-situ construction • Recognized as a modern method of construction (MMC).
