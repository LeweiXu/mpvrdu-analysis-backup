# Top Itineraries

## Day One

## North Beach & Chinatown (p112)

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_55_485_107_525.jpg" alt="Image" width="5%" /></div>


Grab a leather strap on the Powell-Mason cable car and hold on: you're in for hills and thrills. Hop off at Washington Square Park, where parrots squawk encouragement for your hike up to Coit Tower for Work Projects Administration (WPA) murals and 360-degree panoramas. Take scenic Filbert Street Steps to the Embarcadero to wander across Fog Bridge and explore the freaky Tactile Dome at the Exploratorium.

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_66_776_122_832.jpg" alt="Image" width="5%" /></div>


Lunch Try local oysters and Dungeness crab at the Ferry Building (p79).

## The Marina, Fisherman's Wharf & the Piers (p48)

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_54_920_99_966.jpg" alt="Image" width="4%" /></div>


Catch your prebooked ferry to Alcatraz, where D-Block solitary raises goose bumps. Make your island-prison break, taking in Golden Gate Bridge views on the ferry ride back. Take the Powell-Mason cable car to North Beach, to take in free-speech landmark City Lights and mingle with SF's freest spirits at the Beat Museum.

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_64_1158_123_1237.jpg" alt="Image" width="5%" /></div>


Dinner Reserve North Beach's best pasta at Cotogna (p88) or a Cal-Chinese banquet at Mister Jiu's (p121).

## North Beach & Chinatown (p112)

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_55_1306_99_1349.jpg" alt="Image" width="4%" /></div>


Since you just escaped prison, you’re tough enough to handle too-close-for-comfort comics at Cobb’s Comedy Club or razor-sharp satire at Beach Blanket Babylon. Toast the wildest night in the west with potent Pisco sours at Comstock Saloon or spiked cappuccinos at Tosca Cafe.

## Day Two

## Golden Gate Park & the Avenues (p193)

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_496_512_548_552.jpg" alt="Image" width="5%" /></div>


Hop the N Judah to Golden Gate Park to see carnivorous plants enjoying insect breakfasts at the Conservatory of Flowers and spiky dahlias wet with dew in the Dahlia Garden. Follow Andy Goldsworthy's artful sidewalk fault lines to find Oceanic masks and flawless tower-top views inside the de Young Museum, then take a walk on the wild side in the rainforest dome of the California Academy of Sciences. Enjoy a moment of Zen with green tea at the Japanese Tea Garden and bliss out in the secret redwood grove at the San Francisco Botanical Garden.

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_507_880_563_935.jpg" alt="Image" width="5%" /></div>


Lunch Join surfers at Outerlands (p202) for grilled cheese and organic soup.

## Golden Gate Park & the Avenues (p193)

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_496_1022_540_1066.jpg" alt="Image" width="4%" /></div>


Beachcomb Ocean Beach up to the Beach Chalet to glimpse 1930s WPA murals celebrating Golden Gate Park. Follow the Coastal Trail past Sutro Baths and Land's End for Golden Gate Bridge vistas and priceless paper artworks at the Legion of Honor.

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_507_1216_563_1271.jpg" alt="Image" width="5%" /></div>


Dinner You've walked the coastline – now savour a seafood feast at Wako (p201).
