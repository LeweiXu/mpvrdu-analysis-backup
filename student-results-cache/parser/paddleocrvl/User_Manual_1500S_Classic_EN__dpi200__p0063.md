#### 6.3.3 Milk system rinsing

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_85_676_146_729.jpg" alt="Image" width="5%" /></div>


## Health hazard / scalding hazard

- During rinsing, hot rinsing water flows out of the comb spout.

> Never reach under the spouts while rinsing.

The milk system rinsing can be started separately. This makes sense if there are longer periods between dispensing milk beverages.

The milk system rinsing rinses the milk hose, the milk nozzles, and the comb spout.
