# FINANCING YOUR STUDY

## TUITION AND COMPULSORY MISCELLANEOUS FEES

NTU’s tuition fees are heavily subsidised by the Ministry of Education (MOE). The Tuition Grant, which is offered to all eligible full-time undergraduate students, helps to manage the costs of tertiary education in Singapore.

For Medicine Students, you are required to sign the Medical Tuition Grant Agreement with the Government of Singapore, administered by MOH Holdings (MOHH).

SINGAPORE CITIZENS

• Automatically awarded a Tuition Grant

PERMANENT RESIDENTS & INTERNATIONAL STUDENTS

• Bond-free for Singaporean except Medicine Students

• Must apply for Tuition Grant in their online university application



- Required to sign an agreement and commit to working in a Singapore entity upon graduation
