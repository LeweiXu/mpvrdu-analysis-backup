But looking at the borough or even the community district totals does not tell us enough about what kinds of neighborhoods gained or lost capacity or the characteristics of the residents of the communities that gained or lost residential capacity. To do this, we studied the socioeconomic characteristics of the census tracts in which the rezoned lots were located, and compared them to the characteristics of the median census tract in New York City. $ ^{5} $ We found several significant differences.
