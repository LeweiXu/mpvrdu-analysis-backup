# US SPACE TRAVEL A TIMELINE OF MANNED NASA FLIGHTS

On July 21, 2011, the last Space Shuttle flight landed, ending 50 years of manned NASA flights. The US space program began in 1961 and resulted in 166 nearly continuous missions, costing an estimated $7 billion per year. Space travel continues through international partnerships and unmanned missions, but it remains to be seen what is next for American space exploration.
