# Meet your International Office country advisors

Our friendly, specialist regional teams and country advisors offer advice to future international students, and their families – we also have members of staff based in China, India, Nigeria, Pakistan and Vietnam. If you would like to discuss your study plans, visit our website at ntu.ac.uk/mycountry to contact your team.

We work very closely with a worldwide network of Education Counsellors who can give advice on any aspect of studying at NTU. Visit ntu.ac.uk/representatives to find one near you.
