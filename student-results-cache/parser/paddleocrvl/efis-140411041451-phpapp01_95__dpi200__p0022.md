## Engine Sensors

## Oxygen Sensor measures the oxygen content in engine exhaust

• Mounted on the exhaust system before the catalytic converter.

• Voltage out-put of O₂ sensor changes with the change in oxygen content of exhaust.

•Lean mixture decreases the voltage.

• Rich mixture increases the voltage.
