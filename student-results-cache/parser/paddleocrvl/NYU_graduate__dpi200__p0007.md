## WHAT'S IN FOUNDERS?

## FACULTY FELLOWS IN RESIDENCE

## EXPLORATIONS

Located in Union Square/East Village, Founders is home to more than 700 first-year students. It was built on the site of the former St. Ann's church where the historic facade and fence have been preserved as an architectural feature as you enter the building. Founders offers a large outdoor courtyard, a community garden, two TV lounges, one study lounge, and spacious rooms with beautiful views. Additionally, each suite has a small microwave-refrigerator unit provided by the University to be shared by residents within the suite. Founders hosts Faculty Fellows-in-Residence with specializations in sports management and social justice and two Explorations communities focused on sustainability and health & wellness in NYC.
