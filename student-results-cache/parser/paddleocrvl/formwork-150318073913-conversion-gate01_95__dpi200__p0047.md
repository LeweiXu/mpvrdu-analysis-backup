## Safety

•Metal formwork systems can have integral ready-to-use concreting platforms with guard rails and access equipment including ladders. This reduces the need for independent access.

•For systems with disposable formwork, working platforms for concreting have to be erected separately to allow safe access to the top of the column forms. •Formwork systems are available which need to be worked only from one side. This could be an important safety consideration for columns situated at building edges and corners. •Metal systems typically provide robust assemblies.

•Assembly process is simple allowing site operatives to become familiar with health and safety aspects.

•Normally these formwork systems require minimal use of power tools.
