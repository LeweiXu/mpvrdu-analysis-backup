## ANATOMY OF ESOPHAGUS

- Flattened muscular tube, size 18 to 26cm beginning at lower border of cricoid cartilage(opp 6 $ ^{th} $ cervical vertebra) and ending at cardiac orifice of stomach(opp 11 $ ^{th} $ cervical vertebra)

• Divided into 3 anatomical segments i.e.,cervical,thoracic & abdominal
