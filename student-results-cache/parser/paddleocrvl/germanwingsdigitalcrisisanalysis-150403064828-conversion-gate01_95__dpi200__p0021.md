## TRENDS AND BEHAVIOURS

Within 90 minutes – and despite lack of immediate information – political leaders were quick to react and provide comment on the incident.

+ Francois Hollande was the first of the world’s political leaders to comment on the incident.

While the French Civil Aviation Authorities have been active through non-digital channels from the outset (e.g. statement to the media), their social media presence was limited in the first hours. A first tweet came almost ~6 hours after the crash. (17:31 CET)
