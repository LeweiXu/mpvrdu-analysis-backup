## EMBRYOLOGY OF PHARYNX

• Head & neck structures are derived from pharyngeal arches 1 & 2.

• Each arch contain similar component derived from endoderm, ectoderm & mesoderm.

• The cavity within the pharyngeal arches forms the pharynx.
