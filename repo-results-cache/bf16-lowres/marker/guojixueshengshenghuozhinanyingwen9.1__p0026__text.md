## **On-Campus Hospital**

Tsinghua University has its own campus hospital located in the western part of the campus.

The hours of operation for various departments are as follows. Outpatient services time: Monday to Friday; 8:00am – 12:00pm, 1:30pm – 5:00pm Registration for outpatient services: Monday to Friday; 7:45am – 11:50am, 1:20pm – 4:50pm Emergency services: 24 hours
