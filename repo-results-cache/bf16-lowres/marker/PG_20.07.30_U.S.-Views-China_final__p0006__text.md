These are among the findings of a new survey by Pew Research Center, conducted June 16 to July 14, 2020, among 1,003 adults in the United States. The survey also finds that while Republicans and Democrats both have negative views of China and are critical of Beijing's handling of the coronavirus, this criticism is more prevalent among Republicans. Republicans and Republicanleaning independents are significantly more likely than Democrats and Democratic leaners to have a *very* unfavorable view of China, to criticize the Chinese government's role in the global pandemic, and they often want to take a tougher policy approach to the country.
