## **Mobile E-commerce (1)**

- Vserv.mobi states that almost 30% of e-commerce traffic in Asia
  Pacific come from smartphones and tablets.
- Indonesian e-commerce website, lojai.com, recorded almost 20% of their sales come from mobile on May 2014.
- Tokobagus/OLX recorded 800% growth on their Android app in 2013.
- Rakuten managed to grow 438% on mobile during Apr-Dec 2012.
- There are still plenty of BBM Group "online shops" especially for fashion and apparels, in addition to Instagram and Facebook "shops" (F-commerce).
