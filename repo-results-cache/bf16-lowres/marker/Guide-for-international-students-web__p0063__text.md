## **International Scholarships**

We award many scholarships to international students who have offers to study at NTU. These are highly prestigious, competitive awards, worth up to half of your tuition fee. Our scholars have gone on to become strong and highly valued ambassadors for the University.

All scholarships available are listed on our website, with application advice and deadlines. If you're progressing from one of our partner universities, you may be eligible for a partner progression discount. You can still apply for a scholarship, but you won't be awarded both – the higher amount would be deducted from your tuition fees.
