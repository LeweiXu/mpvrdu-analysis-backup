Note: All Bachelor of Engineering programmes are accredited by the Institution of Engineers Singapore, the Singapore signatory of the Washington Accord, through its Engineering Accreditation Board. The Washington Accord is an international agreement for mutual recognition of the substantial equivalence of engineering academic programmes in satisfying the academic requirements for the practice of engineering at the professional level.

A Minor in Business and a Minor in International Trading are offered to all single degree engineering programmes except Data Science & Artificial Intelligence and Maritime Studies.
