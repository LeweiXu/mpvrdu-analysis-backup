The World Health Organization (WHO) has been a key player in [addressing the global spread of](https://www.nytimes.com/2020/04/16/health/WHO-Trump-coronavirus.html)  [the coronavirus,](https://www.nytimes.com/2020/04/16/health/WHO-Trump-coronavirus.html) [which the organization characterized as a pandemic](https://www.who.int/dg/speeches/detail/who-director-general-s-opening-remarks-at-the-media-briefing-on-covid-19---11-march-2020) in early March. But the organization has been [heavily criticized by](https://www.nytimes.com/2020/04/07/us/politics/coronavirus-trump-who.html) 

[President Donald Trump](https://www.nytimes.com/2020/04/07/us/politics/coronavirus-trump-who.html) in recent weeks. In mid-April, he halted U.S. [funding of the](https://www.nytimes.com/2020/04/14/us/politics/coronavirus-trump-who-funding.html)  [organization](https://www.nytimes.com/2020/04/14/us/politics/coronavirus-trump-who-funding.html) – a move [sharply criticized by top](https://www.npr.org/sections/coronavirus-live-updates/2020/04/26/845151290/speaker-pelosi-president-trumps-effort-to-sideline-the-who-is-dangerous)  [Democrats.](https://www.npr.org/sections/coronavirus-live-updates/2020/04/26/845151290/speaker-pelosi-president-trumps-effort-to-sideline-the-who-is-dangerous)

Americans' views of how well the WHO has dealt with the coronavirus outbreak also fall along partisan lines. Whereas 62% of Democrats and Democratic-leaning independents say the organization has done at least a good job in handling the global pandemic, only 28% of Republicans and GOP leaners say the same. Conservative Republicans are less likely to applaud the organization's handling of the virus than moderate or liberal Republicans.

Americans with a postgraduate education are somewhat more likely than those with less education to applaud the international organization's handling of the outbreak, though the differences are muted. Younger Americans also approve of the WHO's performance more than older Americans: 52% of adults under age 30 say it has done excellent or good job, compared with just 39% of those 65 and older.
