Process for seeing a doctor at the hospital
- Register at the registration area (in main building/lobby)
- Go to the medical department you registered at (i.e. internal medicine, surgical medicine, dental medicine)
- Go to the medical consultation area to be assigned to a doctor based on the symptoms felt
- Be examined by a doctor
- Obtain examination/test results
- Given laboratory/examination test script
- Given a medical prescription
- Given a treatment report
- Given a diagnosis/hospital transfer notice
- Go to respective examination room
- Pay for medical prescription/script at payment area
- Pick up prescription medicine
- Go to injection room, or have intravenous drip
- Go to respective treatment room
- Leave hospital
