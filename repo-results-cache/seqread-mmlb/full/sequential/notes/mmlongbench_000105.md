## Chapter 2.2 Museums (Continued)

- **22 THE HANSEATIC MUSEUM AND SCHØTSTUENE**
  - Location: Øvregaten 50 / NO-5003
  - Description: Explore Bryggen as the Hanseatic merchants knew it. Includes German merchants' assembly rooms, Schøtstueene, and UNESCO World Heritage site Bryggen. Guided tours available in summer. Activities for children and garden seats.
  - Contact: +47 53 00 61 10 / hanseatikemuseum.museumvest.no/

- **23 EDVARD GRIEG MUSEUM TROLDHAUGEN**
  - Location: Troldhaugveien 65 / NO-5232
  - Description: World-famous composer Edvard Grieg's villa preserved as it was in 1907. Includes composer's cabin, burial site, concert hall, and museum. Daily concerts from 15 June to 21 August.
  - Contact: +47 930 19 590 / troldhaugen.no/

- **24 FJELL FORT**
  - Location: Festningsvegen / NO-5357
  - Description: Situated ~25 km from Bergen, one of Norway’s most important WWII strongholds guarding Bergen from the west. Well-preserved. Guided tours available.
  - Contact: +47 53 00 61 23 / fjellfestning.museumvest.no/

- **25 OLD BERGEN MUSEUM**
  - Location: Nyhavnsveien 4 / NO-5042
  - Description: Living museum reconstructing 19th-20th century Bergen. Unique collection of ~50 original wooden buildings. Beautiful English-style park and seawater pool.
  - Contact: +47 55 30 80 34 / 55 30 80 30 / bymuseet.no/

- **26 HARALD SÆVERUD – MUSEUM SILJUŠTØL**
  - Location: Siljustveien 50 / NO-5236
  - Description: Home of composer Harald Sæverud and wife Marie. Now a museum set in a beautiful 44-acre area perfect for walks. Focus on music and nature.
  - Contact: +47 55 92 29 92 / siljustol.no/

- **27 HERDLA MUSEUM**
  - Location: Herdla fort / NO-5315
  - Description: Small island museum in the archipelago west of Bergen. Exhibits about WWII, rich fauna/birdlife. Main attraction: German fighter aircraft from WWII. Great for walks, fishing, bird-watching.
  - Contact: +47 970 94 729 / herdlamuseum.museumvest.no/

- **28 HAAKON'S HALL**
  - Location: Bergenhus Festning / NO-5003
  - Description: 13th-century royal banquet hall, largest and most imposing stone building of its kind. Now a living cultural heritage site. Separate exhibition program.
  - Contact: +47 55 30 80 36 / 55 30 80 30 / bymuseet.no/

- **29 KODE ART MUSEUMS OF BERGEN**
  - Location: Rasmus Meyers allé 3, 7 og 9, Nordahl Bruns gate 9 / NO-5015
  - Description: Norway’s second biggest art collection in four museums in the city center. Features works from 15th century to present. Dedicated KunstLab for children. One ticket for all museums.
  - Contact: +47 53 00 97 04 / kodebergen.no/
