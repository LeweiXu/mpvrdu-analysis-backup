This page presents data on attitudes toward robot caregivers, specifically comparing those who would want to use one versus those who would not.

- **Interest in human monitoring**: 48% of Americans would feel better about the concept of a robot caregiver if a human monitored it via camera. 20% would feel worse, and 32% would be indifferent.
