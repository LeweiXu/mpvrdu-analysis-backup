## Throttle Body Injection (TBI) System Description

- Uses one or two injectors.
- Injectors spray fuel into the top of the throttle body air horn.
- Atomized fuel mixes with air and is drawn into the engine.
- Fuel pressure regulator is spring loaded and part of the housing.
- Fuel is injected whenever the engine is running, also called CIS: Continuous Injection System.
