## PAYMENTS LANDSCAPE

- With the increasing digital payments penetration, the share of COD shipments is reducing
- With increasing order values, we are seeing an uptick of EMI payments
- 3rd party wallets albeit a new phenomenon, have a strong value proposition and will be quick to become popular – similar to China
- By 2016, half of Indians will have debit card!

### Online Retail payments in India
Bar chart showing the share of different payment methods in 2013 and 2016P (projected):
- COD: 60% (2013) → 50% (2016P)
- Credit Cards: 16% (2013) → 12% (2016P)
- Debit Cards: 12% (2013) → 15% (2016P)
- Net banking: 12% (2013) → 11% (2016P)
- EMI: 1% (2013) → 5% (2016P)
- 3rd party wallets: 0% (2013) → 7% (2016P)

### Number of Debit Card users in India (In millions)
Bar chart showing the number of debit card users from 2014 to 2016:
- 2014: 399 million
- 2015: 490.77 million
- 2016: 584.02 million (45% of Indians)

Source: RBI Website and Accel Reports
