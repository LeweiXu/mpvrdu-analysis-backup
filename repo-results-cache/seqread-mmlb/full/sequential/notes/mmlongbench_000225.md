## Socializing frequency among older adults
- 81% of older adults who use social networking sites say they socialize on a daily or near-daily basis.
- 71% of older adults who go online but do not use social networking sites socialize on a daily or near-daily basis.
- 63% of older adults who are not online at all socialize on a daily or near-daily basis.

## About this survey
- Data from telephone interviews conducted July 18 to September 30, 2013.
- Sample: 6,010 adults ages 18 and older.
- Margin of error for those ages 65 or older (n=1,526): ±2.9 percentage points.
