## Women working in STEM are more likely to have experienced gender-related discrimination at work

| Type of Discrimination | Men in STEM jobs | Women in STEM jobs |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Earned less than a woman/man doing same job | 6 | 29 |
| Were treated as if they were not competent because of their gender | 4 | 29 |
| Experienced repeated, small slights at work | 4 | 20 |
| Received less support from senior leaders than a man who was doing the same job | 9 | 18 |
| Felt isolated in the workplace | 5 | 11 |
| Been passed over for most important assignments | 4 | 9 |
| Been turned down for a job | 4 | 7 |
| Been denied a promotion | 4 | 6 |

Note: Respondents who gave other responses or who did not give an answer are not shown. Source: Survey of U.S. adults conducted July 11-Aug. 10, 2017.
"Women and Men in STEM Often at Odds Over Workplace Equity"
PEW RESEARCH CENTER
