## Text:
- The share of Latinos who rate their personal finances as “excellent” or “good” has nearly doubled since the Great Recession.
- In 2008, only 4% of Latinos reported being in excellent financial shape and 19% said their financial condition was good.
- In 2015, 8% of Latinos reported being in excellent financial shape and 33% said their financial condition was good.
- The latest Hispanic survey also suggests that the share of Hispanics with a positive view of their pocketbook finances has essentially caught up to the general public: 43% of the general public versus 40% of Hispanics.
