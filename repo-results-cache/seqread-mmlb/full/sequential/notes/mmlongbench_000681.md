Teaching Hours
All ISEP students have to fulfil teaching duties as part of their coursework requirement.
- ISEP students who are Singaporeans and Singapore PRs have to fulfil a total of 40 hours of teaching duties. This is normally to be completed by the end of the 2nd year of enrolment at ISEP. The teaching requirements should be fulfilled within the academic settings of NUS or where appropriate and subject to approval, other institutions within Singapore. There are different options of clocking the teaching hours, as listed in Table 1.
- ISEP students who are International Students are required to perform duties of 312 hours, under the Graduate Assistantship Programme (GAP). Please refer to Table 2, which shows the maximum hours that can be clocked under the respective categories.

| 1. | Undergraduate Teaching | 40 hours | • Compulsory for all ISEP students to participate in undergraduate teaching in NUS.<br>• Normally to be completed by the end of the 2nd year of enrolment at ISEP.<br>• Feedback will be required within 4 weeks of the teaching being completed.<br>• No MCs will be allocated for teaching.<br>• Marking hours cannot be used as part of teaching hours. Only the direct teaching or demonstrating hours can be clocked |

## ISEP Buddy Scheme Details

- It is mandatory for students to participate in the Buddy System.
- Senior must meet with freshmen at least 2 times in that academic year (~1x per semester) and obtain satisfactory feedback.
- **Note: Only 10 hours can be claimed regardless of how many freshmen the senior has.**
- The Online Buddy Report Form must be submitted once every semester in the freshmen's first year of study.
- 5 hours will be uploaded to the Senior's teaching milestone at the end of each semester (up to a max of 10 hours).
- ISEP reserves the right to revoke the 5 hours uploaded if the senior does not fulfill duties or submit required forms.
