Figure 1 is presented, showing AUC scores for various active querying strategies (BALD, Consistency, Coreset, Margin, VAAL, Entropy) and Random selection across three datasets: PathMNIST, OrganAMNIST, and CIFAR-10. The figure demonstrates that most active querying strategies are outperformed by random selection in the initial stages (low budget regime) due to selection bias, where some classes are not selected for training. The caption and text explain that this is the "cold start problem" in vision active learning. The figure also highlights that active learning tends to select data biased toward specific classes, leading to unbalanced class distributions in the selected queries, especially in imbalanced datasets like medical images. The text further discusses two causes of this problem: (i) biased query (selection bias toward majority classes) and (ii) outlier query (reliance on trained classifiers at the start of active learning). The figure is used to introduce the motivation for the paper's proposed solution, which involves using label diversity and contrastive learning to address these issues.
