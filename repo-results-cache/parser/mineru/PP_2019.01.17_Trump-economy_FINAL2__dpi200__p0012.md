Trump's impact on economy seen as positive, on balance
While the public is critical of Trump and his administration in multiple areas, they see Trump's
impact on the economy in a positive light. Overall, 40% think that Trump's policies have made
economic conditions better since taking office, compared with fewer (28%) who say they have
made conditions worse; 29% say they have not had much of an effect.
Since October 2017, the share saying Trump's economic policies have not had much of an effect
