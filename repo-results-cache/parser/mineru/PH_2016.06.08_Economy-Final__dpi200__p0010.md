For many Latinos,
family income is not
keeping pace with the
cost of living
Two other Pew Research
Center surveys of U.S. adults
conducted in 2014 and 2015
show that many Hispanics
say their family income is
falling behind the cost of
living.
In 2015, about half (53%) of
Latinos said their family
income is not keeping up
with the cost of living.
Meanwhile, 37% said their
income is staying about even
with the cost of living, and
10% said it is going up faster
than the cost of living. In
