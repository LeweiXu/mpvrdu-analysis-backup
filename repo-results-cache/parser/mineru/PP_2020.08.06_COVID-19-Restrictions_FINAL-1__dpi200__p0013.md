Public gives the country's handling of the pandemic negative marks
A majority of Americans say the nation's response to the pandemic compares poorly to how other
affluent countries have responded: 62% say the U.S. has been less effective than other wealthy
countries in responding to the coronavirus
outbreak, a quarter say the U.S. response has
been about as effective as these other nations
and just 13% of Americans say the U.S.
response has been more effective than that of
other wealthy countries.
