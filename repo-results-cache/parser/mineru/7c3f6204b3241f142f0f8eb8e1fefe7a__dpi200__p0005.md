The Standard of Review *7 The standard of review on appeal is whether PIC's decision is supported by substantial evidence on the record. \( {}^{\mathrm{{FN20}}} \) Substantial evidence is that which "a reasonable mind might accept as adequate to support a conclusion." \( {}^{\mathrm{{FN21}}} \) It is more than a scintilla, but less than a preponderance of the evidence. \( {}^{\mathrm{{FN22}}} \) It is a low standard to affirm and a high standard to over- turn. If the record contains substantial evidence, then the Court is prohibited from re-weighing the evidence or substituting its judgment for that of the agency. \( {}^{\mathrm{{FN23}}} \) FN20. 29 Del.C. § 5810A.
