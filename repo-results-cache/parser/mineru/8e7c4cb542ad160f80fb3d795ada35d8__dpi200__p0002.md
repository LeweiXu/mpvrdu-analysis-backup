How Have Recent Rezonings Affected the City's Ability to Grow?
Our research looks at the impact of 76 rezonings that took place between 2003 and 2007, the most recent year for which we had data when we began this research. We focus only on lots that already were, or would be (by 2007), zoned to permit residential use.
