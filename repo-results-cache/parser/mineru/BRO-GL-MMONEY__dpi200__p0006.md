The portfolio overview
screen allows you to
trade directly from
this screen. Real-time
portfolio balances
can be viewed on the
Portfolio Overview page
by clicking the
Live Balances
button, providing up-to-
the-minute information.
Short-cut trading capabilities allow you to trade
across as many companies, accounts and funds
as you'd like - saving you valuable time.
The patent-pending shopping cart allows
you to create and save trades for future
execution. Your cart will retain your trades
as long as you are logged in to the platform
and can be edited at any time. With a built-in
aggregation tool, you can see what is in your
queue before you execute trades.
