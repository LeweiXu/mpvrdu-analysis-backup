Keeping in contact with NTU International
There are lots of ways for you to get in touch with us and find out more about studying at NTU.
![](images/0.jpg)

![](images/1.jpg)

Sign up to receive messages
→ Visit ntu.ac.uk/messages to register your contact details.
A member of our international team will contact you to discuss your study options, and we'll keep in touch with regular news and updates.
![](images/2.jpg)

Get your questions answered
Our international enquiries team are highly experienced in answering questions from international students and their families. You can email them at international@ntu.ac.uk.
Visit ntu.ac.uk/questions to find answers to things we get asked a lot throughout the year. You can also live chat with the team on this page.
