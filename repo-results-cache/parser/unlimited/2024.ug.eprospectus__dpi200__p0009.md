ABOUT
Unlock your full potential by joining one of the world's most prestigious Engineering colleges. The College of Engineering in NTU offers a topnotch curriculum, a host of internship opportunities and a Global Immersion programme crafted to broaden your perspective and equip you for a successful career.
OUR
SCHOOLS
• School of Chemistry, Chemical Engineering and Biotechnology*
• School of Civil and Environmental Engineering
• School of Computer Science and Engineering
• School of Electrical and Electronic Engineering
• School of Materials Science and Engineering
• School of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering
*Jointly managed by College of Engineering and College of Science
Note: All Bachelor of Engineering programmes are accredited by the Institution of Engineers Singapore, the Singapore signatory of the Washington Accord, through its Engineering Accreditation Board. The Washington Accord is an international agreement for mutual recognition of the substantial equivalence of engineering academic programmes in satisfying the academic requirements for the practice of engineering at the professional level.
A Minor in Business and a Minor in International Trading are offered to all single degree engineering programmes except Data Science & Artificial Intelligence and Maritime Studies.
