SOLE PLATE
The purpose of a sole plate is to transfer vertical load from one vertical support member to the foundation
The vertical support requires a base plate fitted to help distribute the load to the sole plate, which is usually a sleeve.
If the sole plate is positioned on natural ground, it is important the ground is graded and consolidated to a level surface.
The objective is to achieve maximum bearing beneath sole plate. Once sole plates are positioned and firmly be added they should be checked for level.
