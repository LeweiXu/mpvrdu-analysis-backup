TIR: stands for total internal reflection
TIR occurs when the angle of light is larger than the critical angle
The critical angle is the angle at which the the wave is neither reflected or refracted
If the angle:
-is bigger than the critical angle the light is reflected
-is smaller than the critical angle the light is refracted
If all the ave is reflected then it is called Total Internal Reflection
