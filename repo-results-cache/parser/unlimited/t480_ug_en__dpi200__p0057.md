11 System lock: Use the system lock in the following ways:

- When the system lock is in the unlocked position \( ^{2} \) , the latch is unlocked, and you can attach or detach your computer.
- When the system lock is in the unlocked position \(3^{\mathrm{R}}\), you can remove the system lock from the system-lock slot.
Note: The ThinkPad Basic Docking Station does not ship with a system lock installed. You can contact Lenovo sales to purchase a system lock with the option part number 4XE0Q56388.
