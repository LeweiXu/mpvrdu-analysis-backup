Wearing the watch
To ensure the accuracy of the measurement, place the watch body in the middle of the back of your wrist. The edge of the watch's body should be below the root of the ulnar styloid process, and should not press the root of the ulnar styloid process or be too far away from it. The center of the watch's face should be on your wrist about two fingers width away from the palm.
