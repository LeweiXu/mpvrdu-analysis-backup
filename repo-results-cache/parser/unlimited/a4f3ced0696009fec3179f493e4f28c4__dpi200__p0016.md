V.
For these reasons, we REVERSE the district court's grant of summary judgment to the Libertarian Party on its First and Fourteenth Amendment claim and its denial of summary judgment to the Secretary on that claim. We AFFIRM the district court's summary judgment ruling on the Libertarian Party's equal protection claim. We VACATE the district court's injunction and REMAND for further proceedings consistent with this opinion.
