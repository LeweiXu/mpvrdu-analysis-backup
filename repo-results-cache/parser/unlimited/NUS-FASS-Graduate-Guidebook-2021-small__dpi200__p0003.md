Graduate Studies
@FASS
FASS offers a wide range of graduate programmes through coursework and research, catering to a variety of interests. These programmes offer excellent opportunities for students to further develop their potential as intellectual leaders in many fields.
Programmes by coursework are designed for professional development and lead to a Graduate Diploma, Doctoral or Masters. Programmes by research equip students to work at great depth at the frontiers of knowledge creation. These programmes, which include both coursework and a thesis, lead to a Masters or PhD degree.
For some disciplines, PhD students may submit an article-based PhD thesis as an alternative route to completing a PhD thesis. Depending on the department, students will be required to submit two to three journal articles and an analytical commentary of 8,000 - 12,000 words, more information can be obtained from:
fass.nus.edu.sg/graduate
