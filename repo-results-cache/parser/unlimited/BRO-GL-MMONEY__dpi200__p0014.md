Audit made easier
Transaction history
The platform's transaction history view provides you with up to two years of past transactions. All information is available for export to Excel or PDF and contains critical audit details, including inputter and approver fields. History can be sorted on almost any dimension and can be filtered at the account, company or account group level.
![](images/0.jpg)

The entire audit history is exportable to PDF, Excel or CSV.
![](images/1.jpg)

Convenient filter functionality is built in to the transaction history widget at a top level, providing you with the capability to filter down your account history across a number of factors.
![](images/2.jpg)

You can also filter or sort each column, reorder columns and turn on/off display information. After you log out, your preferred views will be saved when you next visit the platform.
