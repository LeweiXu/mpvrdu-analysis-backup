Measurement – What Activities Should You Measure?
Most marketers focus largely on:
1. Measuring Reach and Response
2. Measuring Sales
3. Not enough measuring Engagement, End Actions, Conversion and Velocity and looking at it end-to-end
![](images/0.jpg)

Delivered Impressions, Page Views, Unique Visitors, Win / Conversions – high-level measures. But they are not very insightful in isolation and without looking at engagement.
