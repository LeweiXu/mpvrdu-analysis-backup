A Marketing Taxonomy...
1. ...provides a common language to enable integration of tools and processes.
2. ...provides increased organizational transparency across planning, execution, measurement and reporting.
3. ...enables more productive and effective marketer to customer interactions via integrated tools/process and increased transparency.
4. ...enables better BI through standardized customer responses.
5. ...reflects the changing needs and behaviors of our customers.
