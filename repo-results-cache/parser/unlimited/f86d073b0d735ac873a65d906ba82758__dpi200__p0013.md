The ECS mandate form can be furnished by ISC on request or can be downloaded from the Company's corporate website www.itcportal.com under the section 'Investor Relations'.
Nomination Facility
Shareholders who hold Shares in the physical form and wish to make / change nomination in respect of their shareholding in the Company, as permitted under Section 109A of the Companies Act, 1956, may submit to ISC the prescribed Form 2B. This Form can be furnished by ISC on request or can be downloaded from the Company's corporate website www.itcportal.com under the section 'Investor Relations'.
