Marketers Claim Customer Segmentation Extends Broadly
Mass, direct and digital communications aren't integrated.
- 27% of marketers say that their marketing is integrated across brand, direct and digital communications.
- 73% struggle to understand the impact that brand, mass, direct and digital communication have on each other and therefore don’t plan programs and campaigns to maximise their combined effectiveness.
"We compare data between brand and direct teams, but it's not really integrated. Customers often tell us that they see an offer in one medium and receive a different offer in another. Customer get frustrated."
A senior marketer at a financial services company.
