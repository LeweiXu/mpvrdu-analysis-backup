Just to quickly summarize what we have covered:

1. God's kingdom is a family.

2. God is our Father and we are His children.

3. Our identity and value as individuals is based on our relationship to God.

4. The kingdom of God is a relational kingdom based on the relationships between us and God and between each other.

5. These relationships are safeguarded by the Ten Commandments.

6. The breaking of the Commandments destroys our identity and value.
