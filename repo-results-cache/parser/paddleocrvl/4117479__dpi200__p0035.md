## Description of Business

## Company Ownership and Management:

Beau will manage all day-to-day operations and administrative duties. A second full time employee will also be hired immediately to help with administrative duties and day-to-day operations. A part time accountant will be hired to help with the finances and record keeping. As business grows, staff will be brought on full time to work at the different entities under the Sparta Bottling Co. umbrella.

## Location:

The old police station is a great facility for several reasons. Perhaps the best being its shear size which allows us to seat many people for food service and allow the building to be rented for large weddings, concerts, and events. It has a nice location just a half a block off Water Street, surrounded by other historic and beautiful buildings. A public parking lot across the street will also be great for accommodating busy weekends. The empty lot on the east side of the building also has potential for use once such discussions are had between owners of said properties. The front and back of the building also have a generous amount of space for outdoor seating and games and the large garage doors will draw a ton of attention once all the doors are changed over to all glass garage doors.
