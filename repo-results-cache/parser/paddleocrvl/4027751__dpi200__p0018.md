#### 2.2.2 Servo Constraints

The factors that determine a workspace area of the robotic arm are its own length from the base up as well as the rotational limitations of the joints. This means that the mechanical constraints as well as servo constraints were imminent. To understand and solve the issue, many tests were undertaken to identify the servo’s rotational limitations. Manual adjustments had to be made to increase the degrees of rotation of each servo and increase the overall range of motion of the arm. Thanks to these tests the workspace area was significantly increased.
