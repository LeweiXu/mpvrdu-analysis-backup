Exposure of T6 treated 319 aluminum alloy to elevated temperatures (180 and 250 °C) also led to thermal growth, albeit to a lesser degree than for the T4 temper. Thermal growth for the alloy in the T6 condition was attributed to the replacement of the  $ \theta $ phase with  $ \theta' $ due to continued aging [54]. The precipitation of the  $ \theta' $ phase in place of  $ \theta $ resulted in a dimensional expansion as shown in Figure 22 [53].
