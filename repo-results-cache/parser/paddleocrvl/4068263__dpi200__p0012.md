# Magnificent views deserve perfect frames.

Scenic expanses deserve the wide views available with the Aspire Series slider window. Because of its horizontal shape, the slider window is the perfect architectural solution to broad vistas or landscapes. And, with a touch of your finger, the window glides open allowing fresh air to fill the room.

## BRAINS AND BEAUTY

All-metal locks securely fasten the window together ensuring protection from the outside and the elements. The durable material and contemporary design complement the style of any home. With flush mounted locks that tilt the sash in, the Aspire Series sliding window can be easily removed for cleaning.

## SLIDING WITH EASE

Whether it is the crisp mountain air, the salty tang of the ocean or the fragrant scent of cut grass, the Aspire slider window brings the outdoors in with a touch of your fingers. The bottom of the unit glides along the vinyl track ensuring a smooth and enduring movement for years to come.
