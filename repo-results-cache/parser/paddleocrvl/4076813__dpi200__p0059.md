## Engineering Submittals

Prior to scheduling a pre-development meeting, familiarize yourself with the ordinances and standards used in the Town of Little Elm. The Subdivision Ordinance and the Engineering Design Manual are online for your convenience.

Required content for the civil plans can be found on page 61 of this Handbook. Plan submittal is completely electronic in Little Elm. Contact the Planning or Engineering staff for the Hightail link. Establish a project manager profile on the MyGov system and pay the fees.

Review generally takes up to three weeks for the first review and two weeks for any subsequent review. Once plans are approved, the project can be released for construction, which starts the second time period – that of construction, inspection, and acceptance.

Prior to the Pre-Con meeting, the Town Engineer will advise the applicant on the items to bring and the number of each for the participants at the meeting. Permits generally necessary for construction are:

• Floodplain Development Permit

• Clearing/Grubbing/Earthwork/Grading Permit

• Public Works Construction Permit

• Building Permit

• TxDOT Dallas District Permit

• SWP3

• Erosion Control Plan

• Notice of Intent (NOI)

All permits must be on premises at all times during construction.

When utilities and paving are complete and final stabilization nears completion, a Pre-Walk Inspection of the site can be scheduled. This will indicate any unfinished or sub-par construction necessary to be remedied prior to final acceptance.

When the punch-list is complete, a Final Walk-Through can be scheduled for the final approval and acceptance of public infrastructure by the Town. Final acceptance and project closure requires a Notice of Termination (NOT), which will precede any release of a full certificate of occupancy (CO).
