# ANNEXE V: QUADRIGA INVESTORS – GRANTIA ANPHORA

# Characteristics of the Sub-Fund

## Investment Objective and Policy

The objective of Quadriga Investors – Grantia Anphora (the “Sub-Fund” in the present section) to provide investors absolute return through primarily investing in the major world FX pair of currencies.

On an ancillary basis, the Sub-Fund will also invest in listed equities and fixed income securities denominated in major world currencies.

The Sub-Fund operates mainly by implementing a directional long short strategy on the Sub-Fund’s asset classes or indices thereof. Short exposure will be achieved through the use of derivative instruments. The Sub-Fund's long positions will be sufficiently liquid to cover at all times the Sub-Fund's obligations arising from its short positions.

The fixed income securities will comprise amongst other: (i) securities issued or guaranteed by Member States and Non-Member States, their sub-divisions, agencies or instrumentalities, and (ii) corporate debt securities and corporate commercial paper. Fixed income securities may have fixed, variable, or floating rates of interest, and may vary inversely with respect to a reference rate.

The Sub-Fund shall not invest in shares of other sub-funds, UCITS and/or other UCIs, including for the avoidance of doubts open-ended exchange traded funds (ETF), above 10% of the net assets of the Sub-Fund.

Subject to the applicable conditions and limits set out in section “Investment restrictions” the Sub-Fund may invest on an ancillary basis in cash, cash equivalent and/or deposits. Under exception al circumstances, the Sub-Fund may be invested up to 100% in cash or cash equivalent (including money market instruments).

The Sub-Fund may hold both non-EUR denominated securities and non-EUR denominated currency positions. Therefore, movements in both non-EUR denominated securities and non-EUR denominated currencies can influence the Sub-Fund’s return. Currency hedging and trading may be implemented using spot and forward foreign exchange contracts and currency futures, options or swaps.

The Sub-Fund will be actively managed.

## Use of derivatives or other investment techniques and instruments

The Sub-Fund will enter in financial derivatives instruments, either listed or OTC, such as, but not limited to, futures and contract for differences, for both, investment or hedging purposes.

To achieve its objective, the Sub-Fund may enter into efficient portfolio transactions such as repurchase agreements and reverse repurchase agreements.
