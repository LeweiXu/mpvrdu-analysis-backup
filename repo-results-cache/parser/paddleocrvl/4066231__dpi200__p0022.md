# Part II - Description of the sub-funds

## ANNEXE I: QUADRIGA INVESTORS – BELGRAVIA LYNX

## Characteristics of the Sub-Fund

## Investment Objective and Policy

The objective of Quadriga Investors – Belgravia Lynx (the “Sub-Fund” in the present section) is to seek superior risk adjusted returns associated with exposure to the equity of companies which, in the opinion of the Investment Manager, are mispriced relative to their inherent or embedded value.

In seeking to achieve its objective, the Sub-Fund will mainly invest in equity securities, financial derivatives instruments, fixed-income securities issued or guaranteed by Member States, their sub-divisions, agencies or instrumentalities and money market instruments, without prejudice of the limits and conditions laid down in the prospectus.

The Sub-Fund has no index tracking objective and therefore does not use any replication method.

The primary issuers which the Sub-Fund may be exposed to include issuers located all OECD members (mainly Europe). The Sub-Fund may also be exposed up to 10% of its net assets in issuers located in emerging countries.

The Sub-Fund shall not invest in shares of other sub-funds, UCITS and/or other UCIs above 10% of the net assets of the Sub-Fund.

The Sub-Fund will be actively managed.

## Use of derivatives or other investment techniques and instruments

The Sub-Fund will invest in financial derivatives instruments for the purpose of achieving its investment objectives.

## Risk Management Process

The global risk exposure will be calculated by using the absolute VAR approach. The absolute VaR of the Sub-Fund may not exceed 20% of the Net Asset Value of the Sub-Fund using a confidence interval of 99% and a holding period of one month.

The expected level of leverage based on the sum of all notional amounts will be approximately 300% of the net asset value of the Sub-Fund. Higher levels of leverage are however possible

## Investor Profile

Investment in this Sub-Fund is suitable for investors seeking a stable income with moderate level of volatility.
