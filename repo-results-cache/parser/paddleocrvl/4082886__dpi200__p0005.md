## PREFACE TO MONOGRAPH

## INVESTMENT FOR THE NEXT 65 YEARS OF EXCELLENCE OF ENERGOPROJEKT

Founded on July 11, 1951, as a small bureau for the design of hydro and thermal power plants, ENERGOPROJEKT has evolved into a world renowned complex business system for design, consulting, engineering and construction of complex infrastructural and other capital projects. The company has been proving its vitality and ability to adapt to changes in the business environment at home as well as in more than seventy countries abroad by successfully completing different projects and building the brand that has become a synonym for a highly professional and reliable partner.

The growth and development of ENERGOPROJEKT has always been primarily based on its employees who have been professionally leading and managing all business objectives and projects in a long-term and comprehensive manner. Inspiration for successful achievement of future goals is built on a wealth of experience from the previous sixty-five years of business – invariably available knowledge and experience of a number of engineers, economists, lawyers, and mostly technicians and workers who have been in a highly professional, competent and self-sacrificing manner successfully managing companies and projects at home and abroad, designing and constructing numerous important buildings and providing invaluable economic and legal support on all projects.

The history of ENERGOPROJEKT is a wealth of various experience transferred from generation to generation since its beginnings binding every new generation to work together on retaining and improving the ENERGOPROJEKT Group's leading position in all fields of business. Numerous successfully completed projects such as hydro and thermal power plants, dams, irrigation systems, hundreds of kilometers of roads and railways, many tunnels of different purpose, thousands of kilometers of transmission lines and electric energy distribution networks, large number of public and commercial infrastructural facilities – hotels, congress centers, hospitals, schools, business and representative facilities, stadiums and sports halls, hundreds of thousands square meters of apartment buildings, and various industrial and other projects at home and abroad permanently bind all employees to continue the good work and further contribution to ENERGOPROJEKT success in implementing cutting-edge technologies as well as constantly improving knowledge and business competence.

In the first four decades, ENERGOPROJEKT was increasingly and continuously developed from a small-size design bureau to an internationally reputable company. However, the growth of the company was decelerated in the 1990s due to the disintegration of the country, international economic sanctions and seriously jeopardized by the consequences of the 1999 bombing of Serbia. Whilst concurrently with handling problems at home, it was necessary to overcome huge changes in the international markets caused by the disintegration of the Soviet Union, wars in Kuwait and Iraq, recession in many countries of Africa, South America and Asia. Also, whilst simultaneously maintaining business activities on a high level throughout the crisis the process of ownership transformation from social to private took place. In this process, ENERGOPROJEKT was a leader in implementing every privatization law. Only with employees' commitment and the successful completion of projects abroad, was it possible to keep ENERGOPROJEKT afloat.
