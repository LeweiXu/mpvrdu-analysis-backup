<div style="text-align: center;">Figure 2.1. Molecular cloning scheme of psc_hSyn1_ArcLight plasmid</div>


Psc_hSyn_ArcLight plasmid was produced in a way that psc_hSyn plasmid was opened with SacI/EcoRI restriction enzymes. From the ArcLight-A242 plasmid, the ArcLight coding sequence was first multiplied by PCR with designed primers that introduced new restriction sites: SacI and EcoRI. Afterwards, this sequence, cut by SacI/EcoRI was ligated into open psc_hSyn plasmid.
