## Record screen

Knock twice on the screen with two knuckles to start or end a screen recording.

## Access Home screen editing mode

Pinch two fingers together on the home screen.

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_188_806_353_997.jpg" alt="Image" width="9%" /></div>


## Display the shortcut panel on the lock screen

Turn on the screen and swipe up from the bottom of the lock screen.

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_187_1077_352_1268.jpg" alt="Image" width="9%" /></div>


## Display the search bar

Swipe down from the middle of the home screen.

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_186_1340_330_1542.jpg" alt="Image" width="8%" /></div>


## Display the notification panel

Swipe down from the upper left edge of the screen.

## Turn on a shortcut switch

Swipe down from the upper right edge of the screen to display Control

Panel and touch ___ to expand the shortcut switches panel (depending on your device model).
