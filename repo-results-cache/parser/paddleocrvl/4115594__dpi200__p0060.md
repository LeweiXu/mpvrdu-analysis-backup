As a result of questionnaires we addressed to migrants and tutors,

Having culinary career is being considered by migrants as transformative experience to take their first steps on integration to Austria, beyond involvement in the labor, but because lack of information on cultural issues, they are not to give correct answers to the questions or don’t have confident enough to keep holding career in this sector. So, they need to receive knowledge on cultural issues, that will enable them to feel more integrated in the kitchen.

• Information on eating habits of Austrian people

• cultural issues in Tyrol region

• having German language skills at least basic level

- theoretical information into practice and information on sanitization is considered by them as important issue (that became more critical because of the pandemic)

Tutors find these topics very remarkable for supporting integration of migrants;

• being aware of cultural values of migrants in the local area

- diversity on learning/teaching- innovative methods that are based on different learning styles of trainees
