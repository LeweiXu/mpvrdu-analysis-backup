### a. The Fewer The Ways Of Expressing An Idea, The Closer The Similarity Must Be To Be Deemed Substantial

There are times where there are only a limited number of ways of expressing an idea. An example may be a short microinstruction routine subject to significant hardware constraints. In such case, the idea and expression have not merged since the idea (function) of the routine can be expressed in a number of different ways (different microroutines).
