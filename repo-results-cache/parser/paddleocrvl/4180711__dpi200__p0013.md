## CHAMPAGNE

We love our Champagne at Core. Quite simply, because Champagne continues to be the envy of the world's sparkling wine producers. Ok, so Champagne sales have taken a hit from Prosecco recently, while English sparkling wine producers are rightly shouting about their usefully high acidity levels as the Champagne region warms up. But Champagne still rules, especially when it comes to ageing. That said, the big Champagne houses are being challenged by a new wave of producers who prefer to wear a pair of jeans than a sharp suit; who might farm organically, and focus on single vineyard and single grape variety wines. Cue the Champagne grower. The rise of the grower-producers has revolutionised this generation in the region. Top Champagne is no longer the exclusive realm of the big players. While single vineyard is the new vanguard, with deliciously complex cuvées from trailblazing terroiriste growers taking the wine world by storm.
