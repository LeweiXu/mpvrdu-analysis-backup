## Marlene Ågerstrand

PhD-student, Royal Institute of Technology (KTH)

maa2@kth.se

Marenes work concerns regulatory aspects of ecotoxicology, with focus on risk assessment and risk management. Currently she is conducting an evaluation of the Swedish environmental classification system for pharmaceuticals. The evaluation concerns driving forces behind the implementation of the system, its scientific basis, actual workings, and efficiency towards reducing environmental risks.
