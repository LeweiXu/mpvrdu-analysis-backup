## BUSINESS ENVIRONMENT

The coronavirus pandemic, when arriving in our region early last year, did create a lot of uncertainty. The immediate impact on Nordic Fibreboard was the loss of customers from regions such as Asia, Middle East and southern Europe when the economies in these regions ground to standstill for a while, which led us to close down our Püssi fibreboard factory. As the year progressed, it became clear that manufacturing sectors, such as ours, were not substantially impacted by the pandemic. Due to travel restrictions, and part closure of the hospitality industry, consumers had more monies available to spend on their home environments, which for us meant increased demand for our products in segments such as construction/renovation and door cores. This trend has continued into the new year.
