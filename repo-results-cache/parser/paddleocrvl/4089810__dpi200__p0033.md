Scores for compassion satisfaction (CS), burnout (BO), and secondary traumatic stress (STS) were calculated for each survey response. BO and STS served as subcategories for compassion fatigue (CF). In the ProQOL-5 survey, CS scores below 23 showed negative satisfaction for one's job. While BO and STS scores above 41 show positive compassion fatigue. The average score for compassion satisfaction was 40.23, burnout was 23.40, and secondary traumatic stress was 22.86. Out of the 90 responses, 30 resulted in moderate to high compassion satisfaction, 30 resulted in low to moderate burnout, and 30 resulted in low to moderate secondary traumatic stress.
