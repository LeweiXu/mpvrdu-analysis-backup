# BLESSED ARE THE PEACEMAKERS

Christ’s Teachings About Love, Compassion and Forgiveness

COMPILED WITH AN INTRODUCTION BY WENDELL BERRY

Bible Studies • Trade Paperback • 9781593761004 $9.95 • 80 pages • 4" x 6.8"

Unfortunately, the teachings of Christ have often been either ignored or distorted by the very people calling themselves Christian. Today, we see a vigorous movement in America fueled by a politicized and engaged portion of the electorate involved in just such ignorance and distortion. Whether directed towards social intolerance or attitudes of warlike aggression, these right-wing citizens have claimed a power of influence that far exceeds their numbers.

Blessed Are the Peacemakers collects the sayings of Jesus, as selected by Wendell Berry, who contributes an essay of introduction. This is a book of inspiration and prayerful compassion, and, we may hope, a ringing call to action at a time when our country and the world it once led stand at a dangerous crossroads.

# THE BOOK OF BOOKS The Radical Impact of the King James Bible 1611–2011

MELVYN BRAGG

History • Trade Paperback • 9781619020108 $17.95 • 368 pages • 6" x 9" • Ebook available

The King James Bible has often been called “The Book of Books,” both in itself and in what it stands for. Since its publication in 1611, it has been the bestselling book in the world, and, many believe, it has had the greatest impact of anything ever published.

The King James Bible has spread Protestantism and been one of the greatest influences on English language and literature. Its influence is near immeasurable, and it has defined modern attitudes towards science, education, and sex. As Melvyn Bragg explored the history of our language in The Adventure of English, so The Book of Books reveals the extraordinary and still-felt impact of a work created four hundred years ago.
