## ROMI (Return On Marketing Investment)

1. ROMI (Return on Marketing Investment) should be a company-wide project to ensure that you maximize your commercial marketing investment.

2. The project requires leadership commitment & guidance to drive sustainable process, infrastructure, organizational, and cultural growth. Company-wide means across Business Groups, Segments, Sales, Finance, HR and Marketing in both CHQ & the Field.

3. ROMI is the vehicle that brings together the executive team, project managers, subject matter experts (SMEs), communications, and change management required to transform innovative, bold ideas into real solutions that are broadly adopted.

4. ROMI is the fusion of art and science in Marketing.
