In the first decade, Energoprojekt designed and put into operation first thermal-power plants - Veliki Kostolac (1952); Kolubara (1956); Morava, Kosovo I i II (1961). Simultaneously, along with the first domestic thermal-power plants, Energoprojekt experts designed new industrial-power plants "Servo Mihalj" in Zrenjanin (1953), "Viskoza" in Loznica (1958) and many more.

The country was in a process of fast recovery with an increasing number of factories, apartments, towns and consumers that needed electric power, i.e. it was necessary to construct sub-stations and transmission systems. Energoprojekt was also entrusted with a task to design and put into operation many distribution systems and fifteen transmission stations in major cities in Serbia.

In order to complete its business activities in the field of energetics, upon the request of the Federal Commission for Nuclear Energy, Energoprojekt started putting together teams for studies, design, and integration into the electrical system as well as siting of nuclear-power plants.

Design of energy facilities did not remain the only activity of the company. Since Energoprojekt, besides having top-notch experts also had visionary directors, it focused on drafting studies and designs for large water management systems for land irrigation, water supply to industries, cities and entire regions.

Experience gained on projects in the country encouraged the management of Energoprojekt to make first business steps out of the borders of Yugoslavia in the mid-1950s. First projects were done in Argentina on construction of hydro technical facilities and melioration systems (regulation of the river San Juan in 1952); in Turkey (the river Akshay together with the Kemer dam in 1953); in Greece (regulation of the river Arna in 1955); in Burma (hydro plant Falam in 1956, HPP Mindat in 1957, irrigation of Promo and Deyndabo areas in 1959); in Syria (regulation of the Oronte river basin in 1954, use of the Euphrates' water in 1957, HPP Blauca in 1959, HPP Yarmouk in 1960); in Pakistan (regulation of the river Kunhar in 1958, HPP Gujranwal in 1959, reconstruction of the Bund Murad Khan dam in 1959, as well as regulation of the Gomal River basin in 1960). These were the first among close to 80 countries where Energoprojekt has operated over the last 65 years.
