# A SENSE OF THE WHOLE

Reading Gary Snyder's Mountains and Rivers Without End

EDITED BY MARK GONNERMAN

Poetry / Environmentalism • Hardcover • 9781619024564 $28.00 • 352 pages • 6" x 9" • Ebook available

In 1997, Mark Gonnerman organized a yearlong research workshop on Gary Snyder's Mountains and Rivers Without End at the Stanford Humanities Center. A Sense of the Whole captures the excitement of these gatherings and invites readers to enter the poem through essays and talks by David Abram, Wendell Berry, Carl Bielefeldt, Tim Dean, Jim Dodge, Robert Hass, Stephanie Kaza, Julia Martin, Michael McClure, Nanao Sakaki, and Katsunori Yamazato. It includes an interview with Gary Snyder, appendices, and other resources for further study.

Snyder once introduced a reading saying most of his work is like a Class III whitewater rapids run where you will do just fine on your own, but Mountains and Rivers is more like a Class V: if you're going to make it, you'll need a guide. As such, A Sense of the Whole is your most expert guide for Snyder's masterpiece.
