## Outbound Shipments:

All shipments going out of Rajasthan must carry Form 49.

The Form can be generated online and it will be named as FORM 49A. Validity

## Period

Both Incoming & Outgoing Form have a validity of 2 Years from the date of issuance.

## E-Tail Shipment Movement:

As of now there is no clear guideline from sales tax department on E-tail shipments hence it is presumed that shipments to Individuals (B2C) in this category will attract the same paper work requirement as mentioned for Individuals.
