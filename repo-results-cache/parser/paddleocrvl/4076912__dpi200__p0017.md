<div style="text-align: center;">Figure 6: Evolution of total numbers of animals used for the first time for research, testing, routine production and education purposes between 2015 and 2018 within EU-28 and Norway</div>


In 2018, the main species used for the first time for research, testing, routine production and educational purposes were mice, fish, rats and birds that together represented 93% of the total number of animals. Species of particular public concern (dogs, cats and non-human primates (NHP)) represented less than 0.3% of the total number of animals. No great apes are used for scientific purposes in the European Union (Figure 7).

With the data from Norway, the repartition of species used for the first time is slightly different compared to 2017, with an increase of fish uses (+13%) and a decrease of mice (-9%), rats (-2%), “Other mammals” (-1%) and birds (-1%) uses.
