## FBQS (Intact and befouled servers)

 $$ \mathbb{V}=\{\mathtt{1},\mathtt{2},\mathtt{3},\mathtt{4}\} $$ 

 $$ \mathbb{S}:\mathbb{V}\to2^{2^{\mathbb{V}}} $$ 

 $$ \mathbb{S}(\mathbf{1})=\{\{\mathbf{1},\mathbf{2}\},\mathbf{1},\mathbf{4}\}\} $$ 

 $$ \mathbb{S}(2)=\{\{1,2\}\} $$ 

 $$ \mathbb{S}(3)=\{\{\mathtt{1},\mathtt{3}\}\} $$ 

 $$ \mathbb{S}(4)=\{\{3,4\}\} $$

Given a set of faulty servers, Vint is the biggest quorum Vint ∈ Q such that:

• ∀v ∈ Ⅳint, v is correct,

• Q|Vint has quorum intersection.
