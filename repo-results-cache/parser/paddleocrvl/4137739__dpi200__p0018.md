### 1.10 Coin Mechanism

This consists in an Azkoyen coin mechanism of either EJECUTIVO or MDB/ICP types or one of another manufacturer as long as it conforms with European Directive EMC 89/336/EEC and its modifications.

### 1.11 Sales Prices

A different price can be programmed for each product selection.

The maximum programmable sales price depends upon the coin mechanism used. With the EJECUTIVO the maximum sales price is 250 times the base coin, e.g., if the base coin used is that of Pts.5 the maximum sales price is  $ 250 \times 5 = Pts.1.250 $, although by working with prices in the coin mechanism in the “Price Holding” mode, 30 different prices can be programmed up to a maximum of Pts.9.995 each.



When a MDB/ICP coin mechanism is used, the maximum sales price allowed by the machine is Pts 9.995.

### 1.12 Coin Acceptance

Coins are accepted by a selector incorporated into the coin mechanism that admits coin of current legal tender.

The maximum amount programmable in a machine with either MDB/ICP or EJECUTIVO type coin mechanisms is Pts. 9.995.
