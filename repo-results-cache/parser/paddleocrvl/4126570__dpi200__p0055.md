## Procedure:

▶ Chop onions

Preheat at  $ 80^{\circ} $C/ $ 175^{\circ} $F in braising mode

▶ Pour oil, add chopped onions and caramelize for 15 minutes

Add beans, garlic, pepper, water and cook for 1 hour. Add more water if needed

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_655_1129_696_1175.jpg" alt="Image" width="2%" /></div>


TIP: soak bean in water overnight to speed up the preparation

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_655_1210_696_1255.jpg" alt="Image" width="2%" /></div>


TIP: using pressure cycle will reduce necessary cooking time to 45 minutes

When beans are tender, add salt, pepper and cumin



▶ Mash beans with an immersion blender Bermixer Pro

## Portion and serve
