## SIGNIFICANT EVENTS AFTER BALANCE DATE

The impact of the coronavirus pandemic is ongoing. With the slowdown in the travel industry, the Company has implemented various cost-saving measures and has maintained a lean core team. This is to service our existing partners and subscribers, while ensuring we are ready to catch the first wave of travellers when the pandemic is under control.

The Company has engaged solicitor to facilitate 1:2 Non-Renounceable Right Issue (NRRI) at an issue price of $0.01 per share to raise approximately $1,017,348. The NRRI will be finalised by the end of August 2020.

Apart from the events disclosed above, no other matter or circumstance has arisen since 31 March 2020 that has significantly affected, or may significantly affect the consolidated entity's operations, the results of those operations, or the consolidated entity's state of affairs in future financial years.
