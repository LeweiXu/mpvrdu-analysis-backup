Book:

The Very Hungry Caterpillar by Eric Carle

Domain:

Prevocational: color identification, communication,

prereading

Objective:

To explore how literacy can enhance and teach

prevocational skills

## Grades Recommended:

Kindergarten through second

Preparation:

Materials: Pictures apples, pears, plums, strawberries, oranges, cake, ice cream, etc. from the book (if possible get actual foods).

## Procedure:

1. Read the story.

2. Identify the foods in the story.

3. Label the colors.

4. Read the color words.

5. Match the color words with the pictures or

actual foods.
