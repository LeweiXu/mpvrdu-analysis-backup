### 8.7 

## BANKS WITH A MICROFINANCE WINDOW

Two commercial banks, namely Opportunity International Bank of Malawi (OIBM) and Malawi Savings Bank (MSB) continued to establish their grip in microfinance. OIBM's microfinance portfolio accounted for 40.0 percent of its total loans of MK4,029.1 million in 2009. MSB held about MK126.6 million, representing 1 percent of its portfolio, in microcredit. NBS, on the other hand, continued to offer loans to small and medium enterprises using the credit line from the International Finance Corporation, an investment arm of the World Bank. The rest of the commercial banks also collected undisclosed amounts of collateral savings from either individual or group borrowers facilitated by MSPs that were not allowed by law to mobilise deposits.
