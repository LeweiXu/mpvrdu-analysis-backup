## STEPS TOWARD GROUP THERAPY

1949 – 1973

Activities in building up the NPAP, teaching member and active in training programs.

1948 – 1951

Training in group therapy with pioneers, such as Asya Kadis, Sandy Flowerman, Alexander Wolf, etc.

1957 – 1973

Instructor at the Centre for Psychotherapy (later Centre for Mental Health) in the Department for Group Therapy.

After 1962



Ruth is active member of the American Academy of Psychotherapy, a meeting place for representatives of newer and classic psychotherapeutic methods, i.e., George Bach, Henry and Vivian Guze, Sidney Jourard, Fritz Perls, Ervin Polster, Carl Rogers, Virginia Satir, John Warkentin, Carl Whitaker.
