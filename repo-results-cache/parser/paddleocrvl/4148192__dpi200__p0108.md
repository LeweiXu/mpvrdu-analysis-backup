Figure 6.4 shows the range gained by using an optimum ground effect profile at various level deceleration altitudes AGL. The wind curves in Figure 6.4 are not a function of height to wingspan ratio since the wind reduction due to ground effect is a function of absolute altitude AGL only. Wind reduction due to ground effect can cause range gains at much higher altitudes than under no wind conditions.
