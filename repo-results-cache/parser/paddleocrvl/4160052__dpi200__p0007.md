## #ProudToBePurina

Throughout this extraordinary time when COVID-19 has impacted all aspects of our lives, our colleagues have gone to extraordinary lengths to care for each other, for pets and the people who love them. To celebrate their achievements, we ran a cover star competition for this report. We asked our EMENA employees to share with us why they were #ProudToBePurina. Here are some of our favourite entries.
