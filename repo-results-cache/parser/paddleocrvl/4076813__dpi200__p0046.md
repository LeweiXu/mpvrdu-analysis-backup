## PLAT VACATION CHECKLIST

## GENERAL INFORMATION

1) The purpose of a Plat Vacation is to nullify a plat in its entirety consistent within the provisions of State Law.

2) See Section 107.04.09 Plat Vacation of the Subdivision Ordinance for more information on the vacation process and the reasons for using this device.

3) The Town of Little Elm is completely paperless in terms of submittals and resubmittals. All required materials shall be submitted via MyGov or in electronic formats (Adobe PDF via email). The Online Submittal Guide can help you through this process.

4) Minor Plats will not be filed with Denton County until all required public improvements have been installed and accepted by the Town of Little Elm, unless specifically allowed in a Development Agreement.

5) Plat Vacations must be heard before the Planning and Zoning Commission (see Section 107.04.09(e) for more information regarding approvals.

6) Plat Vacations must meet all the requirements of a Final Plat and must be noticed in the newspaper of record, as described in Section 107.04.09(c).
