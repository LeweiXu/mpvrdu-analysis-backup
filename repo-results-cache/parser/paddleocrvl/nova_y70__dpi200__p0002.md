## Basic Gestures and Shortcuts

## System Navigation Gestures

Go to Settings > System & updates > System navigation and make sure that Gestures is selected.

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_189_663_282_876.jpg" alt="Image" width="5%" /></div>


## Return to the previous screen

Swipe in from the left or right edges to return to the previous screen.

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_190_933_285_1122.jpg" alt="Image" width="5%" /></div>


## Back to home screen

Swipe up from the bottom to go to the home screen.

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_190_1206_285_1393.jpg" alt="Image" width="5%" /></div>


## Recent tasks

Swipe up from the bottom of the screen and hold to view recent tasks.

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_191_1478_286_1665.jpg" alt="Image" width="5%" /></div>


## Close an app

When viewing recent tasks, swipe up on an app preview to close the app.
