##### 5.2.1. SoaML – Service-oriented architecture Modeling Language

The SoaML (Service-oriented architecture Modeling Language) is a new modelling standard that is under development in OMG (Object Management Group). SoaML aims to add SOA concepts on top of UML (Unified Modeling Language), which is considered as de facto standard in software modelling.
