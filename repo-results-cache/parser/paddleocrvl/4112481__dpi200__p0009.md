Re: Brainerd HRA Update

## General Agency Update

We continue to work with our legal counsel to help acquire a tax forfeited lot in Serene Pines and have that lot added into the Master Purchase and Redevelopment Agreement that we have with Level Contracting. We have a tentative schedule that will guide us through the process and are reviewing the forfeiture proceedings of Lot 5, Block 3 prior to approaching the County to pursue the conveyance of this lot to the County HRA.

The HRA will have 2 job openings that we will be looking to fill in the coming weeks. We have an opening for accounting specialist, and the other opening will be to fill an administrative role within our organization. Our new Maintenance Supervisor, Michael Foote started on June 1 $ ^{st} $ and we are excited to have him on board.

## Rehab

The NE Brainerd SCDP grant has rehab completed on one commercial unit, nine mixed use units, one single family rental rehab and three owner occupied units. We have two owner occupied units and one duplex unit in construction. There are two multi-family and five single family rental rehab applications in the verification process. We continue to seek five more single-family owner-occupied rehabs.
