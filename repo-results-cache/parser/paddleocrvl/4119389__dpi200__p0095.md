## Contraception

Although your fertility may be affected, it is still important to use effective contraception. This is because you may still be producing sperm for some time after treatment.

Your doctor may recommend you use effective contraception for 6 to 24 months after treatment. This is because sperm produced after treatment may still be fertile but could be damaged. This means that a child conceived soon after radiotherapy could have abnormalities. But at the moment, there isn’t any evidence that children born to fathers who have had radiotherapy have an increased risk of abnormalities.
