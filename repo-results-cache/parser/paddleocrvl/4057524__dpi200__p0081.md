## ABOUND

## PROJECT IDEAS

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_96_425_408_660.jpg" alt="Image" width="18%" /></div>


Easily switch out interchangeable panel tiles to achieve different aesthetic or functional goals.

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_444_427_752_658.jpg" alt="Image" width="18%" /></div>


With a minimal kit of parts and intuitive universal connectors, Abound can be specified, ordered, installed and reconfigured quickly.

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_793_426_1101_657.jpg" alt="Image" width="18%" /></div>


Keep everything neatly tucked away with a variety of organizational tools, including paper management solutions, folder bins and more.

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_1140_427_1449_657.jpg" alt="Image" width="18%" /></div>


Position power and data outlets at the desktop, panel base or both. Lay-in capabilities in the top of the frame expand cabling capacity and make reconfigurations easy.
