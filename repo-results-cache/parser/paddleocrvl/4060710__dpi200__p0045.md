## Analysis of the data

After postprocessing and analysing the data, we scatter in Figure 28 the information with a filter in +25 minutes of delay and an aggregated exposure to convection of 130 (measured as the sum of probabilities along the route). Looking at Figure 29, one can see that the delays follow a quasi-normal distribution.

We fit a normal distribution with average  $ \mu=0 $ and  $ \sigma=6.7 $ min. These are the values we use in the Delay Event Model described in Section 4.2.4.
