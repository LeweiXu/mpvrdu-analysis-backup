## Net Migration

■ Government expenditures are expected to increase by 5 bn. in 2015 and by 4 bn. in 2016 due to increased number of asylum seekers

- Rough estimates may indicate visible increases in private consumption and construction

However, net effects are difficult to estimate (e.g., due to potential crowding out of alternative investment projects at the local level)

■ Currently, due to net migration the labor force is increasing instead of decreasing

■ From about 500,000 asylum seekers in the current year, by far less than 100,000 enter the labor force

■ Additional asylum seekers may have visible (but small) effects on potential output
