### II. THE PHENOMENON OF CYBERMOBBING

Online shaming is essential for normative role enforcement. $ ^{20} $ But oftentimes, such shaming devolves into cybermobbing, a practice which inflicts irreparable harm unrelated to a violated norm. This section discusses case studies of cybermobbing and evaluates its social utility. On balance, it concludes that cybermobbing has a net negative effect on society and requires a statutory solution.
