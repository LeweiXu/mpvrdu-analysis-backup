## WHEN IS A WRITTEN RESPONSE TO THE COURT PAPERS DUE?

- LOOK AT THE TIMETABLE BELOW. If the last day for the other party to respond falls on a Saturday, Sunday, or legal holiday, you do not count that day. The last day you count to determine if you can file the default papers, must be a day when this court is open for business.

- INCLUDE WEEKENDS AND HOLIDAYS. In counting the days, include weekends and holidays until you reach the number of days in the Timetable below. If the other party files a written Response or Answer with the court, you CANNOT FILE BY DEFAULT.
