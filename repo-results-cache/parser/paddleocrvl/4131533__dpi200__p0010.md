### 3.3 Assembly

1. Remove all the ODiSI 6 components from the packaging and verify that the components are undamaged.

2. Place the unit on a stable surface capable of supporting the entire weight of the unit.

3. Confirm that airflow into and out of the vents at the side and rear of the unit is not obstructed.
