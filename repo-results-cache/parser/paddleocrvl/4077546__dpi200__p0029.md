## Outfall 002A

## Compressible Media Filtration

## • WWETCO Filters

– Durable synthetic balls which are compressed to create a porosity gradient resulting in the removal of large and small particles throughout the media bed

– Benefits to this type of implementation are the small footprint and relatively simple operation. While this technology is ideal for solids removal, any application for this analysis will require disinfection to meet permit limits.
