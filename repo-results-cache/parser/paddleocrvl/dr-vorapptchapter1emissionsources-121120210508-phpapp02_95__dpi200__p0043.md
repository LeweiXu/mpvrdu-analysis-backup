## Diagnostic Metrics - Example

<div style="text-align: center;">Lead Funnel Progression</div>


Displays how many of your Leads are converting into Marketing-Qualified Leads (MQLs), how many of your MQLs are converting into Sales-Accepted Leads (SALs), how many of your SALs are converting into Sales-Qualified Leads (SQLs), and how many of your SQLs are becoming actual Sales Won Opportunities (SWOs).
