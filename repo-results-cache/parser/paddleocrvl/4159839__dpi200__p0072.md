## Book:

The Three Billy Goats Gruff by Paul Galdone

Domain:

Recreation-Leisure: prereading, auditory discrimination, communication

Objective:

To explore how literacy can enhance and teach recreation-leisure skills

## Grades Recommended:

Kindergarten though second

Preparation:

Materials: Buckets or empty oatmeal boxes, tape player and tape.

Procedure:

1. Read the story.

2. Discuss the predictable pattern of, "Trip, trap, trip trap".

3. Model that every time the goats go on the bridge and the students hear those sounds that they use the buckets or boxes to make the sounds of the goats crossing the bridge.

4. Practice the difference in sound that each goat made due to their size. Use a tape player to record soft, medium and loud "trip trap" sounds. Instruct and model to the students that they also read those words as they use their instrument.
