19. Expose and celebrate the natural and cultural features within Chapel Hill.

20. Improve the sight lines at the Chapel Road/ Shore Road intersection by removing ledge and vegetation along Shore Road.

## Goddard Mansion

21. Determine the appropriate level of preservation and access to Goddard Mansion.

Option A (Secure it) - Replace the existing fence with more permanent high quality fencing in the building's apertures. Consult a Structural Engineer to evaluate stabilization needs for the immediate entry vestibule and steps, and adjacent overhead structure. Make minimal structural improvements to allow for superficial access to the stoop.

Option B (Access to the a Portion of the Interior) - Consult a Structural Engineer to evaluate the stabilization and structural needs to stabilize the central portion of the exterior and interior of the building to allow for limited and controlled access only into the main section of the structure. No access is granted to the barn portion of the structure.

Option C (Historic Relic- Front Wall) - Consult a Structural Engineer to evaluate stabilization and structural needs to remove all but the front wall of the Mansion to establish an ruin artifact of the past. Establish an outdoor space as part of the wall. Add Interpretive panels.

22. Develop a small exterior overlook on the east side of the mansion to provide a safe and secure place with filtered views of the water.

23. Eliminate excessive asphalt paving at the Mansion and replace with native vegetation and lawn area to provide additional event space and / or overlook.

24. Add native plant material along the property line to provide a visual, vegetated buffer.

25. Plant the slopes on the east side of the mansion to provide safe, secure, and designated access and to reduce the erosion.

## Battery Keyes

26. Determine the appropriate level of preservation and access to Battery Keyes.

Option A (Secure It) - Add a fence in the apertures and door ways to restrict access. Consult a Structural Engineer to determine how to repair the steps and handrails to provide safe access to top of the Battery.

Option B (Removal and Add Overlook) - Remove all or a portion of the battery and replace with a new overlook that reinterprets the battery and creates a safe viewing platform.

27. Reduce the width of the road leading to the battery to an 8' wide walking path. Add seating opportunities along this approach to the battery, and remove excessive pavement in front of the battery to establish an open space.

28. Remove any invasive plant material and add native plant material along the shore's edge to reduce erosion and stabilize the shore's edge.

29. Provide safe, controlled access points to the rocky shore - See General Recommendations.
