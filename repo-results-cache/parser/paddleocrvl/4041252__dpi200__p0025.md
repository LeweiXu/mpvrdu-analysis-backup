## Build Your Own Telescope

Short of ideas to keep the kids busy during summer? The Build Your Own Cardboard Telescope kit is a great way to get your young ones started at stargazing on a budget. Except for the lenses and mirror, the kit is made entirely out of sustainable cardboard and is designed to slot together with no glue. The end result is a 16x magnification 72cm telescope with a sliding tube to focus, a viewfinder and an angle finder.
