## Evaluation of botanicals on different stages of YSB

Among the botanicals evaluated, Acorus rhizome extract exerted maximum ovicidal action which was evident by significantly low percentage of larval hatching of YSB (5.58 %), followed by NSKE (5 %) showing 21.35 per cent hatching and Milletia leaf extract (5 %) with 34.08 per cent hatching of YSB eggs (Table 3 & Figure 1).
