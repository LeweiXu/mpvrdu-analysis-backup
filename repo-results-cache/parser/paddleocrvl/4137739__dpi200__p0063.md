#### 3.4.9 Motor Presence Detection

Each time service is requested from the machine, the Control Card confirms the existence of the motor for the selection requested via the I/O Card.

## Operation:

+12 v are applied to the motor which is in series with a voltage divider composed of two resistors and the existing voltage between the common point an the negative is taken. If the measured voltage is 0v no motor exists and if the voltage is 5v, one does. This information is sent to the U11 and then to the Control Card. As can be seen, activation is not necessary to confirm its existence.
