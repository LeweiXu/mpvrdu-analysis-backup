# ANNEXE VIII: QUADRIGA INVESTORS – STABLE RETURN

# Characteristics of the Sub-Fund

## Investment Objective and Policy

The objective of Quadriga Investors – Stable Return (the “Sub-Fund” in the present section) is to provide investors with absolute returns through investment in UCITS or open-ended UCIs investing, through different types of strategies such as tactical asset allocation strategy, long/short strategy, FX strategy, global macro strategy as well as market neutral strategy, and in all type of UCITS eligible asset classes (the “Target Funds”).

The Target Funds will be selected by the Management Company on a discretionary basis, and the Management Company will not be constrained by any geographical, sectorial or currency limitations. The Sub-Fund may invest in Target Funds managed by the Management Company and in other sub-funds of the Company, in which case no issuing or redemption charge will be charged on subscription to, or redemption, of units/shares in these Target Funds. Through its investment in investment funds, the Sub-Fund may obtain exposure to derivatives as well as to non-traditional asset classes such as commodities and, to a limited extent, to asset-backed securities, or contingent convertibles.

In addition, on an ancillary basis, the Sub-Fund may implement directional long or short strategies on listed equity indices and bond futures by entering directly into derivative instruments. The Sub-Fund's long positions will be sufficiently liquid to cover the Sub-Fund's obligations.

Subject to the applicable conditions and limits set out in section “Investment restrictions” the Sub-Fund may invest on an ancillary basis in cash, cash equivalent and/or deposits. Under exception al circumstances, the Sub-Fund may be invested up to 100% in Money Market Instruments, cash or cash equivalent.

The Sub-Fund may hold both non-EUR denominated securities and non-EUR denominated currency positions. Therefore, investors should bear in mind that the change in exchange rate may impact the Sub-Fund’s return.

The Sub-Fund will be actively managed.

## Use of derivatives or other investment techniques and instruments

The Sub-Fund will enter in financial derivatives instruments for both investment and/or hedging purposes. In particular, financial derivatives instruments used for the purpose of hedging and investment may include listed futures and equity index futures.
