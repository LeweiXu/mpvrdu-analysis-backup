Salts, mainly NaCl,  $ MgCl_{2} $ and  $ CaCl_{2} $, in crude oil can cause severe problems in refinery processes. These problems include:

☑ Corrosion induced by the acids formed by chloride salt decomposition.

✿ Fouling of heat exchangers and other units by salt deposition.

The poisoning of catalysts in downstream processing units.

Therefore it is important that the Crude is desalted before being charged to the distillation unit.

The salt in the crude oil is picked up from the reservoirs or during transportation. The crude may also contain other suspended solids such as sand, clay, iron oxide particles and trace elements. (In Brent crude these trace metallic elements are Vanadium and Nickel.)
