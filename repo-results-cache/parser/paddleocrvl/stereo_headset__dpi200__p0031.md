## Connecting to a paired Android smartphone

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_101_404_148_551.jpg" alt="Image" width="2%" /></div>


Unlock the screen of the Android smartphone if it is locked.

Turn on the headset.

Press and hold the ☐ (power) button for about 2 seconds.

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_170_655_426_834.jpg" alt="Image" width="15%" /></div>


You will hear the voice guidance say, “Power on”. Check that the indicator (blue) continues to flash after you release your finger from the button.

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_170_999_545_1179.jpg" alt="Image" width="22%" /></div>


If the headset has automatically connected to the last connected device, you will hear the voice guidance say, "Bluetooth connected".

Check the connection status on the Android smartphone. If it is not connected, proceed to step 3.

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_102_1392_146_1437.jpg" alt="Image" width="2%" /></div>


Display the devices paired with the Android smartphone.

1. Select [Settings] - [Device connection] - [Bluetooth].

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_216_1524_495_1746.jpg" alt="Image" width="16%" /></div>


2. Touch the switch to turn on the Bluetooth function.

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_220_1846_493_2111.jpg" alt="Image" width="16%" /></div>


When Bluetooth is turned on, your device can communicate with other nearby Bluetooth devices.
