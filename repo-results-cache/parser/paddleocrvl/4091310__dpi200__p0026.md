## What we offer

We offer a network for committed people who want to get involved in society – beyond party, religion and other borders.

We are convinced that we can only solve conflicts by keeping an eye on the systemic issues. This applies to internal-family situations as well as problems in larger areas.

We start where we can achieve change through our actions. We choose not to wait for others to solve the problems that we can solve ourselves.

"Bridges for Hope and Peace" brings people together who want to be active. Together, we can plan strategies for the support of peace-making processes – and implement them.

We give a voice to people who are unable to do this for themselves. We achieve this through public relations work and lectures.

Our projects can be funded in many ways (ideally, financially and personally)
