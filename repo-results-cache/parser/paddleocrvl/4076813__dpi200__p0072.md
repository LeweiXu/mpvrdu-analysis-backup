## STORMWATER POLLUTION PREVENTION (SWP3) CHECKLIST

## GENERAL INFORMATION

(1) Prior to any earth disturbing activities, the following conditions need to be addressed and accepted through the Town of Little Elm’s Engineering Department

(2) Information on construction policies can be found in Article 107.05, Construction Plans & Procedures, of the Subdivision Ordinance.

(3) Required plan submittals must be in compliance with the Engineering Design Manual and the Subdivision Ordinance, Section 107.08.02, Water, Wastewater & Drainage Requirements and Environmental Standards, subsection (f), Stormwater.

(4) The Town of Little Elm is completely paperless in terms of submittals and resubmittals. All required materials shall be submitted via MyGov or in electronic formats (Adobe PDF or Dropbox link via email). The Online Submittal Guide can help you through this process.
