### 2.5 Cetacean strandings

Four hundred and thirty-five cetaceans, comprising of 15 different species were reported to SMASS in 2018. The most frequently reported species was the harbour porpoise ( $ Phocoena\ phocoena $) (n=194, 44.5%) with an increase on the number reported last year. There was no particular geographical bias of these cases. Due to the beaked whale UME event, Cuvier's beaked whales (n=57, 31.1%) were the second most commonly reported species.
