# 1961-1971

In its second decade, Energoprojekt continued with the rapid development by expanding the business to construction and entering new markets abroad – signing design contracts as well as constructing and putting into operation energy, water and industrial plants at home and abroad. Energoprojekt focused more and more on providing complete engineering for complex systems.

Expanding the scope and business activities, Energoprojekt kept improving the organizational structure in order to run the business in the market in the best possible way while adjusting the organization to the legal regulations in the country. The number of departments kept increasing as well as the number of bureaus per department so that certain business activities could be directed and developed more efficiently. Consequently, Energoprojekt entered its second decade with seven departments which would grow into independent directorates in the following decade.

Increasingly facing the world market (the orientation that would be proved right in the difficult years that followed), Energoprojekt started opening representative offices in many countries – both private and joint venture companies. In Zambia, Energoprojekt established a joint venture company ZECCO in 1965 that would realize a number of significant projects in the following years as well as UNICO – a company for urban planning and architecture in 1966. These joint venture companies have constructed numerous projects in Zambia – in Lusaka, Kafue, Ndola and Kitwe.
