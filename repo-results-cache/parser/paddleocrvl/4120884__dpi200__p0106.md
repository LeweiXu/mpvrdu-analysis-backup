The residual stress profiles for the hoop, radial and axial components were tensile along the aluminum interbore. Similar to the residual strain profiles in aluminum, the hoop stress had the highest magnitude which increased from approximately 40 MPa at the top of the cylinder to approximately 170 MPa at the bottom. The axial component had a similar stress profile to the hoop component with the stress increasing from approximately 5 MPa at the top of the cylinder to 130 MPa at the bottom. The radial stress component had a stress magnitude increasing from approximately 40 MPa at the top of the cylinder to 50 MPa at the bottom. By comparing the stress and strain profiles, it can be stated that stress profiles for the radial and axial stress components were largely influenced by the hoop component since the tensile strain was significantly larger in the hoop direction and the calculation of stress with Hooke's Law involves the use of all measured strain components.
