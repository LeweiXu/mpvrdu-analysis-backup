## COLUMN SYSTEM FORMWORK

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_76_208_1255_982.jpg" alt="Image" width="55%" /></div>


The column formwork systems now available are normally modular in nature and allow quick assembly and erection on-site while minimizing labor and crane time. They are available in steel, aluminium and even cardboard (not reusable but recycled) and have a variety of internal face surfaces depending on the concrete finish required. Innovations have led to adjustable, reusable column forms which can be clamped on-site to give different column sizes.
