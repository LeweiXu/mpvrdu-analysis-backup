Definition 1.21. LIMIT OF A SEQUENCE OF SPECIES OF STRUCTURES. A sequence  $ (F_{n})_{n\geq0} $ of species of structures is said to converge to a species F, written as  $ \lim_{n\to\infty}F_{n}=F $, if for any integer  $ N\geq0 $, there exists  $ K\geq0 $ such that for all  $ n\geq K $,  $ F_{n}=_{N}F $. For any species F, it is clear that

 $$ \lim_{n\to\infty}F_{\leq n}=F. $$ 

This concept of limit is compatible with passage to the associated series (see Exercise 1.22).
