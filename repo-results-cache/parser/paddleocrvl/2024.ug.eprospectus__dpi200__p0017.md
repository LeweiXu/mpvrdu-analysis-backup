## ABOUT

The National Institute of Education (NIE), Singapore, is the national teacher education institute and an integral part of the nation's education system. Offering multidisciplinary undergraduate and graduate programmes, the institute empowers you to analyse, tackle, and resolve challenges in various career pathways, both within and beyond the realm of education.

## SPORT SCIENCE & MANAGEMENT (SSM)

SSM is the only full-time undergraduate programme offered by a local tertiary institute that prepares students for sport-related careers. Under this multifaceted four-year programme, you will learn exercise physiology, sport biomechanics, motor skill acquisition, sport psychology, sport management and coaching, on top of other valuable skills.
