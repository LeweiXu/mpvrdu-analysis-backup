Only a fraction of these women will ever be diagnosed (Adamson, et al., 2010). Presently, women suffer from endometriosis-related pain for approximately 10 years before being diagnosed by their gynecologists with endometriosis (Ballard, et al., 2006; Krebs & Schoenbauer, 2019). While there have been many advances in medicine, endometriosis remains a disorder that receives inadequate attention from the medical community (Krebs & Schoenbauer, 2019).
