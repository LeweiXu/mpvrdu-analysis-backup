ADDITIONAL REMARKS :

Short field description

A very deep, excessively drained, white to light gray sand.

The top horizon (Ah) may be divided into two thin layers: 0-3 cm dark colour and 3-11 cm a bleached colour.

The deep subsoil horizon C2 has a more bleached colour than the overlying C1 horizon.

The soil site is located in a sand quarry.

The white sands belongs to the Iquitos Formation.

"X" in the climatic data refers to cloud cover in eights.
