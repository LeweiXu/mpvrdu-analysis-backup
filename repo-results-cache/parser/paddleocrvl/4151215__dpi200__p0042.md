## Irish Repertory Theatre

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_1156_141_1454_427.jpg" alt="Image" width="9%" /></div>


In the 2018-19 season, Irish Repertory, a theatre company dedicated to the Irish American experience, produced 100% White writers helmed by 100% White directors with 98.6% White actors. Out of all 11 shows produced this season, only 1 Black actor was inclusively cast and 1 Asian American designer was hired, making them the least diverse theatre company on our list.
