#### 4.4.4 Use of the impact factor

The majority of respondents don’t use the impact factor in their selection process when looking for articles (Table 4.9). There was no significant difference between the groups regarding the effect of impact factor on their choices ( $ \chi^{2}=04 $, df = 2, p-value = 0.978).
