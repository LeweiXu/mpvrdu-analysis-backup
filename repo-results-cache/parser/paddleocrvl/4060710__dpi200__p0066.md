### 7.2 Results of LFPG to LORP

#### 7.2.1 LFPG to LORP Leg-1: Short-term TP

The short-term TP (refer to Section 3) is applied to propagate the following uncertainties:

- the meteorological uncertainty linked to the future location of the convective cells (inherent to the forecast process);

- the uncertainty in the initial condition, caused either by the uncertainty in the take-off time or by the uncertainty in the trajectory generated by a previously applied trajectory predictor; and

- the operational uncertainty linked to the storm avoidance strategy, as dictated by the topology of the field of storm cells.

This results in an ensemble of propagated trajectories composed by 750 individual trajectories (see Figure 46). In this Figure, which provides a snapshot after 1h of computations, it can be observed the variability in positions due to both the initial condition and the encounter and avoidance of the thunderstorms.
