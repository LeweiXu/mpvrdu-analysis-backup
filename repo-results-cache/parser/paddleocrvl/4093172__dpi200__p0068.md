## Remaining Need: The Regional Water Balance

The first step in determining the remaining need is to evaluate the balance of existing levels of supplies against future projections of demands. Constructing a "Do Nothing" water balance provides a picture of what future reliability would look like with no additional actions or investments in water supply or demand management. The "Do Nothing" analysis determines whether additional developments that help to balance supplies and demands are needed to ensure reliability into the future. This look at the regional water balance incorporates all of the forecasts of demands and supplies described previously in this report.
