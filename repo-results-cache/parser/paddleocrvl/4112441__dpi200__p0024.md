We denote by C the intersection of the two kernels:

 $$ C=\{c\in D\mid\exists m\in M such that q_{B}^{D}(c)=0_{m}^{B},\quad q_{A}^{D}(c)=0_{m}^{A}\}, $$ 

which is called the core, and together with the map  $ q_{C}: c \mapsto m $,  $ (C, q_{C}, M) $ is also a vector bundle over M.
