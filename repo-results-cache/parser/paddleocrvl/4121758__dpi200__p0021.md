#### 6.8.3 Claims

Consolidated gross claims incurred by the general insurance industry totalled MK3.0 billion in 2009. This represents a 21.5 percent increase from the MK2.5 billion incurred in 2008. The deterioration in the claims trend is largely attributable to an increase in motor claims which accounted for 80.7 percent or MK2.4 billion.

In terms of net claims incurred by the industry, MK2.7 billion was reported for 2009 and represents a 16.9 percent increase from 2008 (Chart 7). Claims ratio (net claims incurred as a percentage of net earned premium) remained fairly static at 55.0 percent (2008: 54.2 percent). Among the classes of insurance, motor reported the highest claims ratio of 59.4 percent.
