Carrots (diced)

▶ Egg noodle

## Procedure:

Preheat at  $ 180^{\circ} $C /  $ 356^{\circ} $F in braising mode

▶ Pour oil to the pan, add chicken and begin to brown

▶ Next, add the onions, carrots, celery, spices and stir for 2 minutes

Add the stock to the braising pan and bring to boil

▶ Add noodles and stir

▶ Close the lid and cook under pressure ☐ for 10 minutes
