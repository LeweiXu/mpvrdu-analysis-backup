## Los Angeles Portal Testing vs Total LA County Cases As of May 20, 2020

Key Takeaways:

Since the Los Angeles portal testing sites opened to asymptomatic people, the overall number of tests has increased 165%. Over the same timeframe, total COVID-19 cases have increased about 76%.

The current positive rate at portal sites is 4.8%.

The overall County positive rate is 9.1%.
