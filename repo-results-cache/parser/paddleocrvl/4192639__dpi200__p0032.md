# YOU ARE NOT YOUR FAULT AND OTHER REVELATIONS The Collected Wit and Wisdom of Wes “Scoop” Nisker

WES "SCOOP" NISKER

Buddhism • Trade Paperback • 9781593766399 $16.95 • 272 pages • 6" x 9" • Ebook available

In this collection of his best-known essays as well as a selection of recent and never before published work, Wes “Scoop” Nisker takes readers on both a cultural journey (a tour through the sixties, through the modern environmental movement, the surge of Buddhism to the West) and a personal one, exploring the motivation behind humanity’s search for spiritual enlightenment.

About the author: Wes “Scoop” Nisker is the author of the enduring classic Crazy Wisdom and the widely acclaimed Buddha’s Nature. He is the editor of the Buddhist journal Inquiring Mind. A San Francisco celebrity, having been on Bay Area radio for more than twenty-five years, Nisker is a teacher of Buddhist meditation and philosophy. He lives in Berkeley.
