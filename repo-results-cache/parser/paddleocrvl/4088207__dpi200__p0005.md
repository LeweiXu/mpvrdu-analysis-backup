## PERFORMANCE OF BUSINESS UNITS

## NORDIC FIBREBOARD LTD

Fibreboard sales for 2020 were € 10.00 million, which is 15% decrease from year 2019 sales of € 11.75 million. We sold our products to customers in 28 countries during the year 2020. The main reason for the drop in sales was the loss of display board customers, boards for this sector was produced in the Püssi factory, which we closed in March 2020. However, sales to our customers within the construction sector in our main Northern European and Russian markets have been stable. Finland, our largest market accounted for 26% of total fibreboard sales in 2020 year (up from being 22% of total sales in 2019), Denmark is our second largest market, accounting for 22% of sales in the 2020 (it has also increased compared to year 2019 when it accounted for 14% of sales). Sales in Estonia and Russia have remained at the same level as previous year, while we recorded sales decline in markets further afield mainly due to the negative effect in many such markets caused by the corona virus.

Fibreboard recorded EBITDA of € 597 thousand for full year 2020 (2019: € 68 thousand). The main reason for the improvement in EBITDA were due to increased efficiency by achieving full machine utilisation in the Pärnu factory in 2020.
