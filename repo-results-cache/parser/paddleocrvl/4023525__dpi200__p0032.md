When I visualized proportions of mature animals per day of a lunar month the most prominent difference was no  $ L-cry^{-/-} $animals maturing during the FM-ON phase, contrary to small percentage of  $ L-cry^{+/-} $and  $ L-cry^{+/+} $maturing animals (Figure 1d-e). Instead,  $ L-cry^{-/-} $animals start maturing on the very first day of the FM-OFF and peak soon after. To contrast,  $ L-cry^{+/+} $animals mature during the FM-ON, continue maturing after the FM lights go OFF to peak in the second part of the FM-OFF phase. Interestingly,  $ L-cry^{+/-} $have distinct peaks in first and second part of the FM-OFF phase.
