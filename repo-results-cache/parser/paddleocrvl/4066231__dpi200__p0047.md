# ANNEXE VII: QUADRIGA INVESTORS – AQUA FUND

## Characteristics of the Sub-Fund

## Investment Objective and Policy

The objective of Quadriga Investors – Aqua Fund (the “Sub-Fund” in the present section) is to provide investors with an actively managed absolute return strategy which combines long only investments in US Treasury securities or derivatives and long-only option strategies.

The investment strategy will be achieved by investing in the following assets classes:

i. US Treasury securities: a long directional bias to US Treasury securities via direct or indirect investment through derivatives, in which the Sub-Fund will maintain a minimum long exposure of 50% notional of the net asset value of the Sub-Fund at all times with a maximum of 100%, with no leverage on US Treasury securities;

ii. Insurance via put and/or call options: price insurance via the purchase of put and/or call options on US Treasury securities. Put options will be used to hedge the long exposure to US Treasury securities so as to enhance capital preservation. Depending on the markets situations, this component will represent between 0% and 100% of the net asset value of the Sub-Fund.

iii. Tail risk insurance: investment in options to profit from tail risk scenarios which may impact negatively assets considered to be riskier due to current market conditions. This means that the Sub-Fund will trade positions to make capital gains in case of extreme and/or distressed market situation. This tail risk insurance will be implemented via investment in long only options on equities, credit or US Treasury securities, with a maximum notional exposure of 100-200%;

iv. Liquidity: the Sub-Fund will invest in short-term US Treasury bills or bonds actively selected by the Management Company. Subject to the applicable conditions and limits set out in section “Investment restrictions” of the prospectus, the Sub-Fund may also hold on an ancillary basis in cash, cash equivalent (including short-term bonds) and/or deposits.

Long or short exposure will be achieved through the use of derivative instruments on the Sub-Fund’s asset classes as further listed below.

The worst-case scenario for the long-only option strategies (both the call and put options) might be the loss of the premium paid upfront, which may lead to a high volatility of the net asset value. The Sub-Fund's long positions will be sufficiently liquid to cover at all times the Sub-Fund's obligations, including the payments of the premia related to the options.
