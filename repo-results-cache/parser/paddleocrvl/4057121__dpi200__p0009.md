## Non-GAAP Financial Measures

Media Cash Flow is defined as operating income, plus depreciation and amortization (including amortization of program broadcast rights), non-cash compensation and corporate overhead, loss on disposal of assets less payments for program broadcast obligations.

Adjusted Media Cash Flow is defined as Media Cash Flow less corporate and administrative expenses.

Media Cash Flow Margin is defined as Media Cash Flow divided by net revenue.
