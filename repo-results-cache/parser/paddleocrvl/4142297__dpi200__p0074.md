<div style="text-align: center;">Figure 3.13 The fluorescence change of ArcLight-A242 VSFP induced by local KCl application</div>


The cortical neurons were transfected with psc_HCMV_ArcLight construct and optically recorded at DIV15/DPT5. Adding 0.15M KCl solution locally to the cell in 5 portions of 100  $ \mu $l every 5 s, the fluorescence decrease has been observed at 200 ms exposure time. This implies the stimulation of action potentials followed by the decrease of fluorescence by  $ 11.27 \pm 1.51\% $.

Observing both of the proteins upon membrane depolarisation in the green filter channel, it is noticeable that their fluorescence intensity changes in the expected direction. The decrease in fluorescence intensity in both VSFP expressing cortical neurons (Figure 3.12, Figure 3.13), correlates with the time when 0.15M KCl solution was locally applied towards the measured cell. Since the solution was injected manually, the time span between adding each portion is not exactly 5 s. In the case of ArcLight A-242, in total 200 data points from raw data prior and after the peaks (0 - 5 s and 30 - 67 s) were used to calculate  $ F_{0} $. The maximum value,  $ F_{max} $, is the highest value of the peak. Those values were used to calculate  $ \Delta F/F_{0} $. For this measurement,  $ \Delta F/F_{0} $ is  $ 22.6 \pm 4.37\% $ (n = 5). The signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) was 4.43.
