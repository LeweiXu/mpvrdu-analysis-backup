# THE FAITH TO DOUBT

Glimpses of

Buddhist Uncertainty

STEPHEN BATCHELOR

Memoir • Trade Paperback • 9781619025356 $15.95 • 128 pages • 5.5" x 8.3" • Ebook available

In this engaging spiritual memoir, Stephen Batchelor describes his training, first as a Tibetan Buddhist and then as a Zen practitioner, and his spiritual struggles. "It is most uncanny that we are able to ask questions, for to question means to acknowledge that we do not know something... It includes a yearning to confront an unknown and illuminate it through understanding. Questioning is a quest."

A contemporary Buddhist teacher and writer, Batchelor is best known for his agnostic approach to Buddhism, arguing that it is a constantly evolving culture rather than an immutable religious system. Through his writings, translations, and teaching, Batchelor engages in a critical exploration of Buddhism's role in the modern world, which has earned him both condemnation as a heretic and praise as a reformer.

# THE GRASS FLUTE ZEN MASTER Sodo Yokoyama

ARTHUR BRAVERMAN

Zen • Trade Paperback • 9781619029132 $16.95 • 160 pages • 5.5" x 8.3" • Ebook available

Arthur Braverman is an American author and translator, primarily translating from Japanese to English. A Zen Buddhist practitioner, Braverman lived in Japan for seven years and studied at Antai-ji temple in 1969, training under Kōshō Uchiyama. He is the author of several Buddhist biographies, including Mud and Water: The Teachings of Zen Master Bassui, A Quiet Room: Poetry of Zen Master Jakushitsu, and Warrior of Zen: The Diamond-Hard Wisdom Mind of Suzuki Shōsan. He lives in Ojai, California.
