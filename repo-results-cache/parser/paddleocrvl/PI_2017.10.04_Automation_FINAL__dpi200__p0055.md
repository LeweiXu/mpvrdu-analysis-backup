Older Americans are especially supportive of this notion: 69% of those 65 and older would feel better about this concept if interviews were conducted traditionally and computers only did initial screenings.

On the other hand, the public responds much more negatively to the notion that these programs might use publicly available data about candidates (such as the material they post on social media) to help inform their decisions. Half of Americans say this practice would make them feel worse about this concept, while just 19% say this would make them take a more positive view.
