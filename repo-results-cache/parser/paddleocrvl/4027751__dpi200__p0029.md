The Denavit-Hartenberg parameters are the following:

●  $ a_{i} $ is the distance from  $ z_{i-1} $ to  $ z_{i} $ measured along  $ x_{i} $

$\alpha_{i}$ is the angle from $z_{i-1}$ to $z_{i}$ measured along $x_{i}$

●  $ d_{i} $ is the distance from  $ x_{i-1} $ to  $ x_{i} $ measured along  $ z_{i-1} $

•  $ \Theta_{i} $ is the angle from  $ x_{i-1} $ to  $ x_{i} $ measured along  $ z_{i-1} $
