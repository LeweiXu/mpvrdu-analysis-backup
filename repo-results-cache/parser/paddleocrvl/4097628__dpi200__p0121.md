- The Rochester (New York) Police Department (RPD) and Rochester Institute of Technology developed and validated a risk assessment tool as an effective predictive device in retaliatory gun violent disputes. For every one point increase in the assessment tool score that a dispute received, the odds of violence occurring in that dispute increased by 29 percent. Trends in aggravated assaults and murders in Rochester have continued to decline in the year and a half since the RPD implemented its SPI intervention.
