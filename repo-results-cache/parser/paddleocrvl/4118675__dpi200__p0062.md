Soil profile located on the left bank of the Urubamba river, in a fan (derived from colluvium-alluvium material). It is a deep, well drained, dark reddish brown clay loam.

This land has been cultivated for about 100 years, the last nine years with Alfalfa in rotation with crops such as potatoes, corn and lima beans. The technology level is high (use of fertilizers, mechanization and pesticides).
