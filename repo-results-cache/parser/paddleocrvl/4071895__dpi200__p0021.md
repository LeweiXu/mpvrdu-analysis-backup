The notion of cycle index series  $ Z_{F} $ gives a simultaneous generalization of both the series  $ F(x) $ and  $ \widetilde{F}(x) $. In fact, we have the following fundamental theorem.

Theorem 1.13. For any species of structures F, we have

 $$ \mathrm{a)}\ F(x)=Z_{F}(x,0,0,\cdots),\qquad\mathrm{b)}\ \widetilde{F}(x)=Z_{F}(x,x^{2},x^{3},\cdots). $$ 

Proof. To establish a), proceed as follows. Substituting  $ x_{1}=x $ and  $ x_{i}=0 $, for all  $ i\geq2 $, in equation (1.12) gives

 $$ Z_{F}(x,0,0,\cdots)=\sum_{n\geq0}\frac{1}{n!}\left(\sum_{\sigma\in\mathcal{S}_{n}}\mathrm{f i x}F[\sigma]x^{\sigma_{1}}0^{\sigma_{2}}0^{\sigma_{3}}\cdots\right). $$
