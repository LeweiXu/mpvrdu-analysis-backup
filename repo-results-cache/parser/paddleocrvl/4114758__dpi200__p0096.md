“Friends are like two ships signaling to each other on a foggy night; happy to have met each other, however brief the moment.”

—Anonymous.

Gianni Biserni, our foreign exchange student from Forli, Italy, became a close friend to all of us during his brief stay in the United States. Throughout the year he gave speeches and wrote stories for the school paper in order to promote understanding between our two countries. Also Gianni participated eagerly in all of the school and town activities. Perhaps even too eagerly … He broke his leg on his first attempt to ski.

We at Roosevelt High School will hold lasting memories of Gianni and wish him the best of luck in the future.
