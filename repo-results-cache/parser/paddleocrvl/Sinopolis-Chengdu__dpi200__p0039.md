## RESTAURANTS AND TEAHOUSES

## Chen Mapo Doufu

Named after its most famous dish (see above), this restaurant specialises in traditional Sichuan fare. In a city where commercial competition and the pace of culinary innovation are both relentless, it's a haven of old-fashioned deliciousness, and a good place to seek out not only the legendary tofu dish, but Gong Bao Chicken, twice-cooked pork, fish-fragrant pork slivers and other local classics. Officially designated a 'time-honoured brand' (laozi hao), the restaurant has several branches across Chengdu; local affectionadoes prefer the headquarters on Yulong Street to the north of the city centre.

Chen Mapo Doufu, 197 West Yulong Street, Luomashi, Qingyang District
