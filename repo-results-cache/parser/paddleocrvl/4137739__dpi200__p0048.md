## Technical Characteristics, Advanced Level

It is of the toroid type whose dispersion is minor which improves performance. It’s output is 96 VA.

It consists of a primary coil whose nominal voltage is 230 VAC, with a functioning margin of 207 VAC to 244 VAC. The secondary coil produces 24 VAC, 19 VAC and 12 VAC, with a maximum intensity of 4 amps.

## Operation:

It consists of a varistor which absorbs tension peaks which may be produced by the network beyond 275 v. If the voltage peaks are of long duration the varistor will short circuit and the 10 Amp fuse will blow.
