## Alaska Tribal Liaison Interagency Group (ATLIG): Working Together to Serve Tribes

## What Tribal Liaisons Do Within an Agency:

☑ Work within our agencies to try to ensure engagement and consultation when needed

☑ Work with tribes to build relationships

Recommendations to Senior Management on Potential Impacts, Sensitive Issues, etc.

☑ Provide an “Open Door” to Tribes/ANOs

■ Educate/Train Agency Personnel (Tribal Issues, Consultation, Cross-Cultural)
