# El Gouna Water Sports Festival

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_139_438_1018_949.jpg" alt="Image" width="75%" /></div>


With the sun shining, the wind blowing, the sound of cheers, plenty of smiles and applause, there was a charge of positive energy in the air to kick-start the day!

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_141_1187_1016_2175.jpg" alt="Image" width="75%" /></div>


The first and biggest Water Sport competition of its kind in Egypt and the region was launched and organized by the creators of Kitemania, @Flyevents, hosted by @Makani Beach Club and @El Gouna Red Sea, with four disciplines under one umbrella, adrenalin-pumping kitesurfing, wakeboarding, windsurfing and sailing.

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_140_2560_1015_3139.jpg" alt="Image" width="75%" /></div>


One hundred & fifty competitors from different nationalities and age groups signed up, all vying for some of the EGP 125000 in cash prizes, as well as kitesurfing gear offered to the GWSF winners. It was a very exciting weekend, packed with so much passion for sport, action and thrills.

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_140_3496_1016_4071.jpg" alt="Image" width="75%" /></div>


The weekend brought many people together for the love of the sport, from competitors to spectators and sports enthusiasts, creating so much opportunity to further expand the water sports scene in Egypt.
