## The Public Theater

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_1158_141_1453_427.jpg" alt="Image" width="8%" /></div>


Despite producing a play written by an Asian playwright, The Public had one of the worst percentages of any non-profit when it came to hiring Asian American actors, only 3.4% of all roles. For a company that used inclusive casting to cast 42.1% of their roles, it betrays a glaring area of unconscious bias when Asian American actors only accounted for less than a percentage point (0.7%). The Public Theater hired more Black and Latinx actors than any other non-profit, casting 61.2% of its roles with BIPOC actors, increasing from 53.6% the season prior. They hired 5 BIPOC writers out of 15, and were the only non-profit to produce a play written by a Latinx writer. At 26.7%, The Public had more BIPOC board members than any other non-profit and were second in the hiring of BIPOC designers. While there is still room for improvement, these factors combined to place the Public at the top of our Most Diverse list.
