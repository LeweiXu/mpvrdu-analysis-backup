# 1951-1961

Government of the People's Republic of Serbia issued a decree number 254 on July 11, 1951 on merging two state-owned companies – design bureaus of the People's Republic of Serbia "Hidro-Elektroprojekt" and "Termo-Elektroprojekt" into "Hidro-Termo-Elektroprojekt" (the name was soon changed in "Energoprojekt"). Engineer Živko Mučalov was appointed General Manager of the new company.

In order to create this, for former Yugoslavia, a giant company that in the beginning had only 120 employees and by October 1, 1951 almost 223 employees, Živko Mučalov had help from exceptional associates - Dimitrije Savić, Head of Thermal Department; Milan Verčon, Head of Hydro Department; Vladimir Ljubojević, Head of Electrical Department; Momčilo Đurović, Head of Administration and Rajko Joksimović, Head of Financial Department.
