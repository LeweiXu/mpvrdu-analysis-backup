# TRIPPING WITH ALLAH

Islam, Drugs, and Writing

## MICHAEL MUHAMMAD KNIGHT

Contemporary Islam • Trade Paperback • 9781593764432 $15.95 • 256 pages • 5.5" x 8.3" • Ebook available

Amazonian shamanism meets Christianity meets West African religion meets Islam in this work of reflection and inward adventure. Michael Mihammad Knight, the “Hunter S. Thompson of Islamic literature,” seeks reconciliation between his Muslim identity and his drinking of ayahuasca, a psychedelic tea that has been used in the Amazon for centuries. His experience becomes an opportunity to investigate complex issues of drugs, religion, and modernity.

Tripping with Allah provides a look into the construction of religion, the often artificial borders dividing these constructions, and the ways in which religion might change in an increasingly globalized world.
