# LETTER OF UNDERSTANDING NO. 8 RE: Cider Company Positions, Wage and Classification Changes

BC Tree Fruits Cooperative

(the "Employer")

-and-

United Food and Commercial Workers International Union,

Local 247

(the "Union")

July 13, 2018

In order to stay competitive in the labour market, to keep up with production in the most efficient way possible, and to encourage internal posting opportunities, the Employer and Union have entered into this letter of understanding.

## SCHEDULE AAAA - Creation, Names, and Wage Adjustments

As per Article 16 - Job Vacancies, sub Article 16.01 - Job Classifications, the employer will be adding to the Cidery operations a separate seniority list named SCHEDULE "AAAA" Classifications. All rights of seniority, posting practices, and inter-list movement will be commensurate with current established practices and the collective agreement.
