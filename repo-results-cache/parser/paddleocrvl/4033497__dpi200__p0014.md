## Recommendations

## Overflow Parking

30. Establish paved parking in the area that is currently Overflow Parking. Expand the parking lot towards the Children's Garden. Remove and relocate the existing parking in the east end of the Parade Ground to the newly paved Overflow Parking Lot. Eliminate the road leading to the Overflow Parking lot (Wheatly Road) and utilize the existing road system, where possible gain access to the newly paved parking in the Overflow Parking lot. Eliminate the section of road that extends beyond the overflow parking area and is behind the Children's Garden to reduce unnecessary impervious surfaces.

31. Establish a “green” corridor (native trees and shrubs) from the parking lot down to Powers Road along the north side of the new Overflow Parking lot. Establish a wide path with this corridor to allow for east-west pedestrian and bike circulation.

32. Establish a North – South path across this section of the Park. Repair the steps and path leading from the Overflow Parking lot area down the slope towards the Pump Station.

33. Establish a node and information kiosk at the Overflow Parking lot as both an organizing site element and to display Park information and maps.

34. Eliminate the old fort wall associated with the Fort's former hospital and crumbling steps. Re-grade and establish a new park space.

## Parade Ground

35. Bleachers- Consider the following Options

(Option A: Integration) - Integrate the bleachers into site by adding plants (trees and shrubs) between the parking area and bleachers. Repair or replace the existing bleachers to provide a safe and stable bleacher area. Establish an ADA accessible path from the parking area to the bleachers.

(Option B: Relocate) – Consider a new location (See Waterfront Section) for the bleachers.

36. Establish the existing road that runs along the south side of the parade ground as a walking promenade. Provide seating nodes and trees and plant pods. Add water and electrical infrastructure along this new promenade.

### 37. Ball Field

(Option A: Remain) – Leave the ball field as is.

(Option B: Relocate) – Relocate the ball field and related facilities to a location outside the park and integrate the field into the Town's other facilities. Loam and seed the area to establish lawn area.
