This kingdom is doomed, of course, because

the life force sustaining it belongs to an animate being and one day the guilt of rejecting Him will crush out the life of those who stubbornly refuse to acknowledge their identity as children of God. It is doomed because nothing can take away the pain and loss that come from rejecting that identity. Here we learn the truth of the proverb, “There is no rest for the wicked.”

If we return to the Garden of Eden within this context, we see that on the outside Satan seems cunning and clever as he seeks to thwart his expulsion from heaven. Yet on the inside there lies a heart full of emptiness and insecurity that is struggling to form a new identity and to escape the ever widening chasm of hopelessness.
