#### 8.2. Education Sector in Jhelum

##### 8.2.1. Education Facilities

In Jhelum the numbers of boys’ schools and girls’ schools are almost equal. There are 502 government boys’ schools and 515 government girls’ schools. In these 1,071 schools, 161,726 students are enrolled. High schools have 562 students per school on average (most of the high schools have 10 classrooms) and primary schools have 83 students per school on average.

This lack of subject specialists directly affects the quality of education in Jhelum District.
