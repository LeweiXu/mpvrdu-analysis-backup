## Geographic overview

The REIT’s portfolio is geographically diversified. As of March 31, 2021, the REIT’s 80 properties were located in 20 states with a presence in 22 MSAs. The REIT has 43 properties, or 53.8% of the total portfolio, located in the U.S. sunbelt region. Markets within this region benefit from strong underlying demographic trends, above average employment and population growth. This provides the REIT opportunities to progressively drive operational efficiencies and sustainable growth.
