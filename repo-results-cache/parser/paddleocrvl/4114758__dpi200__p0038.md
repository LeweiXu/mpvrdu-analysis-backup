“Devils Squeeze Out Victory,” the prize-winning Tri-Hi float exclaims.

No wonder Virginia fans were exultant! For the first time in Virginia’s football history, the determined Blue Devils won the Iron Range Conference. It took the team a year of hard work combined with skill in order to achieve such a reward. The team and all of the fans will remember always this success with pride.

An eventful football Homecoming added to the year's success. The activities began with the assembly where Helen Fondurulia was crowned. The other lovely candidates were Ardelle Johnson, Linda Peterson, and Sue Znameroski. Following the assembly, there was the traditional parade of floats led by the VHS band. Tri Hi's "Devil's Squeeze Out Victory" won the first place award. The evening's events began with a roaring bonfire and a victorious game followed by the dance.

The glowing bonfires set off the spark that ignited the flame of victory.
