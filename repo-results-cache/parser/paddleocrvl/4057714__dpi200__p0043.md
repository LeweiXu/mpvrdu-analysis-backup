## Canada’s Low-risk Alcohol Drinking Guidelines (2011)

## Daily:

• Women: 0–2 standard drinks

• Men: 0–3 standard drinks

## Weekly:

• Women: 0-10 standard drinks

• Men: 0-15 standard drinks

## What's a standard drink?

1.5 oz 40% spirits 5 oz 12% wine 12 oz 5% beer

• each equals 0.6 oz of absolute alcohol
