To expose this trend, I will turn first and primarily to the cases involving restrictions on “adult entertainment” establishments, including book stores, movie theaters, and nude dancing establishments that offer for sale sexually explicit performances or materials. Then, to demonstrate that disenchantment with morals rationales is not limited to the adult entertainment context, I will look to the Court’s analysis of restrictions on particular sexual acts, the cases regarding restrictions on “offensive” noncommercial speech, the “blue laws” jurisprudence, the Court’s treatment of non-nuclear family formations, and finally, to the analysis of the morals justification in Lawrence.
