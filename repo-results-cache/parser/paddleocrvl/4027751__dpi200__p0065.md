## Appendix A

## DS3225 digital servo specifications:

Electrical Specification :

Operating voltage range: 4.8 - 6.8V

Operating speed (5.0V): 0.15 sec/60 degree

Operating speed (6.8V): 0.13 sec/60 degree

Stall torque (5.0V): 21kg/cm

Stall torque (6.8V): 25kg/cm

Control Specification:

Control System: PWM(Pulse width modification)

Pulse width range: 500~2500  $ \mu $sec

Neutral position: 1500 $ \mu $sec

Running degree: 270° (when 500~2500  $ \mu $sec)

Dead band width: 3  $ \mu $sec

Operating frequency: 50-330Hz

Rotating direction: Counterclockwise (when 500~2500  $ \mu $sec)

Mechanical Specification:

Gear ratio: 275

Bearing: Double bearing

Connector wire: 17.7 inches (450 mm)

Motor: 3-pole(s)

Waterproof performance: IP66

Retrieved from: https://www.osepp.com/downloads/pdf/LS-0009AF_datasheet.pdf
