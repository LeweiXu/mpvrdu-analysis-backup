ADDITIONAL REMARKS :

Short field description

Deep, excessively drained pale brown sand. At a depth of 115 cm starts a thick, mainly by organic matter, cemented hard, water stagnating layer, the Spodic horizon.

The site is situated in an erosion gully. It is located near lake Quisto-Cocha.

"X" in the climate data refers to cloud cover in eights.

1) The soil is formed in the Iquitos formation, after the podzolisation process, consisting of homogenous white sands, principally consisting of quartz.

2) The original vegetation is a dense rain-forest, cleared for agriculture and abandoned as a forest fallow, locally known as "purma".
