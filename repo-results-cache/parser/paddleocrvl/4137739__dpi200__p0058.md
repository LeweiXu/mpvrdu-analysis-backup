#### 3.4.4 Low Voltage Detection

When the voltage goes 10% below the nominal, the machine shuts down. The control of the network voltage is achieved by controlling the 24 Vcc voltage creating a voltage divider with resistors R71 and R72 and applying the common point to the "+" of the operational amplifier LM324 (U17A) so that when the 24 Vcc diminishes by 30%, pin 1 of the U17A is tripped changing the +5 Vcc to 0 V and as it is connected to pin 18 of the U15, the latter informs the Control Card.
