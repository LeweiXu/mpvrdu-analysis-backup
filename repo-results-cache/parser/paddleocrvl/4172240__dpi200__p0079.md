Gurupa. IMO 8506438. Length 134 m, 5,064 t. Deflagged from Brazil to Gabon for her last voyage shortened to Guru. Classification society Lloyd's Register of Shipping. Built in 1987 in Papenburg (Germany) by Jos L Meyer Werft. Owned by Petroleo Brasileiro S.A (Brazil). Sold for demolition in India. 420 US$ per ton.

Gurupi. IMO 8516897. Length 134 m, 5,080 t. Deflagged from Brazil to Gabon for her last voyage shortened to Guru 1. Classification society American Bureau of Shipping. Built in 1987 in Papenburg (Germany) by Jos L Meyer Werft. Detained in 2004 in Punta Arenas (Chile). Owned by Petroleo Brasileiro S.A (Brazil). Sold for demolition in India. 420 US$ per ton.
