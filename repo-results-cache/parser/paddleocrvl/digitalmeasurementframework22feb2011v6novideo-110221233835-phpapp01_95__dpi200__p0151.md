## Developing and Using Campaign Codes for Tracking

#### English Definition: The campaign code (e.g. Smartcode) is what enables you to tie the cost and pipeline information to your campaign

## What is a Smartcode?

“Intelligent” promotion code tied to every campaign and key campaign elements.

Carried through your systems for better campaign tracking.

Improved reporting that includes lead and pipeline data.
