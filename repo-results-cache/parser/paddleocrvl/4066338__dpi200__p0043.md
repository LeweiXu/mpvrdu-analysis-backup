##### 3.2.3.3 LCH

The CIELCH color space is related to the CIELAB color space. It is a cylindrical version of CIELAB where C is the chroma channel and H is the hue channel. As with the CIELAB and CIELUV color spaces, the LCH color space attempts to gain perceptual uniformity for changes in color values.
