## Pitfalls to Avoid

• Preparing a group of data that do not tell a logical and compelling story of value

• Overemphasizing Level 1 and 2 data; underemphasizing Level 3 and 4 data

- Using too much narration and not enough visuals like graphs, charts, and bullets
