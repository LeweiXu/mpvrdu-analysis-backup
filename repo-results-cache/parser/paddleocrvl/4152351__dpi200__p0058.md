## TCI spreads (too) quickly?

In numerous letters and documents from Ruth C. Cohn's estate, there is a concern that Theme-centred Interaction is spreading too fast in Europe and beyond (especially India). The WILL organisation would not be able to keep up with the necessary qualification of TCI teachers. Ruth had initially approached psychotherapists of different schools in Europe, for whom TCI was an additional qualification. But soon scientists, teachers, pastors, people from the business world, etc. came to her seminars.
