## General Village Information: Know Where You are Going!

■ Populations: ~ 100 – 5000 populations

Alaska has been occupied by Alaska Natives for thousands of years.

Alaska communities are very different from each other – some based on salmon, others on whaling, others on walrus, others on reindeer herding, or fishing and hunting, etc.
