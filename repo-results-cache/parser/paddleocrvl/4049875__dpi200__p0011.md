### C. Fund Structure

## Fund

Investors may apply for Class A and Class B Units of the Fund, which is domiciled in Australia. The Fund is a managed investment scheme registered with ASIC under the Corporations Act. The Responsible Entity has appointed a number of service providers who are involved in the ongoing operation and administration of the Fund.

A diagram of the fund structure showing the key flows of investment cash is available in the section “Investment Structure of the Fund” on page - 15 - of this PDS.

The Fund invests in futures and derivatives on credit, volatility and foreign exchange. The collateral held at the level of the Fund will be held as cash, Cash Equivalents or interest-bearing instruments as explained in the section "Cash Management" on page - 21 - of this PDS.

## Key service providers of the Fund

- CFM LLP is the investment manager of the Fund and CFM SA is the investment advisor of the Fund.

HSBC is the administrator and custodian of the Fund.

KPMG is the independent auditor of the Fund.

- The Responsible Entity regularly monitors the performance of the Fund's key service providers against the service levels documented in its service agreements with each key service provider.

See "Service Providers on page 18 - for further information."

Related party relationships CFM LLP, the investment manager of the Fund, is an affiliate to CFM SA, which is the investment advisor of the Fund, being authorized to perform discretionary portfolio management.

Due diligence on the investments and service providers of the Fund The Responsible Entity has delegated the investment decision and monitoring processes of the Fund to the Investment Manager under the Investment Management Agreement. The Investment Manager determines the investments of the Fund. In relation to the service providers of the Fund, the Investment Manager follows a due diligence process in their appointment and ongoing monitoring of these service providers, assessing factors such as resourcing, capability, reputation and costs.
