Hope Duff Prepper.com writer - is one of our Old School Survival Boot Camp vendors. She will be selling patriotic and self-reliance inspired clothing and gear from her booth - along with paracod projects.

Adam Hellman - Making Survival Gear With Paracord and Spiders and Snakes

In the snakes and spiders class attendees will see live native snakes and learn how to identify venomous ones, how to avoid them, and how to treat a bite. Hellman will also share his knowledge about common Midwest spiders that can cause a substantial health issue if bitten by them - and which spiders you want to have around your homestead. Depending upon what Adam is able to catch before boot camp, there may be live spiders to view, as well. Adam is teaching both an adult and child version of this course.

## Paracord Make and Take

In both the child and adult classes, attendees will learn how to make and take their own survival paracord bracelet.
