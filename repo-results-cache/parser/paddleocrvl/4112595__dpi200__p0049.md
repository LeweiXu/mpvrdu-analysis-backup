### 5.2 Standard of Living

A measure of economic performance is per capita disposable income. While data is only available at a county level, the evidence indicates the disposable income per person in Limerick exceeded the national average but was still lower than in Dublin. Measures to assist Limerick to gain increases in per capita incomes should continue to be an objective of future plans for the city. Indecon, however, notes that a sustainable model of development should focus on more than standard measures of income.
