ADJUSTMENT OF A QUADRILATERAL

Method of Correlatives.

(Geodetic Quadrilateral)

In the plain quadrilateral as shown in above figure, there are seven angle conditions and three side conditions that must be satisfied to make such a figure geometrically possible. These ten conditions can all be covered by three angle equations and one side equation.

1. The sum of the eight corner angles must be exactly  $ 360^{\circ} $. This furnishes one angle condition.

2. The opposite angles where the diagonals cross must be equal. This furnishes two angle conditions.

3. In each of the four triangles formed among the stations, such as NP'D', the sum of the three angles must be exactly  $ 180^{\circ} $. This furnishes four angle conditions.
