## Abstract:-

The purpose of the paper is to prevent the drug interactions such as Adverse Drug Reactions (ADR) by using our technological solution which will help the treating doctors / physicians to explore the details of drugs in order to find out life threatening effects such as allergies, Adverse Drugs Reaction complications and the contraindications related to liver and renal defects and harmful side effects during pregnancy or lactation.
