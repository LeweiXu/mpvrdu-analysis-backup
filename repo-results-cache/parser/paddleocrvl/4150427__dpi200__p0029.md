## To recap:

In the Stony Rises a surplus animation flows through and from vast systems of ancient basalt guide-channels and collection-pools that have been construed and curated by the Gunditjmara people.

In the Stony Rises, ‘nature and culture have at least this much in common: both compel the living to serve the interests of the unborn.’ $ ^{3} $

In the Stony Rises, ‘the unborn’ are not only the people but also the eels and every other undead thing that depends both upon the reliable return of the seasons and upon the knowledgeable curation of the flowing energy in the winds and waters.
