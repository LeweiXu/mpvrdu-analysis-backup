## ABOUT

The Lee Kong Chian School of Medicine (LKCMedicine) offers a five-year Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Surgery (MBBS) programme designed to prepare you to become future-ready medical professionals who are discerning and confident users of technology while delivering compassionate, patient-centred care.

## LKCMEDICINE'S 5-YEAR PROGRAMME

YEAR 1 – Integrated science in a medical context 1

YEAR 2 – Integrated science in a medical context 2

YEAR 3 – Core clinical medicine in practice

YEAR 4 – Medicine for all stages of life & in diverse settings

YEAR 5 – Preparing for practice as a doctor
