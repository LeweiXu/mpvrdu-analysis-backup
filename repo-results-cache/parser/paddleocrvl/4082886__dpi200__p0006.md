The new millennium brought more favorable political, social and economic terms of doing business in the country. In that period, many companies got destroyed in the process of ownership transition. However, ENERGOPROJEKT successfully managed to remain the only large-size company in Serbia capable of facing new challenge and difficulties of the transitioning period and to realize all business plans at the same time. Many reputable Serbian construction companies were broken into pieces and sold to new owners having no interest or ambitions to invest capital in the companies' recovery and business growth. In contrast, based on the preserved financial potential and references gained in the foreign markets, ENERGOPROJEKT managed to build its way up and participate again in tenders at home and abroad as well as to overcome obstacles of the privatized banking system and state redundancy to stimulate development of the domestic economy.

In the first decade of transition changes, ENERGOPROJEKT proved its strength and vitality in the process of several hostile takeover attempts and managed to successfully defend itself thanks to great contribution of employees and management in cooperation with the administration of the Republic of Serbia. In the period of a positive change in the state's relation towards domestic companies, ENERGOPROJEKT also succeeded in reclaiming its position in the domestic and foreign markets by signing more contracts and completing more projects as well.

ENERGOPROJEKT, going through all of these crises, proved to be able to persevere in the difficult times by employing its human, material and financial resources that are also a prerequisite for a rapid and comprehensive development today. Whether ENERGOPROJEKT will continue to develop in the existing ownership structure or this area will face changes, greatly depends on the ability to adjust to the demands of the environment, the market and investors at home and abroad, as well on meeting expectations of the owners, employees and investors.

In order to achieve all of the aforementioned, it is necessary to reaffirm the traditional values of ENERGOPROJEKT and develop new ones, as shown in this monograph published on occasion of the ENERGOPROJEKT 65th anniversary. Finally, it takes many years of perseverance and self-sacrifice to create and develop such a complex and strong business system but only a few years to destroy it. Having this in mind, all employees are firm in the decision to continue building a better ENERGOPROJEKT, thus building a better world.
