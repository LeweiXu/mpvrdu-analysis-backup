## Building Competency in Tribal Engagement

## Foundational Engagement Competencies:

Ask how mission/activity could affect subsistence and their perspective on ways to minimize impacts

Provide clear information you know -- Don’t force them to ask the “right” question

Allow tribal members to discuss issues in their own language.

Allow for periods of silence.

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_1527_7_1970_338.jpg" alt="Image" width="22%" /></div>


Re-state the tribal concern to confirm your understanding of it.

Don’t speak loud, fast, argue, or point.

Don’t put others down, or use jargon or abbreviations.

PRACTICE and consider what issues or concerns might arise BEFORE the meeting happens.
