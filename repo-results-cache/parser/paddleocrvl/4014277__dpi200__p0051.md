###### 2.8.3.1. Doha Securities Market

The first low number 14 of financial market was issued in 1995, while the official trading started on May 1997 with 17 companies and market value at one billion USD. In 2005 the law was issued to allow foreign investors to own a maximum of 25% of the shares traded, in the same year, Qatar Financial Markets Authority was established to organize and supervise the activities of the financial market. and later in 2009 the name of the market was changed from the Doha market to the Qatar Stock Exchange QSE after the partnership agreement with NYSE Euronext.
