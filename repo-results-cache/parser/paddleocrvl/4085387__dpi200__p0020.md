# CONGRATULATIONS!

THALEA STRING QUARTET

## THE 2021 ANN DIVINE EDUCATOR AWARD WINNER

Returning in November 2021 for the Fischoff Arts-in-Education Residency.

The Fischoff National Chamber Music Association is delighted to announce the Thalea String Quartet as the winner of Fischoff's 2021 Ann Divine Educator Award. This national award recognizes the educational outreach work of Fischoff Competition alumni who have demonstrated outstanding and imaginative programming for children and youth in the United States.
