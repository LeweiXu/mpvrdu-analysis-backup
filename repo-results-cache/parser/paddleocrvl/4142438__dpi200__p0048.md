Under Code section 111(c), if a deduction creates or increases a loss carryforward of the taxpayer, the NOL is treated as a tax benefit despite the fact that the deduction has not yet provided any reduction in taxes and will do so only in the future when it may be applied to offset future income. $ ^{201} $ All three tax benefit cases involved precisely this situation: the taxpayer had accrued and deducted, but not yet paid interest on the cancelled debt, and the accruals created NOL carryforwards (rather than reductions of current tax) which remained alive at the time of the agreement. $ ^{202} $
