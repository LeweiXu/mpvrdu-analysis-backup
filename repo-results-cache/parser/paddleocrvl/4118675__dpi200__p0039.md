A moderately deep, well drained, dark brown clay loam. The deeper subsoil has a yellowish brown colour. Soil profile located in a man-made terrace with 5 to 8 % slope which is a mountainside with 30% slope. The mountainside without man-made terraces show an increase in its grade of erosion and some landslide. "Others" in the climate data refers to the cloud cover in eights.
