## Proper interventions

Senior leadership got personally involved in major initiatives where cross-organizational teams did not get assembled and ensure they did as quickly as possible.

Training analyzed the reasons cited for lower confidence than expected to implement the plan. Based on feedback from training graduates, training team members provided a “mentor-the-mentor” program, providing support with leaders new to mentoring or who just wanted more assistance in the role.

## Pitfalls to Avoid

• Allowing participants to ignore post-training evaluation activities

• Failing to look at all data objectively

• Overreacting to one-off comments (either positive or negative)
