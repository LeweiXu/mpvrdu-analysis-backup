## Mobile Internet Payment Options

How do users pay for mobile ecommerce in Indonesia?

Indonesia is a predominantly prepaid market, carrier billing can be considered as airtime/balance deduction. Upon confirmation, the user&#x27;s airtime balance will be deducted and payment is completed.</td></tr><tr><td style='text-align: center; word-wrap: break-word;'>Mimopay</td><td style='text-align: center; word-wrap: break-word;'>Mimopay is the pioneer of accepting 3rd party payments using telco&#x27;s prepaid card (Smartfren&#x27;s). Mimopay works with XL and Telkomsel for carrier billing. Similar to Coda, Mimopay also require users to key in their mobile phone no to confirm via SMS. For Smartfren prepaid reload cards, users can just key in the secret code into the merchants&#x27; website to claim the credits. Mimopay is the first PSP in Indonesia that deploys its own Android SDK to assist apps merchants in adopting Mimopay&#x27;s various channels.</td></tr><tr><td style='text-align: center; word-wrap: break-word;'>Mandiri e-cash</td><td style='text-align: center; word-wrap: break-word;'>Mandiri e-cash is the latest product of Mandiri, the largest bank in Indonesia. Mandiri e-cash has UMB/USSD access menu on every telco (*141*6#) or an app for Android/iOS users. Mandiri e-cash enables people with no bank account to easily own a Mandiri e-cash account by using their mobile phone no as their account no. This is a common product for banks to have because of the huge amount of unbanked people in Indonesia.</td></tr><tr><td style='text-align: center; word-wrap: break-word;'>BCA Klikpay</td><td style='text-align: center; word-wrap: break-word;'>BCA Klikpay is BCA&#x27;s answer to PayPal. BCA is the largest consumer bank in Indonesia, it has the largest no of ATMs and EDCs in the country. BCA Klikpay allows users to deduct their bank account balance directly. Most people own a BCA account in major cities across Indonesia, so this is a very convenient payment method.</td></tr><tr><td style='text-align: center; word-wrap: break-word;'>Doku/Veritrans/ E2Pay/Indopay/ Finpay/IPayMu/ IPay88</td><td style='text-align: center; word-wrap: break-word;'>Doku and IPayMu aim to be Indonesia&#x27;s PayPal. While Veritrans, E2Pay, Indopay, Finpay, and IPay88 are credit cards payment processor. Doku has its own wallet and has the widest coverage of merchants. Major merchants utilize Doku Wallet and Doku as their credit cards payment processor.</td></tr></table>
