## Introduction

Planning, training, reinforcement, and evaluation up to this point should have created significant value to the agency if they were conducted effectively. Now it is time to demonstrate that value. It can take multiple forms for several different audiences. These audiences include, but are not limited to:

• Agency stakeholders

• OPM

• The American public

Each audience will likely require a different type of effort and demonstration.

In this stage you will build your chain of evidence and demonstrate ROE. You will tell a “story of value”. This will show the “return on” by all those involved for the “expectations” of the stakeholders. If good work has been done during the first two stages, demonstration of value is relatively easy.
