Flying too high a penetration airspeed makes the standard profile more like an optimum ground effect profile and therefore decreases range gained using the ground effect profile. Thus, if the penetration airspeed is not precisely known or the airspeed indicator is not accurately calibrated, a standard profile should be flown about 2 knots faster than the estimated penetration speed for each 10 knots of headwind.
