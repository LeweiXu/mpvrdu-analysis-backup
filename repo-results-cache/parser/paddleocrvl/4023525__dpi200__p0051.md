## 5 Discussion

Platynereis dumerilii possess light entrained circular and circadian clocks controlling lunar timing of sexual maturation and daily locomotor activity respectively  $ (12) $. Cryptochromes were confirmed to act as blue light receptors mediating light entrainment in animals like Soganus luridus and D. melanogaster  $ (11,25) $. Platynereis possess a light responsive drosophila-type cryptochrome ortholog L-cry  $ (12) $, so I hypothesized that L-cry loss-of-function Platynereis mutants  $ (L-cry^{-/-}) $ exhibit difficulties in circular and circadian function as well as possible downstream mechanisms like development, maturation and regeneration. My experiments confirmed  $ L-cry^{-/-} $ exhibit a maturation delay likely caused by an overall developmental delay, altered lunar timing of sexual maturation and circadian rhythmicity issues indicating they are more susceptible to masking effect of light.

### 5.1 Sexual maturation delay and the circularunar clock

In spite of the circular-nuclear clock advising animals to spawn shortly after new moon, in our laboratory culture approximately 6% of  $ L-cry^{+/-} $ and  $ L-cry^{+/+} $ animals (figure 1d-e) spawned during the full moon (FM-ON). To contrast, none of the 116  $ L-cry^{-/-} $ animals matured during FM-ON ( $ p<7\cdot10^{-3} $; Figure 1f). Even though  $ L-cry^{-/-} $ maturation seems to have a circular-nuclear rhythm in periodic lunar conditions, it is significantly different from  $ L-cry^{+/-} $ and  $ L-cry^{+/+} $ maturation patterns.  $ L-cry^{-/-} $ maturation peaks soon after the FM lights go off while  $ L-cry^{+/+} $ maturation peaks towards the end of FM-OFF, which in nature translates to'soon after NM'.  $ L-cry^{+/-} $ spawning pattern looks like a sum of both  $ L-cry^{-/-} $ and  $ L-cry^{+/+} $ patterns (Figure 1e), which indicates that one functioning L-cry allele is influencing the circular-nuclear periodicity.
