# 6.7 INSURANCE INDUSTRY OVERVIEW

The insurance industry assets grew by 14.5 percent from MK60.4 billion in 2008 to MK69.1 billion during the year under review. About 84.6 percent of these assets belong to the life insurance sector which recorded a growth rate of 14.8 percent in 2009, compared to 12.3 percent asset growth for general insurance.

However, in terms of premium income, general insurance accounted for about 81.3 percent of the industry's gross written premiums during the year. Life insurance gross premiums contracted by 9.9 percent, compared to 25.2 percent growth for general insurance. Total insurance gross premiums increased by 12.3 percent to MK9.8 billion in 2009 (2008: MK8.7 billion). As a result of the slow performance, insurance penetration $ ^{3} $ was recorded at 1.5 percent in the review period. This is significantly lower than the 2.1 percent attained in 2008 mainly as a result of factoring out pension contributions collected by the insurance companies.

### 6.8 GENERAL INSURANCE

#### 6.8.1 Overall Performance

The general insurance sector continued to grow in 2009 albeit at a slower pace as compared to 2008. The core business of insurance underwriting substantially increased while at the same time claims expenditure decreased resulting in a significant improvement in underwriting results. Investment income however, dropped in 2009 forcing the industry to post lower operating profit than the previous year resulting in an overall decrease in the performance of the industry.

#### 6.8.2 Premiums

##### 6.8.2.1 Gross premiums

Gross premium income for the general insurance industry grew by 25.2 percent to MK7.9 billion (Chart 1). All classes recorded increases in gross premium, however motor class continued to dominate the gross premiums by accounting for 57.8 percent of the premiums collected, followed by fire with 16.4 percent (Charts 2 and 3). For the last four years, gross premium income for the general insurance industry has been growing at an average rate of about 28.4 percent.
