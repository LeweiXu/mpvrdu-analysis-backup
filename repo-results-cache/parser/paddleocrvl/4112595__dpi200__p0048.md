As shown in Figure 5.2, the reduction in the number of people on the Live Register in the last decade has been more pronounced in Limerick City and County than in the rest of the country, indicating a marked improvement in the labour market in Limerick. Building on this success and responding to the post-Covid-19 impacts particularly on youth unemployment should be a key objective for the city. Of concern, however, is that recent evidence suggests the share of under 25s on the Live Register has increased from 12% to 15%.
