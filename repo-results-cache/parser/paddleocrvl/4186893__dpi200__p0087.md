### 26. Non-operating miscellaneous income

In the case that the Company sells non-listed securities, which were acquired by exercising compensation rights, etc., the proceeds collected which exceed the fair value at the time of acquisition of those securities are credited to the non-operating miscellaneous income. Further, in case that the Company sells restricted listed shares, which were acquired by exercising compensation rights, etc., the amounts which are calculated by deducting the fair value at the time of acquisition of those listed securities from market value of those shares at the lapse of restricted terms are credited to the nonoperating miscellaneous income. Such accounting treatment of the Company resulted in nonoperating miscellaneous income of ₩14,876,215 thousand and ₩3,186,674 thousand for the years ended March 31, 2009 and 2008, respectively.
