## Ingredients for 90 lt/24 gal pan

Basmati rice

▶ Warm water

Carrots (chopped)1,5 kg / 3,3 lbs

Green beans

Scarlot (chopped)

Eggs (30 pcs)

Baked ham

Water

3x1,5 kg / 3x3,3 lbs

3x2,5 lt /3x0,66 gal

1,5 kg / 3,3 lbs

1 kg / 2.2 lbs

60 g / 2 oz each

1 kg / 2.2 lbs

6 It / 1,58 gal

Note: for the 90 lt / 24 gal pan the receipt is done in 5 GN1/1 trays: 3 trays for rice, 2 trays for vegetables

## Procedure:

▶ Rinse rice 2-3 times with cold water

Put 1.5 kg / 3.3 lbs of rice on each tray H60 with 1.7 kg / 3.7 lbs of warm water per each tray

Use false bottom for trays placed on the bottom and suspended frame for the trays placed on the top

Add 6 lt / 1,58 gal of water in the well and get boil at 220°C / 428 °F in braising mode

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_654_1201_696_1242.jpg" alt="Image" width="2%" /></div>


TIP: with small quantities of liquid preheat in braising mode to speed up the cooking process

▶ Close the lid and cook under pressure ☐ for 4 minutes

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_656_1383_695_1424.jpg" alt="Image" width="2%" /></div>


TIP: to ensure the best performance wait that water boils before starting the pressure phase

Open the lid and remove all the rice



▶ Add the vegetables on trays

▶ Close again the lid and cook under pressure ☐ for 2 minutes

Remove the vegetables, false bottoms and suspended frames

▶ Rinse and dry the well

Preheat again at 180°C/356°F in braising mode

Add oil, eggs on one side, on the other side the cooked vegetables

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_1141_1232_1180_1271.jpg" alt="Image" width="2%" /></div>


TIP: put the desired salt in the eggs

Stir for few seconds, add the rice and stir for few seconds more

Ready to serve
