With the aim to avoid the extinction, we applied the partial control method [23]. First we have constructed a map. Different choices are possible, but in this case we have chosen to build the map with the successive local minima  $ (P_{n}, P_{n+1}) $, where  $ P_{n} $ denotes the nth local minimum. This set of points generates an approximately one-dimensional curve of the form  $ P_{n+1} = f(P_{n}) $, shown in Fig. 10. Notice that, the iterates of any initial point for which  $ P_{n} > P^{*} $, follow a chaotic dynamics until they finally asymptotes to zero when it crosses a critical value  $ P_{n} < P^{*} $, which actually implies the extinction of the predators population. The map constructed through this way is just an approximation, so we also introduce a disturbance term  $ \xi_{n} $ into the map, in order to model potential mismatches.
