We estimate the time scales of AMOC variability by calculating the variance spectrum of AMOC anomalies at each latitude (Figure 4.8). The variance spectrum reveals frequencies or periods for which variations are largest, expressed by the power of variance.

Decadal variability on time scales of  $ \sim $20 years and more is smaller in all Middle Miocene experiments compared to PCTL, but we have to acknowledge that results may not be robust for the short interval under consideration. Variability of Barents-shelf overflows, an important source of deep water in MCTL and MTET, dominates on interannual time scales of 4–10 years between 75 and 80°N, but is less pronounced in MTET.
