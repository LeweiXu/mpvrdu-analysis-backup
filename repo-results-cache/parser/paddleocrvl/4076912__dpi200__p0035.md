####### IV. 2.2.1.2. Translational and applied research

Translational and applied research accounted for about 2.97 million uses of animals in 2018.

The four main areas of translational and applied research were animal diseases and disorders, human cancer, human nervous and mental disorders and human infectious disorders. The inclusion of data from Norway brought animal diseases and disorder to first place (+340%), and significantly increased uses for animal welfare (+129%).

Proportionally lowest severities were reported for plant diseases, animal welfare, human endocrine/metabolism disorders (Figure 18).
