# Your home away from home

Being happy where you’re living is such an important part of your student experience. That’s why we have a fantastic choice of accommodation available for you, and it’s really important to start your research early.

We offer a huge range of both University and private accommodation to suit every budget. All our accommodation is high quality, modern, and meets high safety standards. And it is located in convenient locations on – or close to – campus.

We can offer flexible accommodation contracts, perfect if you're a Pre-sessional English (PEAP) student or if you're starting a Masters in January.
