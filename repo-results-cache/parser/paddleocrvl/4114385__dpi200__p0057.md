## Inequality Across Generations

Human capital investment decisions often made by parents

• High-income parents typically invest more  $ \Rightarrow $ positive correlation between socioeconomic outcomes of the parents and the outcomes of their children

• Regression towards the mean: A tendency for income differences across families to decrease over time as families move toward the mean income in the population

• Not all parental income is invested in children

• Diminishing returns to education

• Regression toward the mean in ability
