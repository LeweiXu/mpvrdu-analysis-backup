In the EU, around 80% of Atlantic salmon supply went to retailers while the remainder was sold to foodservice establishments. This is a higher retail share than in previous years due to Covid-19 restrictions, which partly closed the foodservice sector. It is expected that the foodservice sector will recover when restrictions are eased. Approximately 70% was sold fresh. Of the different products, fillets had the largest market share of 45% followed by smoked. “Other VAP” consists of all value-added processed products, except smoked salmon.
