#### 1.1.5 Using combinatorial operations on species

Another way of producing species of structures is by applying operations to known species. These operations (addition, multiplication, substitution, differentiation, etc.) will be described in detail in Sections 2.1, 2.2, 2.3, and 2.4. Here are some examples:

– the species  $ E^{3} $, of tricolorations,  $ E^{3} = E \cdot E \cdot E $,

– the species  $ E_{+} $, of non-empty sets,  $ 1 + E_{+} = E $,

– the species H, of hedges (or lists of rooted trees), H = L(A),

– the species Der, of derangements,  $ E \cdot Der = S $,

– the species Bal, of ballots (or ordered partitions).
