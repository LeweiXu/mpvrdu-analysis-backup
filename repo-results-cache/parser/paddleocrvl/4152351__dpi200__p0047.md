Those who act courageously are aware of the risk involved. Therefore, courageous people are usually not free of (physical, psychological, social) fear. This actual fear of the risk must be distinguished from that "hang over fear" that we carry within us - not least from childhood. Fear can be so great that it "eats up" all the energy to act courageously. Courageous people have strong concerns, values, beliefs/trust that inspire their actions. They "find solutions to many problems that could not be found if they were fearfully and rigidly stuck in old concepts and patterns" (Cohn)
