## Abstract:-

The purpose of this paper is analytical study of microcantilever based force sensor using piezoresistive sensing element. Microcantilever based force sensor is a low cost, highly sensitive leads to improve the reliability, repeatability and cost effectiveness of MEMS/NEMS technology, which makes it suitable for micromanipulation application such as microgripper and microbotics. The dimension of fixed part of sensor is  $ 1000 \mu m^{2} $ with height of  $ 200 \mu m $. The microcantilever have dimensions  $ 130 \mu m $ length,  $ 40 \mu m $ breadth and  $ 50 \mu m $ height along with piezoresistive sensing element mounted on it. The range of force per unit area applied of it is  $ 5 \times 10^{6} $ N/m $ ^{2} $ which is sensed by cantilever, can be observed from simulation results. The amount of force required by microbot for micromanipulation is in the Micro-Netwon to Nano-Newton.
