## Roman Grobic

Post Doc., Department of Chemistry, Umeå University roman.grabic@chem.umu.se

Roman Grabic is a researcher within environmental and analytical chemistry. He performs method development of new techniques for LC/MS/MS and LC/LCMS/MS analysis of pharmaceuticals in the environment. He has developed multiresidual methods for analysis of wide range of pharmaceuticals in different matrixes as drinking, surface and waste water or biological fluids.

## Malena Göransson

Student, Royal Institute of Technology (KTH)

malenag@kth.se

Malena Göransson is a student from the environmental biotechnology programme at KTH doing her Masters thesis project within Mistra-Pharma. The aim of the project is to make a systematic analysis of the sensitivity of standard vs. non-standard tests for environmental risk assessment of pharmaceuticals.

## Joakim Larsson

Associate Professor, the Sahlgrenska Academy at the University of Gothenburg. joakim.larsson@fysiologi.gu.se

Leading research on drugs in the environment since 1998, now merging toxicogenomics with ecology and pharmacology. Contributed to identifying ethinylestradiol as drug of environmental concern in the 1990ies. Recently identified production sites as a source for drugs to the environment. Project leader in the MistraPharma programme.

## Richard H Lindberg

Post Doc., Department of Chemistry, Umeå University richard.lindberg@chem.umu.se

Richard Lindberg is a researcher within environmental chemistry. He performs research about the fate of pharmaceuticals in sewage water treatment plants and in the environment. He has developed several novel analytical methods for the determination of pharmaceuticals in various matrices including sewage sludge.
