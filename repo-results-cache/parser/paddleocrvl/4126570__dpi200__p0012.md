# Gulash (Meat stew)

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_137_551_308_654.jpg" alt="Image" width="10%" /></div>


International

total time:

1 h 10 min

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_823_1_1654_715.jpg" alt="Image" width="50%" /></div>


## Ingredients for 90 lt/24 gal pan

Meat

▶ Oil

Onion (chopped)

Sweet red pepper

Wine

▶ Peeled tomatoes

Broth

Salt

45 kg / 66 lbs

750 g / 1,7 lbs

4.5 kg / 10 lbs

3 kg / 6.6 lbs

3 It / 0,8 gal

9 kg / 20 lbs

4,5 lt / 1,2 gal

## Procedure:

Preheat at  $ 220^{\circ} $C /  $ 428^{\circ} $F in braising mode

▶ Add the meat and sear

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_655_948_695_989.jpg" alt="Image" width="2%" /></div>


TIP: to obtain a good searing result we recommend to not exceed the quantity indicated below

Mix everything and add oil

▶ Add wine after 3 minutes

▶ Add vegetables, salt, peppers, mix of paprika and then tomatoes  

▶ Close the lid and cook under pressure for 30/ 40 minutes (depending on size of meat)



<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_1139_917_1181_959.jpg" alt="Image" width="2%" /></div>


TIP: to ensure the best performance wait that sauce boils before starting the pressure phase

If needed, cook with open lid for 10 more min if the sauce is too liquid
