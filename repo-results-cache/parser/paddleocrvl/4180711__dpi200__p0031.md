## BOURGOGNE - CÔTE DE NUITS

Stretching from Dijon to just south of Nuits-St.Georges the Côtes de Nuits is Burgundys premier area for red wine production, like most of Burgundy its soils are predominantly limestone and marl with clay and sand, near the 47 $ ^{th} $ parallel its one of the northern most regions to produce premium red wine.
