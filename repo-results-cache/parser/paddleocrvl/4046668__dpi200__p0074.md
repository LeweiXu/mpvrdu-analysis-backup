The immediate effect of this return to a more efficient forest policy is to reduce the earnings of the agricultural sector to the extent that the expansion in log exports reduces resources available for agriculture and causes the kina to appreciate. In the longer run, however, it may expand the availability of resources for agriculture in so far as land cleared by loggers is subsequently used for agricultural purposes. This may turn out to be an additional positive effect of the government's forest policy reversal.
