#### 2.3.1 ‘Corkscrew’ or spiral trauma cases

Fifty-seven seals were reported as having trauma consistent with spiral or corkscrew injuries. These cases were reported from nine different regions of Scotland. The majority of these were Hg seals  $ (n=43) $:  $ P_{v}(n=12) $ and those too decomposed or data deficient to be identified  $ (n=2) $. It is now considered highly likely that the majority of historic “spiral or corkscrew” cases were due to predation, most likely by adult grey seals. More detail can be found in Section 6.
