## The Beatles

• 1960 went to Hamburg, Germany to play in strip clubs 8 hours a night, 7 days a week.

• The Hamburg trips yielded 270 nights of performing at 8 hours/night in only 1.5 years.



• By 1964 when the Beatles came to the US, they had played at least 1,200 gigs ( $ \approx $8,400 hours).
