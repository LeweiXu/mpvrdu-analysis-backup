## TROUBLESHOOTING TIPS

## Before Calling For Service

Reviewing the charts on the following pages may save you from calling for service.


<table border=1 style='margin: auto; word-wrap: break-word;'><tr><td style='text-align: center; word-wrap: break-word;'>Problem</td><td style='text-align: center; word-wrap: break-word;'>Possible Causes</td><td style='text-align: center; word-wrap: break-word;'>What To Do</td></tr><tr><td rowspan="4">Dishwasher doesn&#x27;t start</td><td style='text-align: center; word-wrap: break-word;'>Fuse blown, or the circuit break tripped.</td><td style='text-align: center; word-wrap: break-word;'>Replace fuse or reset circuit breaker. Remove any other appliances sharing the same circuit with the dishwasher.</td></tr><tr><td style='text-align: center; word-wrap: break-word;'>Power supply is not turned on.</td><td style='text-align: center; word-wrap: break-word;'>Make sure the dishwasher is turned on and the door is closed securely. Make sure the power cord is properly plugged into the wall socket.</td></tr><tr><td style='text-align: center; word-wrap: break-word;'>Water pressure is low</td><td style='text-align: center; word-wrap: break-word;'>Check that the water supply is connected properly and the water is turned on.</td></tr><tr><td style='text-align: center; word-wrap: break-word;'>Door of dishwasher not properly closed.</td><td style='text-align: center; word-wrap: break-word;'>Make sure to close the door properly and latch it.</td></tr><tr><td rowspan="3">Water not pumped form dishwasher</td><td style='text-align: center; word-wrap: break-word;'>Twisted or trapped drain hose.</td><td style='text-align: center; word-wrap: break-word;'>Check the drain hose.</td></tr><tr><td style='text-align: center; word-wrap: break-word;'>Filter clogged.</td><td style='text-align: center; word-wrap: break-word;'>Check coarse the filter.</td></tr><tr><td style='text-align: center; word-wrap: break-word;'>Kitchen sink clogged.</td><td style='text-align: center; word-wrap: break-word;'>Check the kitchen sink to make sure it is draining well. If the problem is the kitchen sink that is not draining, you may need a plumber rather than a serviceman for dishwashers.</td></tr><tr><td style='text-align: center; word-wrap: break-word;'>Suds in the tub</td><td style='text-align: center; word-wrap: break-word;'>Wrong detergent.</td><td style='text-align: center; word-wrap: break-word;'>Use only the special dishwasher detergent to avoid suds. If this occurs, open the dishwasher and let suds evaporate. Add 1 gallon of cold water to the bottom of the dishwasher. Close the dishwasher door, then select any cycle. Initially, the dishwasher will drain out the water. Open the door after draining stage is complete and check if the suds have disappeared. Repeat if necessary.</td></tr></table>
