### 3.2 Illumination

Its purpose is to illuminate the interior of the machine for improved exposure of the products.

It consists of a 30 W fluorescent tube, a 30 W reactance and a primer.

Illumination is controlled by software from the Control Card. The I/O Card governs the relay (RL2) found on the Power Card.
