## RELATION BETWEEN CONCRETE SETTING TIME AND SLIPFORM RATE:

•It is calculated by using the following equation:

##  $ V_{s} = (h_{1} - h_{1}) / (t_{s} - t_{f}) $ Where

Vs – slipform rate (mm / h)

H1 = the distance from the top of the slipform panel to the average curing front (mm)

H2 = The distance from the top of the slipform panel to average freeboard (mm)

Ts = setting time (h)

Tt = Time from mixing concrete to placing
