## 8 Nano-SIM card slot

The nano-SIM-card slot is in the battery compartment. If the computer supports the wireless wide area network (WWAN), a nano Subscriber Identification Module (SIM) card might be required to establish connections. For information about replacing or installing the nano-SIM card, see “Replacing the nano-SIM card” on page 102.
