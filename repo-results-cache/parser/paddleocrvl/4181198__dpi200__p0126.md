Unmet Annual Performance Measurement Targets and Supplantation Programs Receiving MIDD Funding Prior to 2016

Of the 37 original MIDD strategies, 19 (51%) had annual performance measurement targets that were unmet at least one time between 2008 and 2015. Targets were considered unmet if less than 85 percent of the established goal was achieved after adjustment. Adjustments were typically made when fewer programs or staff positions were funded than planned, when start-up allowances were made, and when programs were unable to fill staff vacancies.
