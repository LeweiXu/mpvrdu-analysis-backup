## Chapter 8 Conclusion

After many months and countless hours of testing, the robotic arm known as 'Tasneef' is finally able to detect the microcontroller boards given and sort it in the given preset storage box based on the shortest distance. It all started with a couple servos, a stripped body frame, and a wooden plank. The team worked hard to achieve the best results, and thanks to the diversity in the team, everyone had an input that shaped the robotic arm to what it currently is today.

The tests that were done during the evolution of this robot helped us understand ideas and it embedded the concepts in our minds.

But the story is not all sunshine and butterflies. In fact, we were faced with many challenges. The servos used for the yaw, pitch and gripper proved inefficient because of their quality. In fact, these are cheap servos designed not to last very long, but their sizes suited our design, so we had to deal with this massive drawback.

In the future we would opt to use stepper motors to animate our robotic arm since modern day stepper motors are very robust and have high reliability with very few failures.

Getting basic components for our robotic arm proved to be a challenge because of a limited amount of supplies in the country. The servos for our base shoulder and elbow were not easily found and are now out of stock. It took a very long time to find someone with a laser cutter the size of our plexiglass, but we prospered thanks to many hours of research

When it came to image detection, we faced problems with the data needed to train the model. The images need to be extremely diverse to provide different scenarios. But with 1200 images we still had an overfitted model. This is due to the limited time of training, making the monitoring of the loss a challenge. Even though we used Google Cloud to train our model we needed more memory and a faster GPU, but renting servers is very costly and can run up to $3/hour for a multi-GPU.

Coming to software, we encountered a problem with the threads because we had limited computational power, meaning we could not try anything related with multiprocessing. That is why, the thread design was a very important part of the software structure as it is the backbone of the main processes, and therefore this design must be powered in a correct fashion.

In terms of the future, this robotic arm can transform into something very sophisticated. By changing the base configuration and hardware of the robotic arm, it is certain that this model will improve tremendously. And with a bit more time it would be possible to gather enough data for the model to not be overfitted.

Three years in the mechatronics field amounted to this project, and even though this model has some flaws, it is one where the team stands by with. The team in question might’ve lost their minds in the process and they definitely drank about 100 liters of Nescafe, but in the end they got a fully-fledged product. Who really knows what the future has in hold, since every member in the team is thinking of going into a different specialty. But one thing is for certain, if they ever worked on a project again, it would be one for the books.
