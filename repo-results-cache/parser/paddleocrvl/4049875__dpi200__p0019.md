# What is the Fund’s Investment Strategy?

## Investment Objective $ ^{3} $

The Fund’s investment objective is to achieve long-term capital appreciation through returns that seek to be uncorrelated with traditional asset classes. The Fund aims to give effect to this objective by investing in accordance with the Program. The net assets not invested in the quantitative trading strategy will be held as cash, Cash Equivalents or interest bearing instruments.

## Investment Strategy

The Fund is a quantitative trading fund that invests according to the Program, which is designed by the Investment Advisor.

The Investment Manager specialises in systematic trading strategies which trade financial instruments based on statistical analysis of price and fundamental data. The Program has been implemented as a computer program that generates trading signals for each instrument included in the Program’s trading universe.

The Program trades the financial markets on a global basis focusing on markets providing sufficient liquidity and supporting infrastructure. Trading may be extended to new markets when liquidity and market infrastructure allows. The strategy may be based on momentum or mean reversion with a focus on longer-term patterns.

The Program implements the long-term trend following strategy by taking positions in a set of futures contracts across a variety of underlyings including medium and long-term bonds, short-term interest rates, stock indices, commodities and foreign exchange. The Program may also take positions in derivatives on credit, volatility and foreign exchanges. Trade execution within the Program is generally electronic in all asset classes mentioned above and is based upon execution models that seek to optimise market entry and exit costs. The strategy is based on price and econometric data and is statistical and systematic in nature. A range of technical and/or market specific systems that seek to profit from long-term trading patterns are employed within the strategy.

## Risk Management

The computer-based proprietary technology implementing the Program includes an integrated risk management system which monitors the risk of the Fund’s Portfolio on a continuous basis. The leverage of the individual trading strategies is adjusted on the basis of volatility forecasts in order to target a stable portfolio risk over the medium-term.

In addition, the Investment Manager employs an independent risk management team which monitors the market risk of the Fund using independently developed market risk models. The independent risk team also monitors operational risks in relation to the trading activity of the Program. The Investment Manager maintains a number of risk targets and limits for the Program based mainly on volatility, value at risk ("VaR") and maximum exposures.

The target volatility of each Class of Units is set by the investment committee of the Investment Advisor. Under normal market conditions, the target volatility of Class A Units will be set at between 5% and 15% per annum, with the expectation to hold the target volatility at close to 10% on average. Similarly, the target volatility for Class B Units will under normal market conditions be set between 7.5% and 22.5%, with the expectation to hold the target volatility at close to 15% on average. Notwithstanding the foregoing, the Investment Advisor reserves its full discretion to cut the target volatility of a Class, even to nil, when it perceives that such a risk reduction may preserve capital.

PLEASE NOTE THAT THE REALIZED VOLATILITY OF A CLASS MAY DIFFER MATERIALLY FROM ITS TARGET VOLATILITY. THE INVESTMENT MANAGER MAKES NO REPRESENTATION THAT THE FUND OR A CLASS OF UNITS WILL ACHIEVE ITS TARGET VOLATILITY.
