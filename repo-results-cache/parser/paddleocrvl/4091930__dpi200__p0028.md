## George Hart Award

The George K. Hart Award, in memory of a former National Treasurer, was started in 1975 to honor the Section which showed the greatest percentage of membership increase within the current fiscal year. The first “George Hart Award” was presented to the Pittsburgh Section in 1976.

The 2021 George K. Hart Award is presented to the Tampa Bay Section. The Tampa Bay Section had a membership increase of 45.3% from April 2020 to April 2021.

### George K. Hart Biography

Became a National Director in 1965 and assumed the duties as National Treasurer in 1967. Mr. Hart passed away on October 2, 1975. Before becoming active in the National Society, Hart was very active in the Williamsport Section from its inception.
