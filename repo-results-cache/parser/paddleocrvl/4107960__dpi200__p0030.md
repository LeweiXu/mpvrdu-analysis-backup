### 3.6 SAIC GeoMechanics Laboratory

Sample homogenization was completed at the SAIC GeoMechanics Laboratory in Las Vegas, NV. This facility is an industrial-type building with separate facilities for personnel offices and material handling. The primary function of the laboratory is for rock mechanics studies. The laboratory has rock mechanics equipment, including sieves, rock crushers, and sample splitters. The personnel associated with this laboratory are experienced in the areas of sample preparation and sample homogenization. In addition to the sample homogenization equipment, the laboratory contains several benches, tables, and open space. Mercury air monitoring equipment was used during the sample preparation activities for personnel safety.
