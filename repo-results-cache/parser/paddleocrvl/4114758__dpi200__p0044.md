# Successful Year.

This year our swimmers were a tough match for any team. They won the majority of the meets and placed second in the Iron Range conference, losing the golden duck to Ely by only one point. In the state meet Donn Schlotec placed third in the finals in the 200 free style. Dick W. Maki got sixth doing the butterfly and George Harvey got seventh in the backstroke.

The co-captains elected for the next year were George Harvey and Mike Hahne.
