## Ingredients for 90 lt/24 gal pan

Pears

Red wine

▶ Sugar

▶ Cinnamon

Cloves

Orange peel

16 kg / 35 lbs

10 l/ 2,6 gal

4 kg / 8,8 lbs

20 g / 0,7 oz

6 g / 0,2 oz

15 g / 0,53 oz

## Procedure:

Preheat at  $ 150^{\circ} $C /  $ 302^{\circ} $F in braising mode with wine

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_654_903_696_944.jpg" alt="Image" width="2%" /></div>


TIP: with small quantities of liquid preheat in braising mode to speed up the cooking process

Put sugar with spices and stir until boiling for 10 minutes or till boils



Boil until half of the liquid evaporates

▶ Add the pears, close the lid and cook under pressure 🍳 for 5/10 minutes (depending on how mature fruits are)

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_1138_969_1181_1013.jpg" alt="Image" width="2%" /></div>


TIP: to ensure the best performance wait that wine boils before starting the pressure phase

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_1138_1086_1181_1131.jpg" alt="Image" width="2%" /></div>


TIP: use the false bottom to avoid that fruits stick on the bottom
