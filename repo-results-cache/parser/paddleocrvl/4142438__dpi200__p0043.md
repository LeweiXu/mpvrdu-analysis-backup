In theory, the same issue also arises when Congress cancels a tax debt by means of a private bill. Congress occasionally uses private bills to reverse a court judgment or other final tax determination in order to provide ad hoc tax relief. $ ^{185} $ Congress has sometimes provided similar relief through a narrowly drawn retroactive provision of a revenue act which may or may not have general application. $ ^{186} $ Congress could easily insert a clause in private bills to prevent further tax, but it has never done so, undoubtedly because Congress did not expect tax relief to be taxable.
