# JOURNEY TO THE END OF ISLAM

## MICHAEL MUHAMMAD KNIGHT

Current Events / Islam • Trade Paperback • 9781593762469 $16.95 • 360 pages • 5.6" x 8.2" • Ebook available

In Journey to the End of Islam, Michael Muhammad Knight—whose work has led to his being hailed as both the Jack Kerouac and Hunter S. Thompson of American Islam—wanders through Muslim countries, navigating conflicting visions of his religion. Visiting holy sites in Pakistan, Syria, Egypt, and Ethiopia, Knight engages both the puritanical Islam promoted by Saudi globalization and the heretical strands of popular folk Islam. The conflict of “global” and “local” Islam speaks to Knight’s own experience approaching the Islamic world as a uniquely American Muslim. What results is a struggle to separate the spiritual from the political, Knight searching for a personal relationship to Islam in the context of how it’s defined by the external world.
