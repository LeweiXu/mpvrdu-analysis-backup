#### 13.5.2 Case study: Passive Observer for Heading Autopilots Using only Compass Measurements

Passivity-Based Pole Placement

The observer error dynamics can be reformulated as two subsystems for yaw angle/rudder bias, and yaw rate. Fossen and Strand (1999) have shown that these systems form a passive interconnection if the observer gains are chosen according to

 $$ \boldsymbol{k}=\left[\begin{array}{c}-2(1-\lambda)\frac{\omega_{c}}{\omega_{0}}\\ 2\omega_{0}(1-\lambda)\\ \omega_{c}\\ K_{4}\\ K_{5}\end{array}\right] $$ 

where  $ \omega_{c} > \omega_{0} $ is the filter cut-off frequency

 $$ \begin{aligned}\dot{\hat{\xi}}_{w}&=\hat{\psi}_{w}+K_{1}\operatorname{ssa}(y-\hat{\psi}-\hat{\psi}_{w})\\\dot{\hat{\psi}}_{w}&=-\omega_{0}^{2}\hat{\xi}_{w}-2\lambda\omega_{0}\hat{\psi}_{w}+K_{2}\operatorname{ssa}(y-\hat{\psi}-\hat{\psi}_{w})\\\dot{\hat{\psi}}&=\hat{r}+K_{3}\operatorname{ssa}(y-\hat{\psi}-\hat{\psi}_{w})\\\dot{\hat{r}}&=-\frac{1}{T}\hat{r}+\frac{1}{m}(\tau_{\operatorname{wind}}+\tau_{\operatorname{N}})+\hat{b}+K_{4}\operatorname{ssa}(y-\hat{\psi}-\hat{\psi}_{w})\\\dot{\hat{b}}&=-\frac{1}{T_{b}}\hat{b}+K_{5}\operatorname{ssa}(y-\hat{\psi}-\hat{\psi}_{w})\end{aligned} $$ 

 $$ 0<1/T_{b}<K_{5}/K_{4}<\omega_{0}<\omega_{c} $$
