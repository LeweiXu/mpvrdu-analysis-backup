## Disciplined Agile Delivery (DAD) is a process decision framework

The key characteristics of DAD:

– People-first

- Goal-driven

– Hybrid agile

– Learning-oriented

– Full delivery lifecycle

– Solution focused

– Risk-value lifecycle

– Enterprise aware
