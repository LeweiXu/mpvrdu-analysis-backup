## Option 1_ At Ship Cove

## Site/ Park Integration

(+) The building location engages a unique part of the Park with a direct access to the Park's natural, scenic and historic resources.

(+) Capitalizes the long views to the Ocean.

(+/-) The building is highly visible from Powers road.

(+) Potential to nestle the building into the hillside to minimize the visual impact- Potential of having one floor visible on the high side of the slope and two floors visible on low side of the slope.

(-) The building is close to the Park's South property line which may present noise issues with the abutting neighbors.

## Function / Program

(+) The building is able to support the wedding venue at Goddard Mansion.

(+) The building can support and range of education opportunities and programs that relate to the park; historic, environmental.

(-) Utilities currently do not exist in this location, but the connection to Town sewer is close.

(+) The building is close to parking at Ship Cove.

(+) There is the potential to have a separate entrance with the use of Chapel Hill Road. - Useful for nighttime activities.

(-) The bathroom facility is weighted to the northern section of the park.

## Option 2_ At Terminus of Parade Ground

## Site/ Park Integration

(+) The Building is centrally located within the Park.

(+) The building capitalizes on significant and wide panoramic views in this location.

(+) The building and the outdoor spaces establish a strong terminus for the Parade Ground.

(+) The outdoor spaces is able to flow out onto the open lawn areas at the Parade Ground.

## Function / Program

(+) The building and outdoor spaces has the ability to serve community events that actively

use the Parade Ground such as the Family Fun Day.

(+) The building’s outdoor spaces can support Vendors.

(+) The building location is highly visible.

(+) The building location compliments the Picnic Shelter and Kitty's Point.

(+) The centralize location offers a bathroom facility to all visitors.
