#### 3.4.10 Motor Position Detection

In order for the Control Card to detect when the motor makes a  $ 360^{\circ} $ turn, it incorporates a run-end microswitch activated twice through a double cam when the turn is completed. Each run-end activation implies a  $ 180^{\circ} $ turn.

## Operation:

The negative contact of the motor is applied to one of the ends of the run-end microswitch so that when this switch is closed it directly polarises the transistor passing the collector from 0v to 5v. This change in voltage is transferred to the U12 and from there to the Control Card thus informing it that the motor has performed a  $ 180^{\circ} $ turn.
