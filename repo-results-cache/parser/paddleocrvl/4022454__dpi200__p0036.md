Gurugram loses ~5.5% of its GDP each year to the various costs of air pollution, higher than other metropolitan areas, e.g. Mumbai and Bengaluru, which suffer GDP losses of 1.9% and 1.3%, respectively.

The impact of presenteeism in Gurugram is ~7X of absenteeism, compared with a national picture where presenteeism is 4X costlier than absenteeism. This is due to the high concentration of skilled professionals whose jobs entail a higher cognitive burden and thus suffer the greater passive effects of air pollution.
