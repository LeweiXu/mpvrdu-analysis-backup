## Border Security Agencies

Another very important aspect of Nigeria's security architecture has to do with the border control agencies, such as the Immigration Service and the Nigeria Customs Service. These two Para Military agencies perform important functions in safe guarding Nigeria's land, sea and air borders. The importance of their function is now urgent given the prevailing security challenges of terrorism, smuggling of small arms and light weapons, human trafficking and cross border crimes.
