# MANAGING DIRECTOR'S REVIEW

During the financial year, the Company spent $1.688 million on exploration. Additional joint venturer contributions of $0.376 million saw total expenditure on the Company's tenements exceed $2 million. Expenditures were directed towards a range of exploratory activities with the bulk of funds utilised to conduct drilling programmes. We maintain our view that gold and copper should remain our commodity focus going forward.

While commodity prices in $US for all major mineral products are at or near multi-year lows, the depreciation of the Australian dollar has worked to balance the impact to some extent.

Gold is currently trading near A$1,600 per ounce, over A$200 higher than one year ago, and we believe the increased valuations of gold producers from 12 months ago evidences a renewed market confidence in this metal. Over the course of the financial year we have increased the proportion of our exploration budget directed to gold exploration, a situation likely to continue into 2015/16.

Copper, despite its ups and downs in the last 12 months, is currently selling for over A$7,300 per tonne which is only marginally lower than its price the same time a year ago. Recent announcements from some of the world's larger copper producers that they will cut production levels at some of their higher cost operations is also likely to result in supply side driven upward price pressure.

The Company’s four projects are each located in highly prospective geological terrains. The Drummond Basin tenements secure ground in a pedigreed region that has delivered multi-million ounce gold production. The Rover ground captures a geological repetition of the plus-five million ounce Tennant Creek district, an area that has already produced significant copper.

In South Australia the Moonta tenement falls in the Olympic Copper Gold Province, a world class belt that contains over 75% of the nation's current copper resources. The Eyre Peninsula project covers a large area in the Central Gawler Gold Province, a poorly exposed belt of rocks which is starting to reveal its hidden wealth.

Of greater significance is the presence on the Moonta, Rover and Eyre Peninsula ground of mineral deposits that confirm the prospectivity of our tenements, and which given the economic conditions, show potential to deliver value to shareholders. We aim to do what is necessary to position these projects to deliver that value, be it through further self-funded efforts or through the involvement of third parties.

While it is too early to claim a mineral discovery on the Drummond tenements, we are extremely encouraged by the results of our exploration to date and believe this project is emerging as a significant epithermal gold district deserving a persistent future search effort.



There is little doubt that the broader market for resources focused companies remains challenging, however Adelaide Resources’ commitment to putting shareholders funds to work on real exploration, the location and mineral endowment of its projects, and the efforts of its small team of explorers serves to elevate it above its many peer group companies.

## Strategy

To achieve the goal of growing shareholder wealth, Adelaide Resources’ directors have formulated a strategy comprising the following key elements:

The Company will maintain a strong commodity focus on gold and copper. Directors believe the outlook for these metals remains positive, and believe these are commodities well suited to our expertise and capabilities. The two metals occur naturally together in several of the Company's key projects, and the capital cost of developing a gold or copper mine of a size that would transform the Company is within its financing capacity.

The Company’s Board believes it is in shareholders’ best interests to retain maximum equity in its tier one projects and so seeks to self-fund exploration programs on these projects when equity markets allow. The Board will contemplate third party investment in a tier one project if it believes the consequent loss of shareholder equity is more than compensated by increased financial certainty and the project de-risking that may accrue.

The Company will pursue opportunities which have potential to unlock value in its other mineral assets, and will maintain an active lookout for quality new gold and copper projects.

The Company will adhere to principles of good corporate governance, caring for its employees, conducting its operations in an environmentally sensitive manner, and maintaining respect for other stakeholders and for the communities in which it operates.
