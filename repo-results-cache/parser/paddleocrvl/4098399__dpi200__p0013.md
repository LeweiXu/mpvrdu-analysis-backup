## Water Use

Salt Lake County Regional Goal

The new regional goal for Salt Lake County is 187 GPCD by 2030. We intend to strive for that as our goal over the course of the next 10 years. South Jordan City would like to reduce its water usage by a minimum of 12% over the course of the next 5 years. Below are charts and graphs that show current water usage and GPCD in South Jordan City including both culinary and secondary water usage. For a detailed look at the new regional goals use the following link: https://water.utah.gov/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/Regional-Water-Conservation-Goals-Report-Final.pdf
