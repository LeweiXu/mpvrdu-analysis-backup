## Overall implications for monetary policy

## ■ Increased risk of policy insolvency

» Stronger incentives to choose a too-accommodative monetary policy stance

## Central bank independence

» May weaken the independence of the ECB by making the Eurosystem more dependent on the fiscal soundness of member states

## ☑ Signalling

» Financial risks may be perceived by market participants as a signal that interest rates will remain low for an even longer period than they would otherwise be

## ■ Price stability

» In extreme scenarios, the financial health of the Eurosystem could be significantly harmed, reducing its capacity to ensure price stability

## ■ Market distortions

» Higher volatilities in bond markets (due to less market depth), associated with the risk of asset price bubbles

## ■ Future room for manoeuvre

» Future attempts to expand its asset purchase programmes may force the ECB to loosen current limits and eligibility criteria
