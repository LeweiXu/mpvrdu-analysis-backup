# ANNEXE III: QUADRIGA INVESTORS – GFED AEQUITAS

# Characteristics of the Sub-Fund

## Investment Objective and Policy

The objective of Quadriga Investors – GFED Aequitas (the “Sub-Fund” in this section) is to provide shareholders with medium to long term attractive total returns, investing across multiple markets and asset classes that provide a high degree of diversification, while assuming some market risk.

To achieve its objective, the Sub-Fund may use such strategies as it may deem appropriate, such as, but not restricted to directional relative value, arbitrage, volatility, etc.

The Sub-Fund will not seek to abide to a given asset allocation, but the Sub-Fund may shift its investments from one eligible asset class to another based on the Sub-Fund's analysis of the best opportunities for the Sub-Fund's portfolio in a given market. To achieve this objective, the Sub-Fund takes advantages of opportunities in combining different approaches (fundamental, judgmental, technical etc.) and different investment horizons (from long-term strategic positions to short-term trading). Short exposure will be achieved through the use of derivative instruments. The Sub-Fund's long positions will be sufficiently liquid to cover at all times the Sub-Fund's obligations arising from its short positions.

The use of these strategies will bring the Sub-Fund to hold positions in diversified and low correlated asset classes including but not limited to global bonds, emerging bonds and currencies. Consequently, subject to all applicable investment limitations, the Sub-Fund may invest in:

- fixed income securities including, with a limit of 20% of the asset of the Sub-Fund, mortgage-backed and other asset-backed securities.

Fixed income securities may have fixed, variable, or floating rates of interest, and may vary inversely with respect to a reference rate;

- money market instruments issue in any currency by any issuer, without rating limit;

- equity securities, in including units of close-ended funds, (e.g., euro, non-euro, emerging market, large-capitalization and small-capitalization, etc);

- financial derivative instruments which seeks exposure to commodities through eligible transferable securities and/or financial indices, as well as investment vehicles (such as exchange traded funds), qualifying as transferable securities under article 41(1)(a) or complying with article 41(1)(e) of the Law of 17 December 2010, which exclusively invest in commodities;
