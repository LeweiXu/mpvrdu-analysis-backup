## Procedure:

▶ Preheat at 110°C / 230°F in boiling mode 🌆 for 5 minutes

Put the tofu in GNI/1 trays. Use the false bottom for the trays placed on the bottom and suspended frame for the trays placed on top

Add 5 lt / 1,3 gal of water on the bottom, close the lid and cook under pressure for 1 minute

TIP: to ensure the best performance wait that water boils before starting the pressure phase

Remove the tofu



▶ Rinse and dry the well

To prepare the sauce preheat at  $ 180^{\circ} $C /  $ 356^{\circ} $F in braising mode and stir all the rest of the ingredients for 4 min

Take the sauce and divide it on GNI/1 trays and add tofu

▶ Cook under pressure 📊 for 4 minutes

▶ Portion and serve when finished
