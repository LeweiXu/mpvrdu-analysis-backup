## Activity Description:

This outcome value estimates that upon completion of the project Millington can avoid an anticipated $60,000,000 in flood damage costs and reduce its flood pump operation hours by 20% over the 2010 and 2011 damage. The baseline is a total of $233 million in damages and 20 hours of flood pump operation.
