## Product Revenue

Product revenue decreased by $1.5 million for the three months ended April 30, 2021 as compared to the three months ended April 30, 2020. The decrease for the three months ended April 30, 2021 was primarily due to the shift to our Framework sales model, which did not include any third-party hardware, and a decrease in legacy product sales in which we would procure and sell third-party hardware to our customers.

## Service Revenue

Service revenue decreased by $0.4 million for the three months ended April 30, 2021 as compared to the three months ended April 30, 2020. The decrease for the three months ended April 30, 2020 was primarily due to a decrease in our maintenance and support revenue provided on post-warranty contracts as customers continue to provide their own solutions and legacy products are decommissioned.

## Gross Profit and Margin

Cost of revenue consists primarily of the cost of resold third-party products and services, purchased components and subassemblies, labor and overhead relating to the assembly, testing and implementation and ongoing maintenance of complete systems.
