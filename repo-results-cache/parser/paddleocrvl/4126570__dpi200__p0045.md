## Procedure:

4 kg / 8,82 lbs

▶ Preheat at  $ 182^{\circ} $C /  $ 360^{\circ} $F in braising mode



Once the temperature is reached, add in oil and begin to brown beef

▶ After 3 minutes add in all of the remaining ingredients and bring to boil in boiling mode 🍳

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_656_1084_698_1129.jpg" alt="Image" width="2%" /></div>


TIP: with big quantities of water preheat in boiling mode to speed up the cooking process

Once the liquid has come to a boil, lock down the lid and cook under pressure for 45 minutes

Open the lid, portion and serve

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_1142_815_1182_858.jpg" alt="Image" width="2%" /></div>


TIP: to reduce the quantity of excess steam released, wait till steam condensation process completes before unlocking and opening the lid fully
