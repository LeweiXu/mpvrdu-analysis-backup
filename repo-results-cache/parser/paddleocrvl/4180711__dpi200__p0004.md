# CORE WINE PAIRING

Working closely with the kitchen team, our sommeliers have carefully selected the following wines to be paired.

## CORE SEASONS

2019 Riesling Trocken, Weingut Georg Mosbacher, Pfalz Germany

Weingut Georg Mosbacher is a family-owned estate with nearly 45 acres of vineyards in the picturesque wine village of Forst situated on the German Wine Route.

2019 Grenache Blanc, Donovan Rall, Swartland South Africa

One of the original “garagista” producers in the Swartland, Donovan can now proudly say that he makes some of the most highly sought-after wines in the country.

2018 Bouzeron Aligoté, Domaine A & P de Villaine, Burgundy France

The 26ha of Domaine de Villaine are centred in Bouzeron an area first planted to the vine by the monks of Cluny in the middle ages, situated just south of the Cote d'Or and north of Rully. Here the poor soils and limestone-rich slopes are reserved for Bouzeron's speciality, Aligote

2013 Monthélie Rouge, Domaine Fanny Sabre, Burgundy France

Fanny Sabre makes wines in a very natural manner, which is not surprising when you consider that she learned her trade from Philippe Pacalet.

2009 Château Langoa-Barton St Julien 3ème Grand Cru Classé, Bordeaux  

Hugh Barton was a descendant of an Irish family which settled in Bordeaux in the 18th century and which has a long and distinguished history in the region's wine trade.

2013 Tokaji Szamorodni Öreg Kiraly Dulo, Barta Pince, Hungary

Öreg Király is not an actual vineyard name, but the name Barta use for their section of the actual Király vineyard. The 10-hectare Barta section is the very highest, steepest part of Király.
