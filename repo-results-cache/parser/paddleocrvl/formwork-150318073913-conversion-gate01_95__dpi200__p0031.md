## HORIZONTAL FORMWORK :

•Slipform methods of construction can also be adapted to horizontal structures and are used for paving, canals, and tunneling.

• The technique is more in use for structures that have continuous walls like silos, chimneys, and piers for very tall bridges.

• It has also been successfully used for construction of buildings, although this requires the manner of leaving inserts for openings like doors and windows to be decided well in advance, as well as also any necessary inserts to support floor slabs after the walls are constructed.
