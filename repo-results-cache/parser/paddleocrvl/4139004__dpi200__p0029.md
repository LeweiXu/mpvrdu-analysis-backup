In the next example, a more complex coating whose boundary is given by parametric equation

 $$ X_{\Gamma}=\left(\begin{array}{l}\left(A+B\sin\left(6t\right)+C\cos(5t)+D\cos\left(6t\right)\right)\cos(t)\\ \left(A+B\sin\left(6t\right)+C\cos(5t)+D\cos\left(6t\right)\right)\sin(t)\end{array}\right),t\in[0,2\pi) $$ 

where  $ A = \lambda $,  $ B = 0.06\lambda $,  $ C = 0.07\lambda $ and  $ D = 0.05\lambda $ and the width is given by equation

 $$ \delta\left(t\right)=\delta_{0}\left(1-0.4\sin\left(7t\right)\right) $$ 

is taken into account(see Figure 12). Thus, the parametric equation of the PEC can be shown as

 $$ x_{\Gamma^{\delta}}(t)=x_{\Gamma}(t)+\delta\left(t\right)\vec{n}\left(t\right),t\in\left[0,2\pi\right) $$ 

The coating with relative dielectric permittivity of  $ 3.0\epsilon_{0} $ and conductivity of  $ 0.05(S.m^{-1}) $ is illuminated at 3 MHz with incidence angle  $ \pi/3 $. The discretization size is roughly  $ \lambda/55 $, and the radius of the ABC is  $ 2.2\lambda $. The errors of total field  $ E_{s} $'s are calculated on the circle  $ \Gamma_{C} $ with radius  $ 2\lambda $ and for order 1, 2, and 3, the equation of the lines are compared in Figure 13. The slope of lines for order 1, 2 and 3 are approximately  $ m_{1}=5.26 $,  $ m_{2}=5.98 $ and  $ m_{3}=9.23 $, respectively.
