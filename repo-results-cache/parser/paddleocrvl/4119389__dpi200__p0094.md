## Fertility

Pelvic radiotherapy is likely to affect your fertility. You may not be able to father children after treatment. This is called infertility.

Sperm is produced in the testicles. These are close to the bladder, prostate, and rectum. The testicles are very sensitive to radiotherapy.

Some men have sperm stored before starting radiotherapy. After treatment has finished, they can have fertility treatments to try to have a baby with a partner.

Infertility can be very distressing and difficult to live with. Some people find it helpful to talk things over with a partner, family member, or friend. Others might prefer to talk to a trained counsellor. Your GP or cancer specialist can arrange this for you. Many hospitals also have specialist nurses who can offer support. And fertility clinics usually have a counsellor you can talk to.
