## Equipment for trenchless pipe-laying (UG100T)

Series UG...T

Puncture length — up to 50 m

Pressure — 700 Bar

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_222_239_863_882.jpg" alt="Image" width="38%" /></div>


UG100T - designed for trenchless laying of different types of underground utilities (water, sewer, electric cable, etc.) by a puncture in the soil of the 1-3 categories under roads, tram tracks, railway embankments, marshy soils, forests, etc. with subsequent cable or polyethylene pipe or metal pipe pulling.

The set includes the end bit. Additionally the system includes extenders with hooks and rods. On customer request their characteristics (depending on the diameter of the pushed pipe and soil properties) and amount may vary.

## Phases and sequence of the pipe-laying:

## PUNCTURE

Inserting the rod into the frame bracket, screwing the end bit (Fig. 1).

Pushing the rod (Fig. 2) all the way into soil with the hydraulic cylinder rod, returning to the starting position.

Screwing the subsequent rod in the previous one.
