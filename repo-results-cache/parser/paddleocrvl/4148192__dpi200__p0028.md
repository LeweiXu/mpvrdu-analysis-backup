The wing  $ C_{Do} $ is primarily due to skin friction and can be estimated by the following equation (8:11-13):

 $$ c_{\tt D o_{w i n g}}=c_{\tt f}\left[1+L\left(\frac{t}{c}\right)+100\left(\frac{t}{c}\right)^{4}\right]\mathrm{~R~}\frac{2\mathrm{~s}_{e}}{\mathrm{~s~}} $$ 

where

 $ C_{f} $ is the turbulent flat plate skin friction coefficient

L is the airfoil maximum thickness parameter

 $ \left(\frac{t}{c}\right) $ is the airfoil maximum thickness to chord ratio

R is the lifting surface correlation factor

 $ S_{e} $ is the wing exposed surface area

S is the wing planform area
