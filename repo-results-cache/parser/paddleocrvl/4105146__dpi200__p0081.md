## Task 3 | Prepare Urban Water Management Plan

Black Water will update the 2015 UWMP for compliance with the 2020 UWMP Guidebook. Information from Task 1 will be used to develop water supply and demand forecasts, water loss audits, Water Shortage Contingency Plan, and associated forms and tables. Black Water will describe the methodology for Water Supply and Demand Assessments; compliance reporting for SBx7-7 targets; conservation reporting (per AB 1668/SB 606); drought risk assessment; reporting on water loss standards (per SB 555); address water system seismic risk mitigation and vulnerability; compliance with SGMA, GSA, and GSP; calculation of energy intensity of water; climate change impact review; and water savings estimates.

- Water Shortage Contingency Plan. Water supplies will be reviewed to address drought conditions over time, as well as short-term emergency outages possibly due to power failures, mechanical failure, groundwater contamination, etc., by providing a Catastrophic Supply Interruption Plan. Alternatives to address these scenarios will be provided, including mandatory reductions in water use.

- Reliability of Water Supply. Black Water will define potential constraints on the City’s water supplies addressed by water year type. The narrative will discuss potential and known constraints and effect on management strategies and supply reliability.

- UWMP Checklist. The UWMP checklist has been provided by DWR to ensure that agencies submitting UWMPs have responded to all of the issues that DWR would like addressed in the reports. This list will be completed and included in the appendix to demonstrate that all requirements have been addressed.

Based on initial review and discussions with Department of Water Resources (DWR) staff and City staff, Black Water will prepare the UWMP in a format meeting the current DWR guidelines for the presentation to the public, and submission to DWR.

## Deliverable(s): 2020 UWMP (PDF format)

Black Water will attend and participate in presentations at publicly scheduled meetings for the 2020 UWMP.
