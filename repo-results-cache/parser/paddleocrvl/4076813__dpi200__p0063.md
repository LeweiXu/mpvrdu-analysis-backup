## CIVIL PLANS

## GENERAL INFORMATION

1) Civil Plans (also known as “Construction Plans”) are submitted following approval of a Preliminary Plat and prior to submitting a Final Plat (which includes replays, amending plats, and minor plats).

2) See the Section 107.05, Construction Plans, for detailed information on requirements and processes.

3) See Section 2.0 of the Engineering Design Manual for detailed information regarding streets and sidewalks.

4) See Section 5.0 of the Engineering Design Manual for detailed information regarding street lighting, gas lines, electrical service, and telephone service.

5) See Section 6.0 of the Engineering Design Manual for detailed information regarding environmental issues.

6) See Section 7.0 of the Engineering Design Manual for detailed information regarding GIS, monuments and markers.

7) See Section 8.0 of the Engineering Design Manual for all standard details.
