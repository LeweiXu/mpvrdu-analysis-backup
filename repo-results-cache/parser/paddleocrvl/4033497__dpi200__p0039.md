## Survey Response (from Questionnaire)

Through the Analysis work with user’s groups and survey the Consultants understand that the CE community has a desire to have the park more focused on the community. Any inclusion of a Facility within the park should be community focused and include bathroom facilities. Below are the results from the Survey.

Visitor Center_ 70% No / 30% Yes

Permanent Bathroom Facility_ 70% Yes / 30% No
