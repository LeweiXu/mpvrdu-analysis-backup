## NORDIC FOOD IN RAW NATURE

Inspired by Norwegian nature, tradition and the changing weather of the west coast we present a new menu for each season of the year with the kitchens own local twists.

Skyskraperen is located at the top of Mount Ulriken with a spectacular view overlooking Bergen and its surrounding mountains, fjords and ever-changing weather.

Welcome to our seasonal 3-course lunch or a full 5-course tasting menu in the evening. Book your table for a unique experience to dine on the highest of the seven mountains surrounding the city of Bergen.
