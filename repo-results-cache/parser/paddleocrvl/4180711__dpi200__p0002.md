# INTRODUCTION

The wine list at Core by Clare Smyth embraces everything Clare and the team love about wine from old, fine and rare to young, new and exciting. Our cellar is home to wines that are already firm favourites, vintages to return to time and time again as well as a host of fresh discoveries waiting to be tried; an authentic reflection on the wine world today.

Our team are entirely dedicated to exceptional wine and hope to offer a list that excites every palette. We are proud of the depth that has been meticulously built into the collection, matching Core's food and service with our shared dedication to excellence.

We appreciate the uniqueness of every bottle and wish to commend the hard work of their production. We strive to do justice to all those involved from the growers, to the winemakers and the barrel crafters. Many dedicated hands were responsible in bringing these wines to our door, and to your table.

To see the vineyards, feel the soil, taste the grapes, and talk to the producers – this is something you can't find in wine books. We travel regularly to wine regions and as sommeliers, we are the connection between the winemaker and the consumer. Each opportunity for our guests to experience the provenance of individual wines fuels our enthusiasm and gives us the chance to share our passion for wine.

We love an inspiring quote, and sometimes a drawing can speak volumes where words can confuse. We have quite a comprehensive list, but we hope that you find it pretty easy to navigate and hard to put down.

We aim to provide the highest level of service but without the pretence. We want you to have fun, and we want to make it personal, tailored to you and your time with us.

It doesn’t matter if you don’t know your Cabernet from your Carignan. In fact, that’s even more exciting for us because we then get to act as your guide and hopefully share something new.
