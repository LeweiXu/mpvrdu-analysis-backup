According to their behaviors and values in communities, makers can be grouped into three different types [Torrey et al. [2009]]. These types help to understand what makers search for in tutorials and, in part, how they use them.

The first type is the "Guru Seeker". They seek out experts in a DIY domain to learn the right way to make things. They want to have in-depth knowledge on a topic and learn every minute detail there is.
