<div style="text-align: center;">Figure 3.5 ITR evaluation by restriction enzyme digestion and gel-electrophoresis for pSub_hSyn1_Butterfly</div>


The photo of the gel represents pSub_hSyn_Butterfly clone enzyme restriction in order to prove the presence of ITRs on each end of the construct.
