Glider pilots rarely have precise wind measurements when soaring, and most glider pilots do not have the McReady ring to compute their best penetration speed when they encounter a wind. Therefore, a glider pilot could easily misjudge his penetration airspeed by up to ±10 knots. Flying the wrong penetration airspeed will change glide range as shown in Figure 6.5.
