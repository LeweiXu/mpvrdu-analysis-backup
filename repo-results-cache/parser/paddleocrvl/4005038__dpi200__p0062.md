We used Threshold Indicator Taxa Analysis (TITAN2, Baker and King 2010) to explore whether the environmental changes significantly correlated with changing distribution and abundance of taxa. TITAN uses indicator species scores across binary partitions of a sample set to detect congruence in taxon-specific changes in abundance and occurrence frequency along the range of environmental variables. Ecological transition points (or zones of rapid change) are identified in the biological data in response to small, continuous increases in an environmental variable (Baker and King 2010). These are the points or values at which significant changes in a taxon's abundance are apparent. Similar to ordinations, this analysis is exploratory, but TITAN has significant advantages: it distinguishes the strength and direction of species response (increasing or decreasing below or above a change point), is nonparametric and is available in R, and the output is shown on a standard graph.
