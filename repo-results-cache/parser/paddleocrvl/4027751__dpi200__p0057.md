## Chapter 7 Hardware Implementation

In terms of hardware, the workspace table is a 1m x 70cm plexiglass. It contains openings to hold the robotic arm, the camera bridge, as well as the circuit board. The location of these openings is carefully drawn using AutoCAD and implemented on the table using a laser cutter.

The table is then covered by a black protective film. The cover is chosen to be of dark color to help with the object detection. The camera bridge is made of 3, 50 cm aluminum extrusions. The extrusions are connected using 90-degree slots. The height of the bridge is carefully studied to lift the camera to a suitable height, ensuring a full frame capture of the workspace and keeping a safe distance from the robotic arm.
