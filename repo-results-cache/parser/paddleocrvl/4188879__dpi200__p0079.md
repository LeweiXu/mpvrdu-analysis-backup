Observations were recorded on yield and yield related traits along with disease reaction to bacterial leaf blight. In field, selection was carried out based on DRR Dhan 48 designated traits (90-95cm height, semi-dwarf, compact, heavy and exerted panicle type, medium slender grain type, days to 50% flowering being 105-110 days and seed to seed duration being 135-140 days) and lines showing the same were chosen. Also, the PRTs were inoculated with bacterial blight pathogen at maximum tillering stage by leaf clipping method under field conditions.
