# Explore Nottingham

Nottingham’s a great city to be a student. There are over 70,000 students living here from all over the world. Over 39,000 of these are studying at NTU!

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_104_846_185_942.jpg" alt="Image" width="8%" /></div>


Students are attracted to Nottingham for a variety of reasons. The cost of living is very reasonable and it's a friendly, welcoming, and diverse student city.

Another great feature of Nottingham is that everything’s really close. Unlike in bigger cities like London, for example, you can easily walk from NTU’s City Campus to the shops and attractions in just a few minutes. And the regular buses and trams mean you can quickly reach other parts of Nottingham too.

Visit ntu.ac.uk/nottingham to find out more.
