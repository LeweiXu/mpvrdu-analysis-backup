# MINIATURES OF A ZEN MASTER

ROBERT AITKEN

WITH AN INTRODUCTION BY NELSON FOSTER

Buddhism / Zen • Trade Paperback • 9781582435367

$14.95 • 240 pages • 5" x 8"

For more than four decades, Robert Aitken Roshi taught the Buddhist practice of Zen meditation, as well as authored more than a dozen books, including one of the best-loved introductions to Zen Buddhism, Taking the Path of Zen. A founder of the Buddhist Peace Fellowship, he was a dedicated political activist.

To culminate a remarkable life, ninety-one-year-old Aitken Roshi offered a collection of 266 short texts in Miniatures of a Zen Master. Some are parables; others are quotations, memories, and commentaries. Resembling Benjamin Franklin's Almanac or the epigrams of Chamfort as much as it does work from ancient sages, these "miniatures" distill a life devoted to teaching and awareness, of being present, showing up, and making a difference.

# THE RIVER OF HEAVEN The Haiku of Bashō, Buson, Issa, and Shiki

ROBERT AITKEN

Poetry / Zen • Trade Paperback • 9781582437101 $15.95 • 208 pages • 5.5" x 7.3" • Ebook available

Known as the study of quiet stillness and introspection, Zen Buddhism distinguishes itself through brilliant flashes of insight and its terseness of expression. In The River of Heaven, these concepts are applied to Haiku, one of the most delicate and interpretive poetic forms.

In this volume, American Zen Roshi Robert Aitken gives new insight into Haiku by poetic masters Bashō, Buson, Issa, and Shiki. In presenting themes from Haiku and from Zen literature, Aitken illuminates the relationship between the two. Readers are certain to find this an invaluable and enjoyable experience for the remarkable revelation it offers: in Haiku as in Buddhism, the silences are as expressive as the words.
