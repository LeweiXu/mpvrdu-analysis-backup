Nomination Blank For

THE KENTUCKY DOWNS

LADIES TURF

28th Running

750,000 - Grade III

 $ ^{*} $ (Includes 375,000 from KTDF)

Be Run: Saturday, September 11, 2021

FOR FILLIES AND MARES THREE-YEARS-OLD AND UPWARD. No nomination fee. $1,875 to enter and an additional $1,875 to start. Supplementary nominations may be made at time of entry on Saturday, September 4, 2021 by payment of $18,750 each (includes all fees). $375,000 guaranteed plus 375,000 KTDF for KTDF eligibles only. After payment of 1% to all owners of horses finishing 6th through last, 62% of the remaining purse shall be paid to the owner of the winner, 20% to second, 10% to third, 5% to fourth and 3% to fifth. KTDF funds correspondingly divided providing a KTDF eligible horse finishes in an award position. Three-Year-Olds, 122 lbs.; Older, 126 lbs. Non-winners of a Graded stake at a mile or over on the turf in 2021, allowed 2 lbs.; a sweepstakes at a mile or over on the turf in 2021, 4 lbs. The maximum number of starters will be limited to twelve with four also-eligibles. At the discretion of the Racing Secretary, the also-eligibles may remain on the program and be scratched up to fifteen minutes prior to pari-mutuel windows opening. In the event that more than twelve entries pass the entry box, the starters will be determined at that time with preference given to The Kentucky Downs Preview Ladies Turf winner at Ellis Park, Graded or Group Stakes winners (in order I-II-III), Stakes winners, then highest turf earnings in 2021. The winner of The Kentucky Downs Preview Ladies Turf at Ellis Park will be entitled to a free nomination, waived entry and staring fees and shall be considered a "preferred" entry for this running. Any horse excluded from running because of the aforementioned preference shall be refunded the entry fee and supplementary nomination fee if applicable. Starters and riders must be named through the entry box by the usual time of closing.

One Mile (On the Turf)

## Nominations Close Wednesday, August 25, 2021
