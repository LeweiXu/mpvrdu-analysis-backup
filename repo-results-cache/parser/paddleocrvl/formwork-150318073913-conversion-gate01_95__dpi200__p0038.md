## Design

The large bays constructed provide exceptional flexibility in the design and layout of the building and allow a high degree of freedom in the final appearance.

## Safety

Integral working platforms and edge protection systems. The repetitive, predictable nature of the tasks involved encourages familiarity with operations. The minimal requirement for tools and equipment further reduces the risk of accidents on site.

## Sustainability

The in-situ casting of units on site and the local availability of ready-mixed concrete supplies reduce transportation impacts. Just-in-time deliveries and near zero wastage produce an overall tidier site with associated cost savings and safety benefits. Concrete's thermal mass coupled with correct insulation and boiler design minimises heating costs and can even reduce air-conditioning requirements.
