The ODiSI 6's fiber optic sensors range from 1 m to 50 m in length for distributed strain measurements and 1 m to 5 m for temperature measurements. Optical fiber's flexibility and small diameter (< 0.2 mm) lends itself to being located and routed through and inside parts and locations not typically accessible with foil gages and thermocouples.

Because optical fibers undergo well defined local optical changes as a result of changes in strain and temperature, the ODiSI 6 is able to interpret the reflected signal along a sensor into independent strain or temperature measurements throughout the length of the fiber at intervals as low as 0.65 mm.

### 3.1 ODiSI 6 Specifications

The ODiSI platform is available in 1,2,4, and 8 channel configurations. Channels are sampled serially at the maximum allowable data rate for the specific sensor length and number of active channels. Both temperature and strain sensors can be used on different channels during the same test. The exception is SC Temperature sensors which can only be connected during the same test.
