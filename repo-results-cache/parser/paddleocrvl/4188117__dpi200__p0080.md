## Performance Summary

Robin Hood scales best in almost all workloads.

Otherwise very competitive with Hopscotch Hashing.

Comfortably ahead (10%) with 10% update load. More competitive (5%) with 20% updates.

Robin Hood dominates other concurrent hash tables. Gap narrows during Hyperthreading.

Transactional Robin Hood scales very strongly until Hyperthreading. Then it dies and never recovers.
