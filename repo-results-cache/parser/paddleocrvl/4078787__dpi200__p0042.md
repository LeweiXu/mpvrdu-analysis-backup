The event, hosted by the March of Dimes, took place in April. More than 1,000 participants registered, raising an estimated $200,000. 8 Medication Cleanout completed a successful spring cycle with an event hosted by TTUHSC at Amarillo. Events were also held on TTUHSC campuses in Abilene and Lubbock. In total, more than 3,600 pounds of unused and expired medications were dropped off for appropriate disposal.
