In Figure 5.19-c the comparison of average ice velocities along section A-B (Figure 5.18) reveals that it wasn't a relevant incremental tendency of David Glacier superficial velocities during monitoring period (1973-2006). The speed errors in graph are derived from the method precision of the different studies (see Paragraph 5.4.3). Average velocity computed since January 1973 to January 1990 along section A-B is  $ 158.5 \pm 0.4 \, cm \, d^{-1} $ ( $ 579 \pm 1 \, m \, yr^{-1} $, black continued line in Figure 5.19-c), a bit lower in respect to  $ 159.3 \pm 1.0 \, cm \, d^{-1} $ ( $ 582 \pm 4 \, m \, yr^{-1} $) the average velocity computed since January 1990 to February 2006. During this time interval, average speed presents an appreciable variability, with a minimum equal to  $ 154.9 \pm 0.8 \, cm \, d^{-1} $ ( $ 566 \pm 3 \, m \, yr^{-1} $) on December 1999 - November 2001 period. and a maximum of  $ 164.8 \pm 1.4 \, cm \, d^{-1} $ ( $ 602 \pm 5 \, m \, yr^{-1} $) on November 2001 – December 2002 period.
