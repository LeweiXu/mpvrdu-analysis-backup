## Gender differences in college-level STEM training and retention in STEM occupations

Overall, among adults who majored in STEM, women are more likely than men to work in a STEM occupation (56% vs. 49%). $ ^{32} $ This difference is driven mainly by college graduates with a health professions degree, most of whom are women. About seven-in-ten (69%) women who majored in a health professions field are working in a health-related occupation, as are 61% of men who majored in a health professions field. (Of those who majored in a life sciences field, 30% of men and 33% of women work in a health-related occupation.)
