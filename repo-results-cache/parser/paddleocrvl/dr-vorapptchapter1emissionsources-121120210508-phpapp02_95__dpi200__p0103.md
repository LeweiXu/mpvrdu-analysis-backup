## Search Engine Marketing (SEM)

Sadly in the area of SEM there is still:

- A significant lack of understanding of good search engine marketing principles and which tactic is appropriate depending upon marketing goals and objectives

- Over attribution of overall campaign performance to the last click (ie. paid search)

• Too many generalities – B2C SEM is not the same as B2B SEM

• Lack of focus on insightful and actionable KPI’s
