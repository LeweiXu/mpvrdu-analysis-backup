## BIHAR

## Inbound Shipments

Registered Dealer

All commercial shipments valued more than  $ \frac{9999}{} $- must accompanied with Form D-IX which is also known as Bihar road permit (Suvidha).

Un-Registered / Individual Dealers

- Shipments valued up to `999/-` can travel into Bihar state without any FORM or Entry Tax.

- For Shipments valued between ‘1000/-’ 9999/-can travel with entry tax into Bihar state. (Entry Tax is varies as per commodity)

- Shipments valued above ‘10000/- require Bihar road permit (FORM D-IX) to travel into Bihar state.

## Outbound Shipments

Form DX is required for shipments valued more than  $ \frac{9999}{} $-traveling out of Bihar. Form DVIII is required for within state movement.

Both Incoming & outgoing Forms have validity period as per Distance.

- IN/Out check post name has been made mandatory in Both the Forms.
