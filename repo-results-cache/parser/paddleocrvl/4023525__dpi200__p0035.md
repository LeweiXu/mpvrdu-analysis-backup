### 4.3 Circadian rhythmicity under 16:8 light-dark (LD) regime

After noticing  $ L-cry^{-/-} $animals exhibit a change in circularar behavior I wondered if consequently their circadian rhythmicity is affected as well. In order to see if animals have any steady locomotor patterns in standard culture room conditions I recorded  $ L-cry^{-/-} $and  $ L-cry^{+/+} $animals under 16 h white light followed by 8 h of dark for 56 h and analyzes the locomotor output. To account for circularar influence, I performed the experiment during new moon (NM) and free-running full moon (FR-FM) phase. In astronomy, NM is a moon phase in which moon disc is not visible in the sky (dark moon) which in laboratory conditions translates to complete darkness.
