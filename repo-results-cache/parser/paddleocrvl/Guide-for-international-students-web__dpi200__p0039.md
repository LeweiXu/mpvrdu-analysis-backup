## University Language Programme

## Learn a language for free while you study

That’s right! As a student at NTU, you can learn a new language or improve your existing language skills whether you’re an absolute beginner, already have some skills, or are at an advanced level. It’s no extra cost. You pay a small refundable deposit which you get back after you finish the course.

Study for 2 hours a week or more – it's up to you.

Why ULP?

• Language skills are a great addition to your CV.

- Relax and make friends outside your degree. You’ll practise with students and staff from across the University, and members of the public.

- Language learning facilitates critical thinking, asking about differences, looking for equivalents in your first language and culture.

- It can also help with finding work after graduation – a certified level of proficiency can help to distinguish you from other applicants.
