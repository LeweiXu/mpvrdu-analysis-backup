We consider here the problem of controlling this kind of maps possessing a chaotic behaviour (see the scheme of Fig. 26). The main difference with the classical partial control scheme is that the control can only be applied in the present state  $ x_{n} $, (is not physically possible to control the past states  $ (x_{n-1}, x_{n-2} \ldots) $). Therefore we need to introduce a new approach to control the system.

Following the idea of the partial control method we consider that the system can be modelled as:

 $$ x_{n}=f(x_{n-1},x_{n-2}\cdots x_{n-m})+\xi_{n}+u_{n}, $$ 

where  $ \xi_{n} $ is the disturbance affecting the state  $ x_{n} $, and  $ u_{n} $ is the respective control applied, both limited by

 $$ \left|\xi_{n}\right|\leq\xi_{0},\qquad\left|u_{n}\right|\leq u_{0}. $$
