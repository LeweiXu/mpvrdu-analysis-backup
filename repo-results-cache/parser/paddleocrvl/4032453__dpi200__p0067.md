## Garrett's firechalice

Zauschneria garrettii

Height: 18 inches

Width: 18–24 inches

Native. Late summer color. Spreads underground and can cover a large area in time. Visited by hummingbirds.

## Poppy mallow

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_199_802_720_1142.jpg" alt="Image" width="30%" /></div>


Callirhoe involucrata

Height: 6–12 inches

Width: 2–3 feet

Does not transplant well due to taproot. Reseeds.

## Hollyhock

Tall plants range from biennials to perennials. Visited by many bees. Leafcutters cut circles out of the petals to use in their nests.

## Black-eyed Susan

Height: approximately 24 inches

Width: approximately 24 inches

Variety pulcherrima is native. Various varieties available in cultivation.

A short-lived perennial. Great late-summer color. Vigorous reseeder.

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_199_1500_722_1841.jpg" alt="Image" width="30%" /></div>


## Agastache

Height: 2–3 feet

Width: 18+ inches

A. foeniculum is native. Other species and varieties also available. Some are cold-hardier than others. Visited by bees and hummingbirds.
