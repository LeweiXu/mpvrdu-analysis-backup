“Bottoms makes a sincere attempt to infuse his accounts... with empathy and understanding.”

—Library Journal

In Spiritual American Trash, Greg Bottoms inhabits the spirit of eight artists' works through engaging vignettes. From the janitor who created a holy throne room out of scraps in a garage, to the wartime mother who filled her home with driftwood replicas of Bible scenes, Bottoms illustrates the peculiar grace in madness. Raised in the working-class South as a devout Christian with a troubled brother, Bottoms understands how these eight outsiders "made art for a higher power and for themselves."

THE MONK

Dialogues on Sex, Faith, and Religion

FRANK BROWNING

Sexuality & Gender Studies • Trade Paperback • 9781619021839 $16.95 • 208 pages • 5.5" x 8.3" • Ebook available

In The Monk and the Skeptic, Browning examines the intersection of sexuality and religion through the framework of conversations between the author and a gay priest. They discuss the nature of secular and spiritual friendship; religious thought on same-sex marriage; the relation of the body to God; and the history of how the church has viewed the body and desire. Browning manages to bring in a host of influences to his discussion: Descartes, Locke, Greek myth, Christian myth, Buddhist myth, Harry Potter, St. Thomas Aquinas, as well as modern writers such as Jeanette Winterson, John Boswell, and Daniel Mendelsohn.
