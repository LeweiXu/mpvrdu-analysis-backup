## Care kit

After each 15,000 dispensed coffees, a message is sent once per day that installation of the care kit is overdue. This must be carried out within one week, otherwise the message is displayed after every brewing cycle.

* Install the care kit according to its instructions

## Descaling

The water hardness, the water flow, and whether a water filter is used determine the schedule for descaling. This point in time is calculated by the WMF 1500 S and displayed.
