## GOALS & INTENTIONS

We have many goals and intentions for this zine.

We want to put forth genuine and honest statements about where we're at, where we hope to be, and how we can be in meaningful solidarity with Black revolutionaries and challenge anti-Black racism in our communities.

We want to be reflective and active.

We want to focus on concrete actions, recognizing multiple manifestations of “the work” that include more than physically showing up to a protest.
