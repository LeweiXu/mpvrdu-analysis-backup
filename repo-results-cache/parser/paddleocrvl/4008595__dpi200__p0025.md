A well designed and operated desalter can achieve between 85 and 99% removal of inorganic salts from the crude oil feed and the water content of the desalted crude will be less than 0.2 volume % of the desalted crude.

The most common setups for desalting units for crude oil are:

✓ Two desalters in series, where the brine from the second unit can be treated and used as wash water for the first unit.

✓ Two desalters in series with no recycle of brine from the second stage.
