PREMIO - OHTLI -

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_764_863_1135_1232.jpg" alt="Image" width="18%" /></div>


Ohtli is a Nahuatl word meaning: The way you and I, we all, must go or live. This awards, the highest one given by the government of Mexico recognizes annually with a medal, a rosette of silver, and a diploma, those who have dedicated most of their lives helping developing professional activities for the welfare of

the community and have distinguished themselves for their work of many years benefiting the Mexican community abroad in any field of human endeavor.
