It should be noted that data concerning the fate of Indigenous children involved in any justice system are exceedingly difficult to parse. $ ^{302} $ Federal data collection is supposed to improve in the coming years due to the passage of the Tribal Law and Order Act. $ ^{303} $ However, there are, in fact, far more updated and detailed statistical reports available regarding the procedures for collecting data in cases involving Indigenous defendants than there are actual reports about case outcomes. $ ^{304} $
