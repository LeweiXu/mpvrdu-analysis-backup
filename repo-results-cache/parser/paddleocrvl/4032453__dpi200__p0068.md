## Narrow-leaf coneflower

Echinacea angustifolia

Heith: 18–24 inches

Width: 12–18 inches

Native. More drought-tolerant than E. purpurea (below) but not as large and showy. Reseeds. Takes a few years to bulk up. May need to grow from seed.

## Purple coneflower

Echinacea purpurea

Height: 2–3 feet

Width: 18–24 inches

Late-summer blooms. Not a xeric plant in some areas of Wyoming.

Performs better with supplemental water.

## Beebalm

Monarda didyma

Height: up to 24 inches. Dwarf 'Petite Delight' is 12–15 inches.

Width: 18–24 inches

Susceptible to powdery mildew. Choose resistant cultivars.

'Fireball' pictured.

## Spike speedwell

Veronica spicata

Height: 12+ inches

Width: up to 18 inches

Various varieties. Visited by bees.
