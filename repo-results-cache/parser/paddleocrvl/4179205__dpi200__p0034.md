## Abstract:-

Silicon is definitely, the most common material used in solar cells. Distributed Generation (DG) based on Solar Photo Voltaic (PV) cells enhance the voltage stability and economical profit as well as reduce the carbon emission. This paper identifies the optimal location of PV base DG in 13-Bus distribution system, using the nature base Particle swarm Optimization (PSO) algorithm. This paper also presents the overview of the process of manufacturing of PV cell. The main objective of this paper is that to demonstrate the economical benefits of optimal PV based power plant. Results prove that the PV base power plant improves the technical performance as well as economical performance of the system.

Keywords:- PSO, PV, Voltage Profile, Economical Benefits.
