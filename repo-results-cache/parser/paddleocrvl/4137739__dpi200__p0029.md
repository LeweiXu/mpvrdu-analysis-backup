### 4.5 Regulating Tray Height

If you wish to vend products that do not fit into the space between trays because of their height, the trays may move in increments of 32mm in the following manner.

1. Shut down the machine.

2. Disconnect the connection cable (A) from the tray.

3. Remove the shelf by lifting it gently.

4. Remove the screws (B) holding the bracket (C).

5. Place the bracket in the new supports and rescrew.

6. Repeat the operation with the other bracket.

7. Replace the tray and reconnect it.
