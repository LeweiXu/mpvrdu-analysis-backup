## Current Conservation Practices

Coordinator and Staff

South Jordan City has a full time Water Conservation Coordinator that manages and directs the conservation program. The coordinator schedules appointments, manages all the program documents and rebates and is able to focus on public outreach and conservation initiatives on a daily basis. In 2019 the City was able to bring on an additional full-time position for the water conservation group. With the added position the City is now able to complete more rebates, audits and consultations as well as assisting customers with leak checks at their homes. These programs and positions are overseen by the Public Works Director, Associate Director and Water Manager.
