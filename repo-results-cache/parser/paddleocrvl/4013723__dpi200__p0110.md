## Starch digestibility

Excreta starch digestibility determined on days 5, 7 and 14 was high ( $ >0.90 $) for all three cereals and there were no significant differences between the cereals (Table 4 and figure 3). Significant age effects (P < 0.0001) were observed, with average digestibility coefficients of 0.95, 0.92 and 0.97 for days 5, 7 and 14, respectively.
