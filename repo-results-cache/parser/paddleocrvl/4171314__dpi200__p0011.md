### 2.2 Avalability and Reliability

Very high reliability and availability is one of the most important mainframe characteristics. Although very little hard data are available, there appears to be general agreement, that mainframe hardware and software feature indeed superior high reliability and availability characteristics. IBM claims an availability for a Sysplex mainframe configuration of 99.999 %. This means a downtime of ca. 5 minutes/year, or less than  $ \frac{1}{2} $ hour during an assumed lifetime of 5 years.

There is not a single feature responsible for high availability and reliability. Rather, there are hundreds or thousands of individual operating systems, subsystems and hardware design characteristics that contribute. A few examples are listed below.
