## Spray arms

It is necessary to clean the spray arms regularly for hard water chemicals will clog the spray arm jets and bearings.

To clean the spray arms, follow the instructions below:

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_423_571_858_968.jpg" alt="Image" width="25%" /></div>


<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_912_571_1348_970.jpg" alt="Image" width="25%" /></div>


To remove the upper spray arm, hold the nut in the center still and rotate the spray arm counterclockwise to remove it.

To remove the lower spray arm, pull out the spray arm upward.

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_422_1140_857_1539.jpg" alt="Image" width="25%" /></div>


Wash the arms in soapy and warm water and use a soft brush to clean the jets. Replace them after rinsing them thoroughly.
