## Macro Environment Within Each Opportunity, Start with Questions, Not Data

Traditionally, organisations are tempted to start by gathering all available data before beginning their analysis.

This leads to an all-encompassing focus on data management – collecting, cleansing and converting data – with little time or energy or resources to understand its potential uses.
