The number of individual policies terminated by surrender increased in 2009 to 827 (2008: 570) representing sums assured of MK119.6 million, up from MK83 million in 2008. In group business however policy surrenders registered decreased to three from twelve in 2008 representing annual insured sum of MK517 million, down from MK1.4 million assured under the 12 policies in 2008. Hence surrender value in terms of annual premiums decreased by MK844 million.

The industry recorded 1,353 policy terminations by lapse. All but five of these were in the individual business category. In terms of annual premiums, the terminations value increased from MK28.4 million to MK112.1 million over the year.

The life insurance sector continued to grow, having registered a steady aggregate growth in total assets of 14.8 percent during the year.
