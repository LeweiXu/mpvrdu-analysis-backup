## If you need to be brought home – Section B9

## If this happened:

As a result of emergency and necessary medical treatment during your trip abroad we decided that it was medically necessary for you to return home either before or after your scheduled return date, or you were unfortunate enough to suffer a serious accident, illness or injury during your trip which resulted in your death.

PLEASE NOTE: We will liaise with your treating doctor(s) about your treatment plan and if required obtain a 'fit to fly' certificate.

We will also liaise with you and advise on, and also put in place, suitable repatriation plans to get your home as soon as it is medically safe to do so in order to achieve your optimal recovery.

PLEASE BE AWARE: If you have not purchased a return ticket, we will deduct the costs of an economy flight (based on the cost on the date you came home) from any costs we have incurred in returning you to your home.

## This is what we will do:

We will pay you up to

## £200,000

for customary and reasonable costs that are medically necessary to enable us to return you to your home or nursing home, or to move you to the most suitable hospital in your home country as arranged by the assistance team;

## £200,000
