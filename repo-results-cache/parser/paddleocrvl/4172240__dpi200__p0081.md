Queen Phenix. IMO 9129718. Length 110 m, 3,381 t. Panamanian flag. Classification society Nippon Kaiji Kyokai. Built in 1996 in Ulsan (South Korea) by Hyundai. Owned by Daiichi Tanker Co Ltd (Japan). 3 detentions: in 2005 in Shanghai (China), in 2013 in Ulsan (South Korea) and in 2015 in Jiaxing (China). Sold for demolition in India.

Senshu Maru. IMO 8014473. Length 283 m, 30,296 t. Panamanian flag. Classification society Nippon Kaiji Kyokai. Built in 1984 in Chiba (Japan) by Mitsui. Owned by Mitsui OSK Lines (Japan). Sold for demolition in India. 523 US$ per ton. The terms of sale include a clause requiring the selected shipbreaking yard to have been delivered a statement of compliance with the standards of the Hong Kong Convention by Nippon Kaiji Kyokai.
