The Sub-Fund shall not invest in shares of other sub-funds, UCITS and/or other UCIs (including but not limited to ETF and other open-ended funds) above 10% of the net assets of the Sub-Fund.

The Sub-Fund will be actively managed.

## Use of derivatives or other investment techniques and instruments

The Sub-Fund may use derivative instruments for the purpose of trading or hedging its currency exposure to other currency than the euro.

## Risk Management Process

The global risk exposure will be calculated by using the absolute VAR approach. The absolute VaR of the Sub-Fund may not exceed 20% of the net asset value of the Sub-Fund using a confidence interval of 99% and a holding period of one month.

The expected level of leverage based on the sum of all notional amounts will be approximately 300% of the net asset value of the Sub-Fund. Higher levels of leverage are however possible.

The use of the sums of notional methodology may result in a high level of leverage which does not necessarily reflect the actual level of risk of the portfolio. In particular, the Sub-Fund’s level of leverage will be predominantly obtained through the use of financial derivatives instruments used both for investment and hedging purposes. This does not automatically mean an increase in the global risk of the Sub-Fund.

## Investor Profile

Investment in this Sub-Fund is suitable for investors looking for a minimum of one year investment and seeking a stable income with moderate level of volatility.
