# Whipped Potatoes

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_136_547_314_657.jpg" alt="Image" width="10%" /></div>


USA

total time:

10 min

Ingredients for 90 lt/24 gal pan

Idaho Potatoes 40 kg / 88 lbs (peeled and kept whole)

Water

Salt

Butter

1,6 kg / 3,5 lbs

## Procedure:

▶ Add water and salt to the pan and bring to boil in boiling mode

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_168_1178_210_1221.jpg" alt="Image" width="2%" /></div>


TIP: with big quantities of water preheat in boiling mode to speed up the cooking process

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_166_1290_210_1337.jpg" alt="Image" width="2%" /></div>


TIP: ensure the salt melts in the water, stir if needed

Peel potatoes with potato peeler

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_825_1_1653_715.jpg" alt="Image" width="50%" /></div>


<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_685_534_769_617.jpg" alt="Image" width="5%" /></div>


gluten free

Add the potatoes to the water and stick the probe into one of them before putting into the water

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_655_885_694_929.jpg" alt="Image" width="2%" /></div>


TIP: for optimal cooking result ensure the potatoes are of uniform size

When water boils, lock down the lid and set core temperature to  $ 85^{\circ} $C /  $ 185^{\circ} $F

▶ Cook under pressure 🍜 till potatoes reach core temperature set

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_655_1178_695_1219.jpg" alt="Image" width="2%" /></div>


TIP: to ensure the best performance wait that water boils before starting the pressure phase

Allow the pressure to release and strain off all of the water

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_1139_776_1180_817.jpg" alt="Image" width="2%" /></div>


TIP: to reduce the quantity of excess steam released, wait till steam condensation process completes before unlocking and opening the lid fully

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_1140_955_1179_996.jpg" alt="Image" width="2%" /></div>


TIP: keep the lid completely open when tilt the well to avoid demerging the gasket

Add the butter and whip the potatoes with Electrolux Bermixer Pro

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_1138_1161_1180_1206.jpg" alt="Image" width="2%" /></div>


TIP: serve within 20 minutes to preserve quality and texture
