ADDITIONAL REMARKS :

Short field description

A moderately deep (determined by the watertable), imperfectly to poorly drained, very dark grey clay loam over a brown to light reddish brown silty clay loam.

The soil profile is located in a concave-convex zone, locally called "waru-waru". The waru-waru are disappearing because they are not used now. This technology has been developed to take advantage of the greenhouse effect and the flood land of the Titicaca lake area.
