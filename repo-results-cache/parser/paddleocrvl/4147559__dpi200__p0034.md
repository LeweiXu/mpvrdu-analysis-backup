# World Travel Market 2016 Industry Report

### 5. Responsible Tourism

UK holidaymakers think that the travel industry rather than the government should take the lead in responsible tourism. Nearly six out of ten (59%) of UK holidaymakers polled said it is the industry’s duty to make tourism sustainable, compared with one in four (25%) who believe that the government should be in driving seat. The majority (55%) of industry respondents agree that the trade has the greatest responsibility to climate change, while 70% believe the tourism industry has become more sustainable over the last 10 years.
