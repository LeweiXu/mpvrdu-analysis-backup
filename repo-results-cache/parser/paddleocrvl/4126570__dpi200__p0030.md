## Procedure:

Soak all the lentils (Rajma, Black Lentils, Chana Dal) over night in cold water

▶ Preheat the pan at 160°C / 320°F in braising mode

▶ Add oil, onions, spices and give a quick stir

▶ Add soaked lentils, water, salt and ginger juilienne

▶ Close the lid and cook under pressure ☐ for 20 minutes

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_654_1239_697_1281.jpg" alt="Image" width="2%" /></div>


TIP: to ensure the best performance wait that liquid boils before starting the pressure phase

Open the lid, add dry fenugreek leaves (optional), cream and butter



Garnish with fresh coriander and serve
