Aggregated (1992, 2002, and 2008) from the NSCWS. Job satisfaction is measured by a standardized response to the following questions: "All in all, how satisfied are you with your job?"; "Knowing what you know now, if you had to decide all over again whether to take the job you have now, what would you decide?"; and "If a good friend of yours told you that he or she was interested in working for your employer, what would you tell your friend?" As can be seen, the gap in satisfaction is occurring among those aged 26-60.
