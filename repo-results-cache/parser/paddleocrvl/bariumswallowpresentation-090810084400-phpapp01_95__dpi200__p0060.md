## TRACHEOESOPHAGEAL FISTULA

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_163_340_970_1538.jpg" alt="Image" width="37%" /></div>


• Congenital/Acquired

• Ideal contrast non ionic water soluble media

• Barium fluid like & pt lying laterally.

• In case fistula not identify laterally, put in prone.

- If fistula seen, stop procedure as barium aspiration result in inflammation and granuloma.
