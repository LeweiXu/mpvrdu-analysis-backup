A moderately deep, well drained, (dark) brown clay. The texture becomes more sandy in the subsoil.

The soil site is located in 'Tierra firme', i.e. not flooded by rivers, on the summit of a hill along the road.

The layers of parent material show an undulating arrangement. This and the irregular texture with depth are indications that the parent material could also be an old alluvium.
