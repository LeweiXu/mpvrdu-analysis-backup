### 2.3 Results

#### 2.3.1 Tree Density and Size

Tree density tended to increase from the edge to the interior of the forest (Figure 3), though results were not significant (P=0.100, Table 2). There was no evidence that level of disturbance was related to tree density (P=0.442). The number of trees in each 0.15 hectare (ha) plot ranged from a low of 226 in the edge plot (HD 1.0) to a high of 311 trees in the low disturbance interior plot (LD 3.0). Tree densities in this study thus ranged from 1506 trees/ha to 2073 trees/ha. Average density was  $ 1734 \pm 213 $ trees/ha.
