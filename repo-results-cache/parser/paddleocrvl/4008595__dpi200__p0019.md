Crude oil is the feed to the petrochemical plant where crude oil is transformed, through different chemical processing units, to more valuable products which are liquefied petroleum gas, gasoline, reformate, Kerosene and jet fuel, diesel, and asphalt. The petrochemical plant consists of storage tanks for the crude oil and the products, desalting unit, oil refinery for crude oil processing, wastewater treatment plant, electric generation plant, and flaring station for normal and emergency emission of gases.
