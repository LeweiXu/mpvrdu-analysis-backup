### 13.1 Sensors for Marine Craft

The primary measurement systems for model-based navigation filters when used onboard a surface craft are global satellite navigation systems (GNSS) and heading angle sensors. More specific,

GNSS position

• GNSS heading

• Magnetic compass

• Gyrocompass

The position and heading angle measurements are used as aiding – to prevent drift when integrating the equations of motion. Sometimes and inertial measurement unit (IMU) is included in the state estimator as optional measurements.
