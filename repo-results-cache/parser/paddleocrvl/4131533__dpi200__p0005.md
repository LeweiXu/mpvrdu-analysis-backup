<div style="text-align: center;">1 Glossary</div>



<table border=1 style='margin: auto; word-wrap: break-word;'><tr><td style='text-align: center; word-wrap: break-word;'>Term</td><td style='text-align: center; word-wrap: break-word;'>Definition</td></tr><tr><td style='text-align: center; word-wrap: break-word;'>Channel</td><td style='text-align: center; word-wrap: break-word;'>A port on the front panel of the ODiSI 6 instrument. There is a maximum of 8 channels on an ODiSI.</td></tr><tr><td style='text-align: center; word-wrap: break-word;'>Configuration</td><td style='text-align: center; word-wrap: break-word;'>A set of Luna defined hardware and software parameters that change the measurement capabilities of the ODiSI 6. Measurement parameters that change between configurations include: measurement rate, maximum sensor length, and gage pitch. The ODiSI 6 software automatically selects the fastest measurement rate based on the maximum sensor length detected and user chosen Gage Pitch.</td></tr><tr><td style='text-align: center; word-wrap: break-word;'>Gage</td><td style='text-align: center; word-wrap: break-word;'>Subset of a fiber sensor that is used to make a single strain or temperature measurement. A fiber sensor is divided into numerous gages which provide distributed measurements of strain or temperature along the fiber.</td></tr><tr><td style='text-align: center; word-wrap: break-word;'>Gage Pitch</td><td style='text-align: center; word-wrap: break-word;'>Length of fiber between the center of a single gage and the center of a neighboring gage.</td></tr><tr><td style='text-align: center; word-wrap: break-word;'>High-Definition (HD) Sensor</td><td style='text-align: center; word-wrap: break-word;'>A fiber sensor that provides a unique Rayleigh scatter signature that the ODiSI 6 can use to make distributed strain or temperature measurements.</td></tr><tr><td style='text-align: center; word-wrap: break-word;'>Measurement Rate</td><td style='text-align: center; word-wrap: break-word;'>This is the rate (in Hz) at which the ODiSI 6 provides strain or temperature measurements.</td></tr><tr><td style='text-align: center; word-wrap: break-word;'>ODiSI Measurement Streaming Protocol (OMSP)</td><td style='text-align: center; word-wrap: break-word;'>JSON based protocol for the transfer of ODiSI 6 strain or temperature data to another PC. A variation of the OMSP is used for the storage of ODiSI measurement data.</td></tr><tr><td style='text-align: center; word-wrap: break-word;'>Reference</td><td style='text-align: center; word-wrap: break-word;'>Data set containing the unique Rayleigh scatter pattern of a single fiber sensor. This is compared to measurement scans to calculate strain or temperature. When a sensor is rekeyed, a new reference data set is captured.</td></tr><tr><td style='text-align: center; word-wrap: break-word;'>Remote Module (Standard Length)</td><td style='text-align: center; word-wrap: break-word;'>Supports sensor lengths from 1 m to 20 m.</td></tr><tr><td style='text-align: center; word-wrap: break-word;'>Remote Module (Extended Length)</td><td style='text-align: center; word-wrap: break-word;'>Supports sensor lengths from 1 m to 50 m.</td></tr><tr><td style='text-align: center; word-wrap: break-word;'>RLC</td><td style='text-align: center; word-wrap: break-word;'>Ruggedized LC connector. These connectors are used for the connections between the standoff cable and ODiSI 6 as well as the remote module.</td></tr><tr><td style='text-align: center; word-wrap: break-word;'>Segment</td><td style='text-align: center; word-wrap: break-word;'>A collection of Gages defined by a Start Gage and an End Gage.</td></tr></table>
