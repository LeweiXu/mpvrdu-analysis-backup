## Chapter 2

## Robotic Arm

### 2.1 Parts

A 5 DOF (degrees of freedom) robotic arm was designed and created using a 3D printer (Figure 10)The first three parts of the arm (the base, the shoulder and the elbow) as well as their corresponding links and servos form the articulated manipulator part of the arm, the remaining parts (the roll, the pitch, and the gripper) form the spherical wrist.

The body of the arm is fully made of plastic. Each servo motor controls the part of the arm that succeeds it. This means that each servo is applying a variable amount of torque.

To understand how powerful each servo has to be, torque calculations were made on every joint.
