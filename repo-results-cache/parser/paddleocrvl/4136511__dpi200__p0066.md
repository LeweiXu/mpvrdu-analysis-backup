### 2. Description of the Spanish incentive regime

In Spain, support to the production of electricity from RES $ ^{10} $ started in 1997 with Law 54/1997 $ ^{11} $, followed in 1998 by Royal Decree (RD) 2818/1998. $ ^{12} $ These legal acts introduced a new regime for renewable energy sources (RES) in electricity, which was enhanced by a generous incentive system for investments in the production of electricity from renewables by RD 436/2004 $ ^{13} $. In 2007 RD 661/2007 $ ^{14} $ replaced the existing support with an increasingly rewarding incentive regime for the production of electricity.
