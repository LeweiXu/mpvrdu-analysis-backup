Lateral water mass transport through open ocean gateways influences the structure of the STG, and induces STG variability on intraseasonal time scales. A modern Greenland-Scotland Ridge controls the strength of the subpolar gyre because it acts as a natural boundary for horizontal transport. The ridge also adds more persistence to the subpolar gyre and suppresses small scale fluctuations.
