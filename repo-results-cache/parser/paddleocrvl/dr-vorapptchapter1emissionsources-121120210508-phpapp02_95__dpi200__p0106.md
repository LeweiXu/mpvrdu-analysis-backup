## Understanding Search Engine Marketing

71% of sponsored search clicks are navigational

- This indicates that almost three quarters of our sponsored search is not bringing in new prospects – it is simply delivering people who are already actively looking for our URL.

## 50 % of search dollars = branded search

- This indicates that more than half or our sponsored search is being spent on navigational clicks.

44% of people who click on paid search are exposed to display ads prior to the last click.

- Yet the majority of agencies and marketers are applying 100% of their campaign performance to the last click (ie. paid search).
