## 8 Conclusions

The study’s objectives were met:

- to collate and analyse laboratory data on compost and digestate quality in Ireland since 2008;

- to compare the Irish data with those in other databases and standards to propose a draft quality standard for compost and another for digestate (whole, liquid and fibre) for public consultation; and

- to review end-of-waste approaches in other European countries to recommend a strategy on how Ireland should implement national end-of-waste criteria for compost and digestate derived from source-separated waste materials.
