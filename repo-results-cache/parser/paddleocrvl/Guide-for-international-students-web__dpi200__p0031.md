## University preparation courses

If you don’t meet the entry requirements for your NTU course, you may want to do one of our preparation courses at our on-campus partner institution – Nottingham Trent International College (NTIC).

Our Foundation Certificate, International Year One and pre-Masters courses cover a range of subject areas, including:



• Art, Design and Media

• Business, Law and Social Sciences

• Computing

• Science and Engineering

As an NTIC student, you'll have access to all University facilities and can enjoy guaranteed entry to NTU when you pass your NTIC course at the required level with good attendance.
