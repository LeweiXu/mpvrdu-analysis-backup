### G. Summary

Table VI raises suspicions that the empirical results of the

preceding sections cannot be relied upon to support the causal relation-

ships that are hypothesized to exist between wage settlements in the

various industries. Table VI shows that a high correlation coefficient

exists between the rate of wage change in each industry. This means that

the settlements in almost any industry could be found to be as significant

as that of the hypothesized industry in explaining the wage movements of

another.

This result, however, should not be surprising if, as was

pointed out at the beginning of this chapter, the wage hierarchy has re-

mained constant over the period of analysis. If emulation is the chief

factor in determining wage settlements, then similar wage movements are

to be expected in all industries.
