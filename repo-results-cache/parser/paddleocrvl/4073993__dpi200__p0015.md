## Strategic development

## Groupe BPCE has engaged a major step in its transformation and the development of its businesses

On February 9th, 2021, Groupe BPCE announced the public tender offer on the 29% Natixis shares the Group didn’t already own, to engage the simplification of its organization

This simplified organisation will increase the strategic flexibility of the businesses and allow acceleration in their development for the benefit of their customers and their performance

And also improve capital flows within the Group, in an increasingly challenging regulatory environment

The tender offer was successful, and the squeeze-out took place on July 21st, 2021.
