### I. BACKGROUND/ANALYSIS:

The Castle Sewer Interceptor Pump Station Pump Station is one of the oldest wastewater lift stations that the City of Atwater currently operates. The original build date is currently not known, but it was originally built by Castle Air Force. The pump station currently receives sewer flows from the Atwater Prison, the area formally known as the old Castle Airforce Base, and from the old base housing that it located on the corner of Buhach Rd and E.Bellevue Rd.

The Castle Sewer Interceptor Pump Station currently serves an average flow of.45 million gallons per day. The pumps that serve this flow are currently temporary portable pumps due to the fact that the current infrastructure at the pump station is outdated and must be fully replaced to be operable.

On March 24, 2020 a Request for Proposals was routed to local companies, Builders Exchanges and posted to the City website. Two (2) proposals were received.

The City of Atwater sent out a Request for Proposals to redesign the Castle Sewer Interceptor Pump Station. Updating the Castle Sewer Interceptor Pump Station is essential to reliability and ensure safety at the site. The pumps that are no longer working at the site are located below grade present confined spaced entry issues.
