## DCET 3 Year Strategic Planning Process 2013 - 2014 - 2015

DCET’s Strategic Planning is a tool for changing your mode of functioning from “reactive” to Proactive.” You anticipate, plan and create the future. It stimulates creative thinking about the future. The plan acts as an excellent public relations piece for investors as well as a blueprint for DCET’s growth. An important benefit of the process is team building only nurtured by an inclusive process. Getting input from all segments of our organization we:

• Improve communication.

• Increase everyone’s investment in the organization.

• Align values and beliefs'

• Solve organizational problems by looking at the whole picture.

• Anticipate obstacles.

• Develop a common vision

• Provide a framework for day-to-day decisions.

• Create a marketing & investment piece.
