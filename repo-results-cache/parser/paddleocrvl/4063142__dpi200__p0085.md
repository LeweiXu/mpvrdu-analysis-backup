### 26. Non-current assets held for sale and subsequently unbundled

RMBH implemented a far-reaching re-organisation on 7 March 2011. This included, inter alia the separation of RMBH's insurance and banking interests, through the transfer of RMBH's insurance interests to a wholly-owned subsidiary, RMI; and the unbundling of RMI to RMBH's ordinary shareholders on a one-for-one basis and the separate listing of RMI on the JSE as an insurance-focused investment entity.

The unbundling transaction resulted in RMBH classifying the RMI as a disposal group held for distribution in line with the requirements of IFRS 5 Non-current Assets Held for Sale and Discontinued Operations. The assets and liabilities attributable to RMI were classified as held for distribution and separately disclosed in the statement of financial position. In addition, RMI represents a discontinued operation as it is a component of RMBH that has been classified as held for distribution and represents a separate major line of business. In line with the requirements of IFRS 5, the income and expenses relating to RMI are presented in the consolidated income statement and statement of other comprehensive income as a single amount relating to the after tax profit and other comprehensive income relating to discontinued operations.

The dividend-in-specie was accounted for at fair value in line with the requirements of IFRIC 17 Distribution of Non-Cash Assets to Owners.

The unbundling transaction was approved by RMBH shareholders on 18 February 2011 and effective date of the unbundling transaction was 28 February 2011.
