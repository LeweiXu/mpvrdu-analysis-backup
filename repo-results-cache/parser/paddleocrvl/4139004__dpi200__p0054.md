Parametrization for curves: used conventions and definitions

Let  $ \Gamma $ be the boundary of a connected regular domain  $ \Omega $ and let  $ x_{\Gamma} = (x(t), y(t)) $ be a counterclockwise parametrization of this curve in terms of a parameter  $ t \in [t_{1}, t_{2}] $ (this parametrization can be only local). The curvilinear abscissa s

is defined as

 $$ d s=\sqrt{x^{\prime}(t)^{2}+y^{\prime}(t)^{2}}d t $$ 

and the (inward) normal (unitary) is defined by

 $$ \vec{n}(t)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{x^{\prime}(t)^{2}+y^{\prime}(t)^{2}}}\left(\begin{array}{c}-y^{\prime}(t)\\ x^{\prime}(t)\end{array}\right) $$ 

The tangential vector  $ \vec{\tau} $ is defined by  $ \vec{\tau} = \frac{d}{dt} x_{\Gamma} $ and is given by

 $$ \vec{\tau}(t)=\left(\begin{array}{l}x^{\prime}(t)\\ y^{\prime}(t)\end{array}\right) $$ 

The curvature  $ c(t) $ is defined by

 $$ \frac{d\vec{n}}{d t}=c(t)\vec{\tau} $$ 

and therefore can be expressed as

 $$ c\left(t\right)=\frac{x^{\prime\prime}\left(t\right)y^{\prime}\left(t\right)+y^{\prime\prime}\left(t\right)x^{\prime}\left(t\right)}{\left(x^{\prime}\left(t\right)^{2}+y^{\prime}\left(t\right)^{2}\right)^{3/2}} $$
