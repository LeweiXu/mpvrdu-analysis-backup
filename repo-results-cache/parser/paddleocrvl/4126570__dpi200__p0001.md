Braising pan of 90 It (24 gal) can contain up to 4 GN 1/1 TRAYS (with suspended frame)

Braising pan of 170 lt (45 gal) can contain up to 6 GN 1/1 TRAYS (with suspended frame)

Please be aware that when using trays in a recipe, amount of full load in a 170 It (45 gal) braising pan, are not necessarily double the amount of full load suggested for a 90 It (24 gal) pan.
