## For That Which is Revealed in Scripture

1. Self (Matthew 6:11; Colossians 1:9-12)

2. Family (Ephesians 6:4)

3. Community (Jeremiah 29:7; 2 Chronicles 7:14)

4. Church (John 13:35; 17:20-21; Philippians 1:9-11; 2 Thessalonians 3:1)

5. Nation (Psalm 33:12; Proverbs 14:34; 1 Timothy 2:2)

6. Non-believers (Romans 10:1; Ephesians 6:18-20)

7. The sick (James 5:14-16)

8. The poor and oppressed (Proverbs 29:7)
