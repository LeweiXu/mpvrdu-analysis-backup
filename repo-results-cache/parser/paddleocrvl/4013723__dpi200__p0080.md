Similarity coefficient analyses allow for the determination of dietary overlap between immigrant and host country diet. This may be used as a measure of dietary acculturation and as such, may help determine appropriate dietary intervention strategies. We are unaware of any studies to date looking at the degree of general acculturation of Pakistani or W. Indies groups in the UK with which to compare our dietary acculturation data. When considering these results, it is important to remember that similarity coefficient analyses need to be conducted in tandem with other measures of acculturation in order to place in context not only whether the diets are similar, but how they are similar and what processes have made them similar. Many factors influence the acculturation process in general, and dietary acculturation specifically.
