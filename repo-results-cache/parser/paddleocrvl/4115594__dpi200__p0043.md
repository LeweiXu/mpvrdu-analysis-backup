### B. Tutor Questionnaires

Compass carried out the questionnaire with 5 trainers living in Tyrol. The methodology used for this activity was the same that was used for migrants and refugees. The participants were asked to fill out the questionnaire that was sent by e-mail. The results of questionnaires were analysed by the project team and were reflected through diagrams as shown below.
