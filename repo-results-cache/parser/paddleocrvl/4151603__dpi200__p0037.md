The self-heating test is limited in the sense that it mostly only distinguishes between very mature and very immature compost and is not as good at measuring stability between the two extremes. Because of the limitation it is not a good enough test for compost used in growing media, but it is suitable for compost plant operators to use themselves to monitor the stability of compost easily at their plant. Operators in plants (e.g. in Belgium and Portugal) use the test to monitor the process before a sample is sent to an independent laboratory for testing.
