For 2010 I hope we will be able to expand the global aspect of sustainable development, introducing countries outside the European Union. The initiated collaboration between the MPA and our sister agency in New Delhi, India, during the Indo-Swedish health week arranged by the Governments of India and Sweden, gives me great hope since sustainable development is one of the agreed areas for further collaboration. Further on the spring agenda is a meeting in Washington on sustainable development of pharmaceuticals including a presentation of the MistraPharma research and a possibility of collaboration with the USA.

Together we should take the responsibility of sustainable development for pharmaceuticals into the future thus ensuring that the international policies set up by the UN and the EU will result in concrete measures.

Christie C. Aberman

Christina R Åkerman

Director General of the Swedish Medical Products Agency

Member of the MistraPharma’s Programme Board
