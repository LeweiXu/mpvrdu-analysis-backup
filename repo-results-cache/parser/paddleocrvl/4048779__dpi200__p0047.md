Students may also drop a course at the mid-term report. card period in February, only after careful discussion and consultation with the teacher, the parent, and the Guidance DepartmentAcademic Counsellor. A Course Change Request Form must be signed by a member of the Guidance Department and the parent in order for this change to be officially noted.

Ontario Curriculum documents are available in full PDF on the Ministry of Education Website: http://www.edu.gov.on.ca/eng/. Course outlines and course summaries are available through subject teachers., Instructional Leaders, or the Director of Academic Counselling. IB curriculum documents can also be requested from the IB Diploma Coordinator.
