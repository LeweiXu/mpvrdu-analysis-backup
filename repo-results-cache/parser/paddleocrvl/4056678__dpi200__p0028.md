## If you are denied boarding on your inbound flight – Section B7

## If this happened:

You were denied boarding on the return journey of your trip due to having or being suspected of having an infectious disease (including Covid-19).

## This is what we will do:

We will pay you up to

## £40

for additional accommodation (of a standard no greater than your original booking) for each full 24-hour period that you are unable to return to your home country up to a maximum of

## £560

We will pay up to

## £300 Europe, £500 Worldwide

towards the cost of return transportation to your home country (of a standard no greater than the class of journey and in the same mode of transport you paid for on your outward journey) at the earliest possible date based on medical or local authority advice.

There is no deductible amount for this section
