## ESPLANADE Collection

The ESPLANADE Collection is a design outdoor furniture line offering a clean flawless finish. The benches can be assembled in sequence with intermediate supports. The waste receptacle features a reclining system to ease the removal of trash or recycling.

Ideal for urban spaces, this outdoor public furniture collection was created by the renowned urban design firm Daoust Lestage Inc.
