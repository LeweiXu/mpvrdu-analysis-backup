## IDENTITY WARS

Come and let us declare in Zion the work of the LORD our God.

—Jeremiah 51:6–10

In the context of this chapter, God’s people are being held captive by Babylon. They have been led astray, but God is going to deliver them, not because they are worthy, but because they are His children.

While the term, “Babylon is fallen,” is a statement of judgment and condemnation, it is simultaneously a promise of freedom to Israel, because Babylon had held Israel captive.

The fall of Babylon contained in the Second Angel’s Message releases spiritual Israel from insecurity, worthlessness and the controlling spirit that compels us to sin. When we recognise that we are accepted in the Beloved, that we are indeed God’s children through the sacrifice of Jesus, all our insecurity and worthlessness is swept away, and we stand free as the children of God.

The Three Angels Messages are also called the Elijah Message, and it is no accident that the final part of this message in Malachi 4:6 says that God will turn the hearts of the fathers to the children and the children to the fathers. In other words, the power of this message will be released when we truly believe that we are children of God, not by anything we have done, but by what Jesus alone has done for us.

Forsake Babylon and her Duracell principles. No longer remain a servant, but cry “Abba Father” and know that you are His beloved child. Through Christ, we are free.
