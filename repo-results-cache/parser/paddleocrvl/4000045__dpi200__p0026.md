## Description, age and tenure

The property comprises the whole of Tower II of Tern Centre, which is a twin-tower office development completed in 1990 in Central. Tower II comprises 18 storeys, in which the ground floor and lower ground floor are used as retail shops whilst the remaining floors are used as offices.

The total gross floor area and saleable area of the property is approximately 37,322 sq.ft. (3,467.30 sq.m.) and 23,130 sq.ft. (2,148.83 sq.m.) respectively.
