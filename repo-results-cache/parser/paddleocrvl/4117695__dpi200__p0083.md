## Conclusions

1. From January 1 to December 31, 2019, 72 cases of violations of the right to freedom of conscience and religion were recorded among Christians in Poland: 5 physical attacks against believers, 35 acts of destruction and insulting places of worship, 18 cases of destruction and insulting of religious symbols and objects of religious worship, 3 events with the features of limiting public confession of faith, 3 cases of discrimination on the basis of religious beliefs and 8 broadly understood events characterized by incitement to hatred on the basis of religious differences or insulting a group of the population or a person because of their religious affiliation.

2. The most numerous group of violations of the right to freedom of religion are acts of vandalism against places of worship (sanctuaries, churches, roadside shrines and crosses). The scale of forms of repression in this category is very diverse, including: arson attempts, devastation, partial destruction of property, placing offensive inscriptions or satanic symbols, burglaries and thefts.

3. 88,88% of the recorded violations of the right to freedom of religion in Poland in 2019 concern acts against the Catholic faith (64 cases). Christians of other denominations were also victims of repression because of their faith: in 2 cases - the Evangelical-Methodist Church and in 1 case - the Evangelical-Augsburg Church. In 5 cases, the violations concerned a value common to all Christians, i.e. insulting a cross or a cemetery, or a reaction to the invocation of biblical teaching.

4. In 47.3% of the violations, the perpetrators of the incidents were men, women constituted 27.5% of the offenders, while in 25.2%, the gender of the perpetrator was not determined on the basis of police reports and media publications.

5. Incidents reported to the Police or the Prosecutor's Office accounted for 69.45% of all repressions. In 30.44%, the violation acts were not reported to the Police or the Prosecutor's Office (5.55%), or no information was provided on the state of the proceedings (25%).
