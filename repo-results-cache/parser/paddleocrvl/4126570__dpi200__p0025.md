## Ingredients for 1 GN 1/1 tray

Eggs

Milk

▶ Cream

▶ Sugar

180 g / 6,3 oz

270 ml / 9,5 oz

270 ml / 9,5 oz

180 g / 6,3 oz

## Procedure:

Mix all the ingredients in the BE5 planetary mixer

▶ Add the cream in the molds

Put the molds in the perforated tray

Add 6 lt / 1,6 gal of water, preheat at  $ 180^{\circ} $C /  $ 356^{\circ} $F in braising mode, put the perforated tray with the molds on the false bottoms and suspended frames

TIP: with small quantities of liquid preheat in braising mode to speed up the cooking process

▶ Chill in the SkyLine Chill $ ^{S} $ Blast Chiller
