The frequency of pools found in each catchment range from 3.4 per km in the Add to 8.6 per km in the Feochan Bheag catchment. A total of 174 (49%) pools were identified as being sub-optimal with the remaining 176 (51%) as being optimal. The predominant type of cover available to fish was the depth of water and from canopy cover from trees. The area of pool habitat potentially available ranged from 714m² in the Abhainn na Cille to 94,220m² in the Add catchment. Loch Nell (1.23km²) and Loch Scammadale (0.96km²) provide large holding areas for adult and juvenile fish, however were not included in the above analysis to avoid disorting the results. A total of 179 (51%) pools were identified as optimal fish holding pools, with 174 (40%) of pools assessed as sub-optimal (see table 2.7 for a definition of optimal and sub-optimal holding pools).
