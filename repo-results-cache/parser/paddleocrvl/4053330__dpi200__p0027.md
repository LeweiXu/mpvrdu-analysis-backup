<div style="text-align: center;">TITLE: Hospitalization Dates Related to Current Services</div>


INSTRUCTIONS: Enter the inpatient 6-digit (MM | DD | YY) or 8-digit (MM | DD | YYYY) hospital admission date followed by the discharge date (if discharge has occurred). If not discharged, leave discharge date blank. This date is when a medical service is furnished as a result of, or subsequent to, a related hospitalization.

DESCRIPTION: The “Hospitalization Dates Related to Current Services” refers to an inpatient stay and indicates the admission and discharge dates associated with the service(s) on the claim.

FIELD SPECIFICATION: This field allows for the entry of the following in each of the date fields: 2 digits under MM, 2 digits under DD, and 4 digits under YY.

## EXAMPLE:

18. HOSPITALIZATION DATES RELATED TO CURRENT SERVICES

MM DD YY MM DD YY

FROM 09 25 2005 TO 09 28 2005
