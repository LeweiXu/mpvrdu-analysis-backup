## Mechanical steel jacks

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_156_1373_627_1864.jpg" alt="Image" width="28%" /></div>


<div style="text-align: center;">Model: DR-10, DR-16</div>


Designed for lifting loads during installation and gauging of heavy equipment, machinery, maintenance and repair of vehicles, etc. Mechanical principle of operation and the ratchet provide full autonomy and safety at work, fixing of weight during and after lifting. The weight can be lifted by upper support having a ribbed surface, or by low pickup, allowing lifting weight, even if only a small space is available. Ratchet gears are protected from dirt and water by steel casing.
