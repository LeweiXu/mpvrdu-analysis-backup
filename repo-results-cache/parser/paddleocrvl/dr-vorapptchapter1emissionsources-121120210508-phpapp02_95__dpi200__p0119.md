CTR Performance in the Short and Long Tail

Yet according to the CONTEXTWEB study, digital marketers continue to overspend on the more costly short-tail without getting a significant boost in click-through rates to justify the outlay. A couple of telling statistics from comScore cited by CONTEXTWEB: while 70% of reportable display spending goes to short-tail websites, Internet users age 18 and over spend just 14% of their time online there.
