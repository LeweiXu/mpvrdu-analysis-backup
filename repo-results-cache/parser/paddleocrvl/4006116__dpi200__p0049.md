## CHAPTER 8 Comparing the Two Kingdoms

Before we move on it would be helpful to summarise the two separate and distinct kingdoms that currently exist in the world; God's eternal kingdom and Satan's kingdom, that were both presented to Adam and Eve in the Garden of Eden. If we think for a moment what defines a kingdom, there are three attributes that we need to consider:

1. GOVERNMENT: A system by which the kingdom is ruled. For example, democracy or dictatorship.

2. CURRENCY: A value system by which the citizens of a kingdom can exchange their goods.

3. CITIZENSHIP: A way of determining how someone can be a member of that kingdom.
