Species with high loadings on the first component are common in the forest interior, whereas species with high loadings on the second component are abundant at the forest edge. The two interior low disturbance plots (LD 1.8 and LD 3.0) tended to cluster together, as did the two interior high disturbance plots (HD 1.8 and HD 3.0), indicating that the more disturbed interior plots tended to have more early successional species than the corresponding low disturbance plots (Figure 5).
