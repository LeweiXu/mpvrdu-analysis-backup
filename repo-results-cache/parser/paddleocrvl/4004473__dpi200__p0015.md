## FUTURE FOCUSED

For more than 100 years Cummins has been at the forefront of new ideas. Here’s a brief look at the company’s remarkable history.

## 1937 

Cummins earns its first profit.

## 1934 

J. Irwin Miller, great-nephew of W.G. Irwin, becomes general manager of Cummins at the age of 24.

1910

Miller becomes Chairman of the Cummins Board.

## 1951 

1920

Clessie Cummins creates the Cummins Engine Company based in Columbus, Indiana (U.S.). William G. Irwin, who employed Cummins as a driver, supplies nearly all of the $50,000 in startup capital.
