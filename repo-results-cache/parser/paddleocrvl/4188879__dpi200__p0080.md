Performance of DRR Dhan 48 in Farmer's field:

Pure seed of DRR Dhan 48 multiplied during Rabi 2020 from 'PRT2' was grown in farmer's field of Shri. Sangi Nagaiah/Tummala Muralidhar of Aroor village, Valigonda mandal, Yadadri Bhuvanagiri District of Telangana, during Kharif 2020 in four acres. Breeders carried out regular field visits and undertook rouging to maintain the seed purity and explained the importance of carrying out rouging in seed production plots to the farmers (Figure 3).
