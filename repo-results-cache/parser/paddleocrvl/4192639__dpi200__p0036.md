# MUHAMMAD

Forty Introductions

## MICHAEL MUHAMMAD KNIGHT

History / Islam • Trade Paperback • 9781593761479 $16.95 • 192 pages • 5.5" x 8.3" • Ebook available

Available July 2018

Muhammad: Forty Introductions offers a distinct take on the life and teachings of Muhammad the prophet, using a forty-hadith collection.

Hadiths are the reported sayings and actions of Muhammad. Here, Michael Muhammad Knight contributes his own forty-hadith collection as a convert ex-punk-rocker-turned-academic who understands the religion for its heterogeneity and changeability. He explores visual portrayals of Muhammad through history; Muhammad's views on homosexuality and the treatment of animals; the evolving definition of jihad; and Muhammad's family's history as migrants and refugees.

# BLUE-EYED DEVIL A Road Odyssey Through Islamic America

## MICHAEL MUHAMMAD KNIGHT

“In the end you realize Knight has grown dramatically during his odyssey, and this is what makes this tale so fascinating. Rarely does a reader get to share an author’s actual personal transformation page by page—it is an intimate experience.”

In Blue-Eyed Devil, Michael Muhammad Knight embarks on a quest for an indigenous American Islam. Traveling 20,000 miles by Greyhound bus in sixty days, he squats in run-down mosques, pursues Muslim romance, is detained at the U.S.-Canadian border with a trunkload of Shia literature, crashes Islamic Society of North America conventions, stink-palms Cat Stevens, and limps across Chicago to find the grave of Noble Drew Ali, filling dozens of notebooks along the way.
