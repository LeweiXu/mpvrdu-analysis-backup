# RED THREAD ZEN Humanly Entangled in Emptiness

SUSAN MURPHY

Philosophy • Trade Paperback • 9781619027831 $16.95 • 240 pages • 5" x 7" • Ebook available

Love, attachment, the passions, gender, carnality, birth, bodily being, mortality, belonging, suffering, hope, despair, personhood, imagination, vitality, the struggle to be fully human—how do these things dwell wholly in emptiness? How do we reconcile their vivid life with “nothingness”? Red Thread Zen explores every corner of the magnificent koan of being “still attached to the red thread,” or “line of tears.” Here, Susan Murphy argues against the bloodless and socially disengaged form of “Buddhism” that is generally being gestated in the West, one that shades too readily into the blandest of bland self-help.
