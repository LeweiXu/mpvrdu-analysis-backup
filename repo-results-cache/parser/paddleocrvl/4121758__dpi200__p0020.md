In terms of market share, NICO General dominated the general insurance market in 2009 with reported gross premium of MK3.0 billion or 37.6 percent of the total market gross premium income. Charter had the least share of the market at MK130.2 million or 1.6 percent (Chart 4).

##### 6.8.2.2 Reinsurance Cessions

The retention level by general insurers for business underwritten decreased slightly over the year to 71.9 percent compared to 73.1 percent the previous year (See Appendices, Table 4). However the retention in terms of absolute figures was greater in 2009 at MK5.7 billion than MK4.6 billion recorded in 2008.

Reinsurance cession for fire and motor classes increased by 39.0 percent and 45.3 percent to MK869.0 million and MK406.6 million respectively over the year. Fire remains the heaviest reinsured single class for the general insurance industry, accounting for more than 39.1 percent of the overall reinsurance cost incurred by the industry. For the last four years, motor insurance has had the highest retention of all classes, averaging 92.1 percent.

Out of the MK2.2 billion ceded to reinsurers, MK754.2 million (34.3 percent) was ceded locally to MalawiRe while MK126.3 million (5.7 percent) was shared among local general insurers. This means that the remaining 60.0 percent went to foreign reinsurers, who also got MK209.4 million or 27.8 percent of MalawiRe's premium income through retrocession arrangements.
