# SHOES OUTSIDE THE DOOR

Desire, Devotion, and Excess at San Francisco Zen Center

MICHAEL DOWNING

Zen • Trade Paperback • 9781582432540 $18.95 • 416 pages • 6" x 9"

“Why did the richest, most influential, highest-flying Zen center in America crash and burn in 1983? Novelist Michael Downing wondered the same thing, and after three years of interviewing members and poring over documents, his Shoes Outside the Door tells the story. Womanizing, BMW-driving Richard Baker was the abbot and visionary behind the rapid growth of the San Francisco Zen Center... Working with direct quotations from students and workers of the Center and its many enterprises, Downing delivers a page-turning exposé of a community that is as laudable as it is laughable.”

—Brian Bruya, associate professor, philosophy, Eastern Michigan University and Center Associate at the University of Michigan’s Center for Chinese Studies

# TRACKING BODHIDHARMA A Journey to the Heart of Chinese Culture

ANDY FERGUSON

Zen • Trade Paperback • 9781619021594 $17.95 • 288 pages • 6" x 9" • Ebook available

In Tracking Bodhidharma, Andrew Ferguson re-creates the path of Bodhidharma, traveling through China to the places where the founder of Zen Buddhism lived and taught, where he explores the origins of Chan [Zen] Buddhism, Bodhidharma's cultural aftermath, and the stories of a man who shaped a civilization.

Tracking Bodhidharma offers a previously unheard perspective on the life of Zen's most important religious leader, while showing how that history is relevant to the rapidly developing superpower of present-day China. By placing Zen Buddhism within the country's political landscape, Ferguson presents the religion as a catalyst for some of China's most revolutionary moments, as well as the ancient spiritual core of the country.
