## GROUP 1: LANGUAGE A

IB English Literature A: This course is designed to focus exclusively on literary texts, adopting a variety of approaches to textual criticism. Students explore the nature of literature, the aesthetic function of literary language and textuality, and the relationship between literature and the world.

The course is organized into three areas of exploration and seven central concepts, and focuses on the study of literary works. Students learn to appreciate the artistry of literature, and develop the ability to reflect critically on their reading, presenting literary analysis powerfully through both oral and written communication. Students will be able to develop critical thinking skills, a personal appreciation of language and literature, and an understanding of how language challenges and sustains ways of thinking.
