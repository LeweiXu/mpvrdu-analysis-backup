Compared to 2017, the proportion of non-human primates coming from self-sustaining colonies decreased slightly (-1%). However, in line with the Directive objectives, the proportion of those being second or higher generation purpose-bred continued to increase (+3%) and those being of first-generation purpose-bred decreased (-2%). This is confirmed over the past four years of reporting (Figure 11).
