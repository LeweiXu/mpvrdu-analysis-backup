Conversely, and more commonly, we have a whole multitude that sees the impossibility of performing the requirements of the law, but rather than entering in by a faith relationship, they pronounce the keeping of God's commandments impossible and therefore never enjoy the freedom of victory in Christ. Whether you seek to perform or seek not to perform, the issue is still performance rather than relationship. Neither of these groups will enter the kingdom of heaven unless they embrace the Ten Commandments in the context of a faith based relationship with Him who died for us.

For the group of Christians who adopt the anti-performance stance and the impossibility of victory in the Christian walk, it soon follows that the god they serve is also unable to perform. Combine this trend with the worldly desire for recognition and it is no surprise to find Christian scholars, teachers, and believers who reject the ability of God to create the world in six literal days. Just as the lawyer gives the answer that he is to love his neighbour but then questions, “Who is my neighbour?” so many scholars today say, “Yes we believe in a six day creation, but what sort of days are they?” Wickedness always seeks to find a way to twist the Scriptures to suit itself; to believe in Christ and yet live according to the world. The demons also believe in Christ and live according to this world.

Once a person has lost the belief in a God who can create a new heart and has learned to ask cunning questions about the plain statements of Scripture, then it is very easy to embrace and accept homosexuality as a Christian norm along with the rejection of male and female roles, in the home and the church, that the Bible clearly lays out before us. This concept is foreign to the kingdom of heaven. Value is always by relationship not by position.

We could list teaching after teaching in the Bible that has been twisted and turned to suit the principles of power, position, and performance, but I think the point has been clearly made that if we claim to be followers of Jesus then we will seek to interpret the Scripture according to the principles of His kingdom rather than the kingdom from which we have all come.
