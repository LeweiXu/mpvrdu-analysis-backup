b) Developments should configure and design buildings and parking areas to enhance the pedestrian and visual environment along SR 410. Specifically, no more than 50% of the street frontage may be occupied by surface parking lots and vehicle access areas (see Figure 14 for example) Parking lots shall not be located adjacent to street corners. The Director may grant flexibility to these requirements provided the applicant can successfully demonstrate that the proposed configuration meets the intent of the standards, the goals and objectives of the downtown plan. For example, a configuration including a higher percentage of parking lots adjacent to the roadway may allow for a more desirable east-west pedestrian corridor with continuous storefronts in the Central Triangle. In such a scenario, the developer would be expected to provide additional design treatments along SR 410 to mitigate negative impacts of the parking lots on the visual environment.
