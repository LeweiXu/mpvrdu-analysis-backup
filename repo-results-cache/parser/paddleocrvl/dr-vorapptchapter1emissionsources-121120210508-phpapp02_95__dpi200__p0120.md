## Long Tail Advertising – CONTEXTWEB Study

All advertiser verticals saw increased CTRs in the long tail as compared to the short tail. Advertisers in the "Alcohol" segment, for example, saw a 50% lift. The lowest long-tail lift observed was 12% for auto advertisers.
