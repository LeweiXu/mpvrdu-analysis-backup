## KILLIAN JAMIESON DIVERTICULUM

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_143_338_943_1458.jpg" alt="Image" width="37%" /></div>


• Killian-Jamieson diverticulum is a pulsion diverticulum, that protrudes through a lateral anatomic weak site of the cervical esophagus below the cricopharyngeus muscle. AP view shows diverticulum (arrow) originating laterally. Lateral view confirms diverticulum does not originate posteriorly as a Zenkers diverticulum would.
