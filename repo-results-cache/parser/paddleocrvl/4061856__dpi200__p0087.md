## Using Creativity for Tai Chi Easy $ ^{™} $ Modifications

While traditional Tai Chi concentrates on performing the movements correctly and doing them in the proper sequence, Tai Chi for health promotion emphasizes simple practices that can be adapted by a wide array of populations. The goal is to be in a state of restful attention, while moving the body gently, deepening the breath and clearing the mind. So, the kind of tension that could be produced by needing to get it right, is replaced with an openness to doing it almost any way that it comes out, thus mobilizing relaxation and inner healing.

As a Tai Chi Easy $ ^{™} $ Practice Leader, be creative, change the order, add parts together in new ways. The keys are:

• Do the practices as best you can.

• Adapt, modify, and tailor the practices for your needs.

• Use the Three Intentful Corrections.

• Stay in your comfort zone.
