## Ingredients for 1 GN 1/1 tray

▶ Cream cheese

Mascarpone cheese

Biscuit

▶ Sugar

Butter

2,5 kg / 5,5 lbs

▶ Whole eggs

Vanilla

Water

1,25 kg / 2,75 lbs

1 kg / 2.2 lbs

1.8 kg / 3.9 lbs

300 g / 10,5 oz

20 pcs / 20 pcs

4 pcs

5 It / 1,32 gal

## Procedure:

▶ With TRK cutter mixer grind butter and biscuits

Put the biscuits on the bottom of the mold (5 mm / 0,2 in thick layer) and press

In the planetary mixer mix the yolk with the sugar



▶ Add cheese, cream and vanilla

▶ Pour the mixture over the biscuit base

Place false bottoms into the pan

▶ Pour water and preheat at  $ 200^{\circ} $C /  $ 392^{\circ} $F in braising mode till boils

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_654_1069_694_1112.jpg" alt="Image" width="2%" /></div>


TIP: with small quantities of water preheat in braising mode to speed up the cooking process

TIP: quantity of water is the same either you cook 1 or 4 trays



<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_655_1171_695_1213.jpg" alt="Image" width="2%" /></div>


Place the trays with molds on the false bottoms and suspended frames (2 trays on the bottom and 2 trays on top on suspended frames for 90 It / 24 gal pan, and 3 trays on the bottom and 3 trays on top on suspended frames for 170 It / 45 gal pan)



When water boils, close the lid and cook under pressure ☐ for 8 min

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_1140_994_1180_1036.jpg" alt="Image" width="2%" /></div>


TIP: to ensure the best performance wait that water boils before starting the pressure phase

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_1139_1110_1180_1151.jpg" alt="Image" width="2%" /></div>


TIP: to reduce the quantity of excess steam released, wait till steam condensation process completes before unlocking and opening the lid fully
