## SCHEDULE A: RECEIPTS

M.G.L. c. 55 requires that the name and residential address be reported, in alphabetical order, for all receipts over $50 in a calendar year. Committees must keep detailed accounts and records of all receipts, but need itemize only those over $50. In addition, Section 3.1.7 of the Town By-Laws further requires that the occupation and employer must be reported for each person who contributes more than $50 in a calendar year. Receipts of $50 or less may be added together, from committee records, and reported on line 10 rather than line 9.

This page may be copied if additional pages are required to report all receipts. If you do so, include your committee name and a page number on each additional page.
