## Reports and Context

It is important that we begin to look at digital data in a 360 degree context of the different channels & tactics.

For example, looking at a paid search report alone and only looking at reach (impressions) and initial response (CPC / CPIR) data is very misleading.

• 44% of all people who click on paid search results have been exposed to display advertising prior to the click. Therefore it is wrong to attribute 100% performance to paid search or the ‘last click’.

You need to look at paid and organic search data together and with broader engagement/performance metrics.

More broadly you need to regularly look at data in overall context of a campaign and or program and this also requires you to develop an overall digital and or broader marketing scorecard for your organisation.
