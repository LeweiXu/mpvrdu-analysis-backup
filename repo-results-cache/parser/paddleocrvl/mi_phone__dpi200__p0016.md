## GEEKS IN THE CITY

Geeks in the City calls forth those who have what some people call “geek interests” but what we personally think of as “awesome interests.” This community is for people who are passionate about everything from TV shows about Time Lords and Targaryens to books and movies set in sci-fi and fantasy realms to everything that art and entertainment has to offer. NYC is a hub of activity for this subculture. Past adventures include attending midnight screenings and premieres, hosting TV marathons and book clubs, video game nights, Dungeons & Dragons game night, zombie laser tag trips to comic book stores, and more. Geeks in the City, assemble!
