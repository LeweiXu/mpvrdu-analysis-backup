## Procedure:

Preheat  $ 180^{\circ} $C /  $ 356^{\circ} $F in braising mode for 5 minutes

Stir fry half of the pork belly for 5 minutes and repeat with the rest of the pork belly

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_657_1009_697_1051.jpg" alt="Image" width="2%" /></div>


TIP: to sear properly the full load quantity, split it in 2 parts for the braising phase

Put all pork belly in the brasing pan and add the spices, wine and vegetables

▶ When boils, close the lid and cook under pressure for 30 minutes

TIP: to ensure the best performance wait that sauce boils before starting the pressure phase





<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_1141_908_1182_953.jpg" alt="Image" width="2%" /></div>


Remove the meat, and whisk the vegetables and sauce with the Bermixer Pro
