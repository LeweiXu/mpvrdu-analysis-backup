## 323 heat-associated deaths in 2020

83% (271) of heat-associated injuries occurred outdoors.

85% of outdoor heat-associated injuries occurred among males. 15% of outdoor heat-associated injuries occurred among females.



63% of male heat-associated deaths occurred in an Urban Area.
