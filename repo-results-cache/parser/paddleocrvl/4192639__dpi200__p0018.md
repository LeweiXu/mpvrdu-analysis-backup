# THE PLATFORM SUTRA The Zen Teaching of Hui-neng

TRANSLATED BY RED PINE

Zen Buddhism • Trade Paperback • 9781593761776 $18.95 • 352 pages • 5.5" x 8.3" • Ebook available

The Platform Sutra occupies a central place in Zen (Ch'an) Buddhist instruction for students and spiritual seekers worldwide. However, unlike the other sutras, which transcribe the teachings of the Buddha himself, The Platform Sutra presents the life and work of Hui-neng, the controversial sixth patriarch of Zen, and his understanding of the fundamentals of a spiritual and practical life. Red Pine (Bill Porter) offers a sensitive, straightforward, and solid translation of this vital Buddhist text. His remarkable insight and commentary provide a guiding hand for seekers and students alike in their quest for dharma.
