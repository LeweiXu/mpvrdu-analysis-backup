## ZENKERS DIVERTICULUM

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_50_243_965_1519.jpg" alt="Image" width="42%" /></div>


• A Zenker's diverticulum is a pulsion hypopharyngeal false diverticulum with only mucosa and submucosa protruding through triangular posterior wall weak site (Killian's dehiscence) between horizontal and oblique components of cricopharyngeal muscle.

- The esophagram shows collection with midline posterior origin just above cricopharyngeus protruding lateral, usually to left, and caudal with enlargement.
