Red lentils

Water

Onion (chopped)

Carrot (chopped)

Cloves garlic

## Procedure:

Preheat at  $ 150\ °C $ in braising mode for 5 minutes

Roast vegetables for 4 minutes

▶ Add lentils, water, spices and switch in boiling mode

▶ Cook under pressure for 30 minutes

For a smooth soup, use Bermixer PRO
