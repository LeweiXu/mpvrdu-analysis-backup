### II. PROVIDING BANKRUPTCY'S "FRESH START" WITH NON-BANKRUPTCY LAW

The best evidence that the law may provide effective debt relief without a bankruptcy discharge is that most insolvent consumers do not file for bankruptcy. $ ^{30} $ Of course, in some cases the creditor may abandon collection efforts because he believes that the consumer will file for bankruptcy if pressed, $ ^{31} $ and many insolvent consumers actually do choose bankruptcy. Therefore, it is useful to describe the debt relief that bankruptcy offers and demonstrate how non-bankruptcy law could (and to some extent does) provide similar relief.

### A. The Protections Afforded by Bankruptcy
