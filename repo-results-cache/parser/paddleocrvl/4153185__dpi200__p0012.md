## Progress since ICI's CLMRS Effectiveness Review Phase 1

The report from phase 1 of ICI's CLMRS Effectiveness Review, Effectiveness Review of CLMRS in the Smallholder Agricultural Sector of Sub-Saharan Africa, 2017, features recommendations to implementers of CLMRS, general recommendations for the cocoa sector and a set of questions to be answered during the second phase of the effectiveness review. Table 1 recalls each of these recommendations, takes stock of what has been partly or fully accomplished and notes where further progress is needed.

Table 1: Summary of progress since the 2017 Effectiveness Review of CLMRS in the Smallholder Agricultural Sector of Sub-Saharan Africa.
