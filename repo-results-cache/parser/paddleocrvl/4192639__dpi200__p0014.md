One of the greatest religious practitioners and philosophers, Eihei Dōgen Zenji is considered the founder of the Sōto school of Zen. A deep thinker and writer, he produced what is now thought to be one of the highest manifestations of Buddhist philosophy. Dōgen's Genjo Koan is the first chapter in that book, and for many followers it is thought of as one of the groundwork texts of Zen Buddhism.

This unique edition of Dōgen's Genjo Koan contains three separate translations, as well as several commentaries by a variety of Zen masters. Nishiari Bokusan, Shohaku Okamura, Shunryu Suzuki, Kosho Uchiyama, Sojun Mel Weitsman, Kazuaki Tanahashi, and Dairyu Michael Wenger all have contributed to this remarkable book.
