## MATERIALS USED

Formwork are mainly of two types

- Steel formwork

- Wooden formwork

Steel formwork is made of

- Steel sheets

- Angle Iron

- Tee Iron

Wooden formwork consists of

- Props

- Planks battens

- Ledgers

- Sheeting
