## Salmon Markets

### 6.3 Top 10 markets by size (2020E)

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_chart_box_203_326_1461_1005.jpg" alt="Image" width="76%" /></div>


In the 10 largest markets by country, consumption per capita varies from 0.06 kg WFE (China / Hong Kong) to 3.24 kg WFE (France). In Norway, Sweden and Finland, consumption per capita is between 6-8 kg WFE. This means that there is significant growth potential among the largest markets.
