There is a significant disparity in literacy rates between rural and urban areas. In 1998, only 42.5 percent of the population in rural areas were literate, as against 64.2 percent in urban areas. The disparity was particularly large for women. In rural areas only 30.3 percent of women were literate, while in urban areas the literacy rate for women was 57.9 percent. Rural literacy rates have improved over the years, however. Between 1981 and 1998, the literacy rate of rural women increased from 14.1 percent to 30.3 percent.
