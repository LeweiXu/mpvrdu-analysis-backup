### 20. Critical accounting assumptions

The following critical accounting assumptions have been applied by management in the prior year accounting for the restructure. For further detail on the restructure refer to the directors' report.

• Dividend-in-specie from FirstRand: FirstRand issued a dividend in specie, in the form of MMI Limited (“MMI”), on which it recognised a gain as required by IFRIC 17 Distributions of non-cash assets to owners. This was treated as an upstream transaction and, therefore, the gain has been excluded when accounting for the group’s equity accounted share of FirstRand’s earnings. The gain was, however, realised in the group’s own IFRIC 17 revaluation of RMI when unbundling the insurance assets to its shareholders. In addition, the portion of MMI attributable to Metropolitan was recognised as negative goodwill on initial recognition of the new MMI associate on the basis that it was not previously included in the FirstRand carrying value.

- Date of the restructuring transaction: The unbundling was approved by the shareholders of RMBH on 18 February 2011 and RMI was listed on 7 March 2011. The accounting standards allow an entity to designate the transaction date as a month end where the transaction takes place during a month. On that basis, 28 February 2011 has been used by management as the effective date of the unbundling.

- Treatment of OUTsurance: OUTsurance was previously consolidated by the group based on de facto control which considered the 45% direct holding and the additional indirect shareholding through FirstRand, resulting in an effective shareholding of 61.7% as at June 2010. Following the unbundling of RMI, the group ceased to consolidate OUTsurance on the assumption that it no longer had de facto control as it had passed to RMI. FirstRand ceased to equity account for its share of OUTsurance as from December 2010 based on the agreement to sell its shareholding to RMI.
