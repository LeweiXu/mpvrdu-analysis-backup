To ensure IoU of generated box and gt box is larger than min_overlap:

 $$ \begin{aligned}\frac{w*h}{(w+2*r)*(h+2*r)}\geq iou\quad\Rightarrow\quad4*iou*r^{2}+2*iou*(w+h)r+(iou-1)*w*h\leq0\\a=4*iou,\quad b=2*iou*(w+h),\quad c=(iou-1)*w*h\\r\leq\frac{-b+\sqrt{b^{2}-4*a*c}}{2*a}\end{aligned} $$ 

## Parameters

• det_size (list[int]) – Shape of object.

• min_overlap(float) – Min IoU with ground truth for boxes generated by keypoints inside the gaussian kernel.

Returns Radius of gaussian kernel.

Return type radius (int)
