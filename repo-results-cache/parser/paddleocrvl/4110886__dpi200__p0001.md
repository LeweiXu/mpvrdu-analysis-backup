## An Exploratory Study of COVID-19 in Brampton’s High Priority Community

This report's research was conducted over twelve days in January 2021 by the dedicated research team at The Social Planning Council of Peel (SPCP). Under the supervision of Principal Investigator, Dr. Srimanta Mohanty, the concerted effort of many, including practicum students from Ryerson University (RyeU), Sheridan College Institute of Technology and Advanced Learning, University of Guelph-Humber (UofGH), University of Toronto Mississauga (UTM), and York University (YU) has contributed to this comprehensive data collection and analysis.
