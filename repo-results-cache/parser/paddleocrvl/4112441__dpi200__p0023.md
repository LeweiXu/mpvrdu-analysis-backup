## Double vector bundles - compatibility conditions

The condition that each vector bundle operation in D is a morphism with respect to the other is equivalent to the following conditions, known as the interchange laws:

 $$ \begin{align*}\left(d_{1}\ +_{B}\ d_{2}\right)\ +_{A}\ \left(d_{3}\ +_{B}\ d_{4}\right)\ &=\ \left(d_{1}\ +_{A}\ d_{3}\right)\ +_{B}\ \left(d_{2}\ +_{A}\ d_{4}\right),\\t\cdot_{A}\left(d_{1}\ +_{B}\ d_{2}\right)\ &=\ t\cdot_{A}d_{1}\ +_{B}t\cdot_{A}d_{2},\\t\cdot_{B}\left(d_{1}\ +_{A}\ d_{2}\right)\ &=\ t\cdot_{B}d_{1}\ +_{A}t\cdot_{B}d_{2},\\t\cdot_{A}\left(s\cdot_{B}d\right)\ &=\ s\cdot_{B}\left(t\cdot_{A}d\right),\\\tilde{0}_{a_{1}+a_{2}}^{A}\ &=\ \tilde{0}_{a_{1}}^{A}\ +_{B}\ \tilde{0}_{a_{2}}^{A},\\\tilde{0}_{ta}^{A}\ &=\ t\cdot_{B}\tilde{0}_{a}^{A},\\\tilde{0}_{b_{1}+b_{2}}^{B}\ &=\ \tilde{0}_{b_{1}}^{B}\ +_{A}\ \tilde{0}_{b_{2}}^{A},\\\tilde{0}_{tb}^{B}\ &=\ t\cdot_{A}\ \tilde{0}_{b}^{B}.\end{align*} $$
