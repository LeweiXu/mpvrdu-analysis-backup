# CORRESPONDENCE.

To the Editor of the 'Visor,'

Dear Sir,

Having now experienced a season at Club Rugby, I see how much many fellows have benefitted by securing their place in their school XV. Whilst playing against the majority of the local Clubs, I have noticed that a large number of players have learned the game at the 'B.I.'

Only a few seasons ago I hear we had a club in the School, and possibly it fell through because of the lack of interest taken in it.

I agree that the Association Football is a very fine game, but it does not appeal to all of us, and it should be possible to form a Club out of the Rugger enthusiasts now attending the School. I hope that by next season the 'B.I.' will be placing a fifteen in the field against the rival schools and that in future years we shall play not one game, but both.

I am,

Yours faithfully,

W.G.V. LV Ic.

(Bidston R.F.C.)
