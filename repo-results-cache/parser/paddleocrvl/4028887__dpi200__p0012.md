Concentration of a large proportion of health care spending on a small group of patients is not unique to Ontario. Research from Manitoba, British Columbia, and the United States confirms that in every health care system, health care resources are skewed toward a small share of the population. $ ^{1} $

## WHO ARE THE HIGH USERS OF HEALTH CARE?

“High users of health care are typically patients at the end of their lives, individuals with chronic diseases or multiple chronic diseases, and infants with high health care needs. The 60 and over age group accounts for the largest proportion of high-cost health care users. High-cost users are also more likely to come from disadvantaged groups in the population and thus more likely to be poor, unemployed, and socially isolated.” $ ^{1} $
