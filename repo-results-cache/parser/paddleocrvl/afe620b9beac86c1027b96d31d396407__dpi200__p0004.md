## CONSERVATION OF ENERGY

During the year under report, the measures initiated/implemented by the Company for conservation of energy included the following main items:

1. Reduced the loading and unloading pressure setting of air compressor up to optimum limits.

2. Enthalpy Control System installed for central air conditioning.

3. Automatic control of cooling tower fan.

4. Switching off the air washer pumps of Air Handling Unit and Humidifier & Ventilation plants by continuously maintaining humidity.

5. Rationalised the use of airconditioning system in the factory in a manner to reduce the total tonnage of the plant without curtailing its use or availability.

6. Individual lighting switches were provided for better control of lighting on/off at main factory office.

These measures have resulted in saving in electricity consumption by 130 units per million cigarettes produced compared to 2001-02.
