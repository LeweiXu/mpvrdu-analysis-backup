2.3.2 Private open space for Townhouses. Townhouses and other ground based multi-family residential units with individual exterior entries must provide at least 200 square feet of private open space per dwelling unit adjacent to, and directly accessible from each dwelling unit. This may include private balconies, individual rear yards, landscaped front yards, and covered front porch areas. Exception: “Common Open Space” designed per Standard 2.3.1 may substitute for up to 50% of each unit’s required private or semi-private open space on a square foot per square foot basis.
