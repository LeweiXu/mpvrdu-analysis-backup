## Mowi

Mowi is the world’s largest producer of farm-raised salmon measured by both volume and turnover. We offer seafood products to approximately 70 countries, are represented in 25 countries and employ 12 200 people. Mowi is organised into three business areas: Feed, Farming and Sales & Marketing.

Total revenue for Mowi in 2020 was MEUR 3,760 and the harvest quantity of Atlantic salmon was 439,829 tonnes (GWT), which was 18% of total industry output.
