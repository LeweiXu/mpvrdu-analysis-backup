## Age Distribution of our Sponsored Students

The increase of enrolment among a younger student population continues. There is a substantial increase in our under-20 age group, with a change that represents over 10% of our student body in 2014-15. Students aged 30-34 decreased by 15%, due to this segment's completion of program study and entrance into the workforce.
