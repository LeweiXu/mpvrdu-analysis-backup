#### 4.2.2 Tissue Valve Leaflet Dynamics

Similar methodology used to process rigid wall tissue valve model can be applied for the flexible walls in order to analyze the leaflet dynamic. Maximum leaflet displacement relative to the center of the domain is shown in Figure 4.9. The maximum displacement of the leaflet is found at 0.2 s with a displaced magnitude of 9 mm away from center. The duration for leaflets to reach the peak opening is approximately 50 ms. The leaflets remain open for approximately 100 ms until 0.3 s with an approximate average displacement of 5 mm. In addition, the closure of the leaflet takes about 50 ms before the end of the systolic period at 0.35 s. Nevertheless, the overall leaflet dynamics profile also has oscillatory behavior if compared with the rigid wall model.
