## Ingredients for

## 90 lt/24 gal pan

Fresh minced beef meat

Fresh minced pork meat

▶ Fresh minced sausage 7.2 kg / 16 lbs

▶ Peel tomatoes 14.5 kg / 32 lbs



Extra olive oil

▶ Mirepoix of onions, 4,5 kg / 10 lbs carrots and celery

▶ White wine

14,5 kg / 32 lbs

29 kg / 64 lbs

290 gr / 10,2 lbs

Salt, Pepper, fresh sauce and rosmary

1,5 It / 0,3 gal

## Procedure:

Preheat at  $ 220^{\circ} $C /  $ 428^{\circ} $F in braising mode

▶ Pour oil, add meat, stir and add white wine

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_654_978_695_1020.jpg" alt="Image" width="2%" /></div>


TIP: to obtain a good searing result we recommend to not exceed the quantity indicated below

When the wine has evaporated, add chopped vegetables and mix

▶ Add the peel tomatoes, salt, pepper and spices, stir all

▶ When sauce boils lock down the lid and cook under pressure ☐ for 30 minutes



<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_1139_931_1179_975.jpg" alt="Image" width="2%" /></div>


TIP: to ensure the best

performance wait that sauce boils

before starting the pressure phase
