### SECTION 9. CONFLICTS

Advanced Consent. BBK has disclosed to CITY, and CITY acknowledges, that BBK advises many of its clients regarding water law issues, such as surface water, ground water, imported water, water transfers, water quality, water rights, and various Delta related matters. BBK has informed and believes that it is not currently representing any other client on water issues adverse to CITY. CITY acknowledges that BBK's current or new clients may ask BBK for advice and representation on matters such as representation concerning groundwater (including the Sustainable Groundwater Management Act (SGMA) issues), groundwater sustainability agencies, transactions, negotiations, representation on CEQA or NEPA issues, administrative hearings before the State Water Resources Control Board or other state or federal agencies, regulatory permitting, disputes, or litigation (“Future Water Matters”), any of which could be adverse to CITY.

As part of this agreement, CITY gives BBK its advanced consent to any future representation of other current or future BBK clients in Future Water Matters. As part of this advance consent, BBK shall promptly inform CITY in writing of any future representation of a client(s) and provide CITY the opportunity to determine if it wishes to replace BBK in those circumstances. If CITY decides to continue to use BBK, then BBK will establish an ethical screen so that the lawyers representing CITY do not share any confidential information of CITY with other lawyers at BBK and vice versa. Regardless of the circumstances, BBK will not disclose any confidential information received from CITY to any other client of BBK.
