## • What is Local Body Tax?

Local Body Tax (LBT) means a tax on the entry of goods into the limits of the city, for consumption, use or sale therein, levied in accordance with the provisions of chapter XIB, but does not include cess as defined in clause (6A)

LBT is account based tax and similar to VAT.

All commercial shipments transported to the respective cities must have LBT no of consignee on Invoice.
