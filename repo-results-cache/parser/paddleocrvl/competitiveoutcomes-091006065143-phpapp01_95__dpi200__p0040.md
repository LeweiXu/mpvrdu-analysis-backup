## Bobby Fischer

International chess grand master at 15.

• Started playing at age 6

- At 7, began weekly tutoring by president of Brooklyn chess club

- At 12, joined one of the strongest chess clubs in the world and met several times a week with chess master Jack Collins with access to his library of chess books.
