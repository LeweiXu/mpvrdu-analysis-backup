## EN After Sales Service:

Any maintenance on your equipment should be undertaken by: -Either your dealer,

-Or another qualified mechanic who is an authorized agent for the brand appliances. When making an appointment, state the full reference of your equipment (model, type and serial number). This information appears on the manufacturer's nameplate attached to your equipment.

## INFORMATION

The minimum period of availability of spare parts listed in the European Regulation 2019-2022-EU and accessible in particular to the user of the appliance, is 10 years under the conditions layed down in the same regulation.
