## The structure of double vector bundles

- In the above figure, we refer to A and B as the side bundles of D, and to M as the double base.

In the two side bundles, the addition and scalar multiplication are denoted by the usual symbols + and juxtaposition, respectively.

We distinguish the two zero-sections, writing  $ 0^{A}: M \to A $,  $ m \mapsto 0_{m}^{A} $, and  $ 0^{B}: M \to B $,  $ m \mapsto 0_{m}^{B} $.

In the vertical bundle structure on D with base A, the vector bundle operations are denoted by  $ \pm_{A} $ and  $ \cdot_{A} $, with  $ \tilde{0}^{A}: A \rightarrow D $,  $ a \mapsto \tilde{0}_{a}^{A} $ for the zero-section.

• Similarly, in the horizontal bundle structure on D with base B we write  $ \pm_{B} $ and  $ \cdot_{B} $, with  $ \tilde{0}^{B}:B\to D $,  $ b\mapsto\tilde{0}_{b}^{B} $, for the zero-section.

The two structures on D, namely  $ (D, q_{B}^{D}, B) $ and  $ (D, q_{A}^{D}, A) $ will also be denoted, respectively, by  $ \tilde{D}_{B} $ and  $ \tilde{D}_{A} $, and called the horizontal bundle structure and the vertical bundle structure.
