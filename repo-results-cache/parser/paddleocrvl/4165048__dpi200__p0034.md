Studying the way the practitioner-subgroup accesses information, their behaviour is similar to the whole practitioner group except for a few main differences reported below (Table 4.5). They rank more highly online NGO reports (Rank 3 in this group, Rank 7 in the Practitioners group) but lower Databases (Rank 5 in this group, Rank 2 in the Practitioners group) and Newsletters (Rank 9 in this group, 5 in the Practitioners group). Among non-online sources, the rankings are similar except for the fact they value more meetings abroad with other professionals, which seems a bit odd a result given that their lack of access is very probably due to their lack of resources.
