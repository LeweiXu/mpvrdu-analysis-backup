## Identifying and Avoiding Scams

In this activity, select one participant to read the part of the scammer and another participant to read the part of the potential victim. The presenter may also decide to play one of these roles. Once a scenario is finished, ask the group to respond to the questions that follow.

Caller: Oh, hi Ellen, how lucky that you're home today. I just happen to have the most amazing investment tip—your money is absolutely safe, the return is guaranteed. And I've had great success with the other stocks I've recommended.

Louise: Wait a minute. There's no Ellen here. You must have the wrong number.
