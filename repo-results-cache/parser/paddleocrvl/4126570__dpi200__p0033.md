## Procedure:

Preheat at  $ 180^{\circ} $C /  $ 356^{\circ} $F in braising mode

▶ Add oil and shallow fry the fish for 6 minutes

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_653_977_697_1022.jpg" alt="Image" width="2%" /></div>


TIP: to shallow fry properly the full load quantity, split it in two parts and cook one part after another

Empty the well and drain oil off

▶ Pour 1 lt of oil (you can use part of oil used for shallow frying) and add onions

▶ Brown onions at  $ 160^{\circ} $C /  $ 320^{\circ} $ F for 2 minutes

▶ Add precooked fish and coconut milk



When milk boils close the lid and cook under pressure for 3 minutes

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_1140_947_1180_989.jpg" alt="Image" width="2%" /></div>


TIP: to ensure the best performance wait that milk boils before starting the pressure phase

Once finished, add chopped coriander and serve

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_1141_1131_1180_1171.jpg" alt="Image" width="2%" /></div>


TIP: you might need to reduce the sauce if it is too liquid. In this case, continue cooking with the open lid at  $ 130^{\circ} $C /  $ 266^{\circ} $F in braising mode till needed
