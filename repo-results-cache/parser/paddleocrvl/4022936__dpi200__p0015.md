~80% of oil currently produced in Western Canada passes through Hardisty on its way to market





Key destination is refineries in U.S. Mid West and Gulf Coast (PADDs II & III)

Declines and increasing political risk surrounding the traditional suppliers of heavy crude continues to drive increased demand for Canadian heavy crude

The three main long-haul pipelines shipping crude from Hardisty currently provide  $ \sim $3.6mmbbl/d of capacity

■ Majority of existing production in Western Canada is from the oil sands

Oil sands do not decline like conventional reservoirs, typically producing around nameplate for decades

Future growth is expected to be predominantly oil sands

Oil sands companies have been able to significantly bring down cost structures in past few years

Existing projects have an average break-even of below US$40/bbl WTI

■ Brownfield projects have an average break-even of below US$50/bbl WTI, but most will require additional egress pipelines to provide takeaway
