## DEHYDRATING FILTER

Its function is to eliminate water content of the refrigerant when it leaves the condenser thus preventing the formation of ice in the interior of tubes impeding refrigerant flow. Water may also negatively affect the motor coil by contributing to the formation of acids.

A silica gel filter is used which acts through absorption without changes in its chemical state when water is collected.
