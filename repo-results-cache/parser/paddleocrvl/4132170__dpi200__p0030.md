A comonad is comonoidal if all its transformations are. However, for the comonad  $ (S, \delta, \epsilon) $ it makes no sense to insist that  $ \epsilon $ is comonoidal as the identity functor is not comonoidal (from X with products to X with tensor). However, it does make sense to require  $ \delta $ is comonoidal. Recall first that, with respect to the product, every functor is canonically comonoidal with:
