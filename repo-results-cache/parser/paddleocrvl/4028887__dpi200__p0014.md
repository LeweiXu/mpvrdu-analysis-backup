In assessing the role of food and beverages on the health care cost for Ontario, the focus is often solely on the publicly funded costs. There is significant evidence that diets that are high in sugar, especially in youth, contribute to dental decay and the build-up of plaque. $ ^{2} $ While this report does not investigate that connection, it is important to recognize that consumers pay directly for health care services, such as dental, that are also impacted by diet and nutrition.
