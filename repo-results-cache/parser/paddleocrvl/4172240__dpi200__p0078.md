# October-November-December 2020

Atlantic Energy (ex-LNG Finima, ex-LNH 564). IMO 7702401. Length 287 m, 28,017 t. South Korean flag. Classification society Korean Register of Shipping. Built in 1984 in Malmo (Sweden) by Kockums. Owned by Sinokor Maritime Co Ltd (South Korea). Detained in 2015 in Nantes (France). Sold for demolition in Bangladesh along with her sistership Pacific Energy. 451 US$ per ton.
