Continuous-Feed Method - The continuous-feed method consists of placing calcium hypochlorite granules in the main during construction (optional), completely filling the main to remove all air pockets, flushing the completed main to remove particulates, and filling the main with potable water. The potable water shall be chlorinated so that after a 24 hour holding period in the main there will be a free chlorine residual of not less than 10 mg/L.
