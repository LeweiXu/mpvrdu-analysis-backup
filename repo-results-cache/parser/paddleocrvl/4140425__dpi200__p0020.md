The difficulties associated with the process of investment and construction, undoubtedly exerted a huge influence in cultural and historical context, and in particular prevailing social and political conditions. It was related to the number of built temples and their architecture - in particular the size of the object, its shape and appearance. In this period, it was only possible by the investor winning the church permits: reconstruction, expansion or construction of an additional sacred objects. The function of religion church (as a social activity, charity, etc.) was limited only to the precincts of the existing temples. Prevented from activities outside the walls of the church. Furthermore, despite the period of intense urbanization of the country, the construction of new church buildings in a completely new place was extremely difficult, almost forbidden.
