#### 13.2.1 Low-Pass Filtering

For sea states where the encounter frequency  $ \omega_{e} $ is much higher than the bandwidth  $ \omega_{b} $ of the control system,

 $$ \omega_{b}\ll\omega_{e} $$ 

a LP-filter can be used to filter out the 1st-order wave-induced forces. This is typically the case for large vessels such as oil tankers.

For smaller vessels, a LP filter in cascade with a notch filter is quite common to use.
