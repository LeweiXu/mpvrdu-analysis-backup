## Synopsis

Agravio takes place in Spain and a region referred to as Flanders. $ ^{1} $ The story begins with Leonora de Ribera stalking onto the stage, proclaiming her need for revenge against her lover, Don Juan, who has gone off to Flanders leaving Leonora in a convent hiding her shame from her family.

Leonora is determined to follow Don Juan to Flanders and avenge herself on Don Juan. Her servant, Flora, suggests she go disguised as a man and to that end Flora fetches men's garments which were left at the convent.
