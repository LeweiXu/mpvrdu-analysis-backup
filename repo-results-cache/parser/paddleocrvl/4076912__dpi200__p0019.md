For birds, the Directive distinguishes domestic fowl (83% of birds in 2018) from other birds. The main species reported as “Other birds” (101,034 in 2018 – Table 7) were turkey and the Great Tit (Parus major).

In this context, it is important to note that from 2021 data onward, further species of fish and turkey have been added as separate categories to reduce the use of “other” categories. $ ^{11} $

For amphibians, the Directive distinguishes rana (15% of amphibians in 2018) and xenopus (57% of amphibians in 2018) from other amphibians. The main species reported as “Other amphibians” (7,543 – Table 7) was bufo (toads).
