## COVID Cases and Death Demographics Data as of May 19 (two-day lag)

## Key Takeaways:

• Data is incomplete with 33.9% of cases and 0.7% of deaths still under investigation

• People 40 and above are overrepresented in cases

• Seniors, Men, Asian Americans, and African Americans overrepresented in deaths

• 1,817 out of 1,970 deaths had comorbidities (underlying conditions) (92.2%)
