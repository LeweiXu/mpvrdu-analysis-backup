# Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. How do I know what my property is zoned?

Every property within the Town limits is zoned according to the current, official zoning map. In order to know what zoning district applies to your property, please download the current, official zoning map. The zoning map shows the Town's zoning districts and locations. If you would like to know the standards that apply to your zoning district, the Zoning Ordinance is available online, Chapter 106 of the Code of Ordinances. Also, the GIS department has interactive maps which display the zoning of every property within the Town of Little Elm.

### 2. How can I change my zoning?

The first step is to download the Development Application Handbook. The applicant must properly fill out a “Universal Application Form” and “Rezoning Checklist” and submit the application to the Planning Department through the MyGov system. All rezoning applications require a public hearing before both the Planning and Zoning Commission and the Town Council. The Planning and Zoning Commission makes a recommendation either approval or denial of the rezoning request and the Town Council makes the final decision.

### 3. What is the “Universal Application Form” and why do I need one?

The “Universal Application Form” is the form that accompanies all applications related to Zoning and Subdivision. This form provides the basic information necessary to process all applications.

### 4. What do I need to submit with my application?

All applications must be submitted with (1) a complete Universal Application Form, (2) a completed application checklist, (3) fees, and (4) all materials listed in the appropriate checklists.

### 5. When do I submit my application?

The Town maintains a “Calendar of Official Submission Dates” that can be found within the Development Application Handbook (next page). These official submission dates mark when applicants may email or upload applications to the Planning Department. All applications are accepted through online programs.

### 6. How do I submit applications?

The Town uses the computer application www.MyGov.us. Log onto the site for Little Elm and it will prompt you in creating a profile, which will be the start of the application process. Utilize our Online Submittal Guides to help guide you through this process.

### 7. What is a pre-application conference and how can it help me?

A pre-application conference is a required meeting between an applicant and Town staff before the applicant submits a formal application. The pre-application conference provides an opportunity for the applicant and Town staff to discuss major development considerations such as utilities, roadways, drainage, Comprehensive Plan elements, specific neighborhood characteristics, and historic information. This meeting is intended to allow for the exchange of non-binding information between the applicant and Town staff to ensure that the applicant is informed of pertinent Town development regulations and processes. This exchange of information is intended to promote an efficient and orderly review process in that an informed applicant submits plans with fewer errors.

### 8. How long will it take to get a decision on my application?

It depends on the type of application submitted and the completeness of the submittal. Most platting applications are decided within 30 days from the submittal date, as required by State Law. Zoning and other applications do not have time frames for approval. The Town works with each applicant to quickly and efficiently work through the review and approval process, generally within a 90 day timeframe.

### 9. Need help?

If you have any questions, please feel free to contact the Town Staff.
