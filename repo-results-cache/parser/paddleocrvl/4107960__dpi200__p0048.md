### 5.4 Audit Findings

The SAIC SITE QA Manager conducted audits of both field activities and of the subcontracted laboratory as part of the QA measures for this project. The results of these technical system reviews are discussed below.

The field audit resulted in no findings or nonconformances. The audit performed at the subcontract laboratory was conducted during the time of project sample analysis. One non-conformance was identified and corrective action was initiated. It was discovered that the laboratory PQL was not meeting specifications due to a reporting error. The analyst was generating the calibration curves as specified above; however, the lower limit on the calibration curve was not being reported. This was immediately rectified and no other findings or nonconformances were identified.
