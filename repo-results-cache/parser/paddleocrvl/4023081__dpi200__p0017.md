Eudes’s Account-Inventory is made up for five different pieces of parchment, the first two, however, have been sewn together to render a record of four separate sheets or rolls each about the length of two standard sheets of 81/2' x 11' pieces of paper set end to end; two are slightly thinner than this contemporary approximation. [Figure 1] In all, as Jaroslav Folda has detailed, the rolls measure as follows:

“Roll A: The first roll is in two parts, originally sewn together:

Part 1 is  $ 16^{11/16} $ inches long and  $ 7^{9/16} $ inches wide at its maximum point; parchment is very thin. Part 2 is  $ 35^{13/16} $ inches long and  $ 7^{13/16} $ inches wide at its maximum point; parchment is thick.

Roll B: The parchment is  $ 19^{1/4} $ inches long and  $ 7^{9/16} $ inches wide at its maximum point; parchment is softer and of a more standard thickness than in roll A.

Roll C: The parchment is  $ 16^{7/16} $ inches long and  $ 5^{7/8} $ inches wide at its maximum point; parchment is standard thickness, but stiffer and much whiter than in rolls A and B.

Roll D: The parchment is 23 $ ^{11/16} $ inches long and 6 $ ^{15/16} $ inches wide at its maximum point: parchment is also very stiff, as with roll C."
