The section starts by providing an exposition of monoidal storage categories: we start from the definition of a storage transformation and explain why this is the same as a coalgebra modality. A monoidal storage category is then defined to be a symmetric monoidal category with products and a storage transformation which is an isomorphism. We then prove that this implies that the modality is a monoidal comonad and, thus, that we do indeed obtain (modulo the relaxing of the closed requirement) what Gavin Bierman called a “new Seely” category.

Next, we return to the main theme of the paper, and formulate the notion of monoidal representation in a Cartesian storage category. We then show that the linear maps of a Cartesian storage category, which has persistent monoidal representation, always form a monoidal storage category. Conversely, the coKleisli category of a monoidal storage category is a Cartesian storage category with monoidal representation. Thus, monoidal storage categories, in which the comonad is exact, correspond precisely to the linear maps of Cartesian storage categories which have persistent monoidal representation.

3.1. COALGEBRA MODALITIES. Let X be a symmetric monoidal category with tensor  $ (\otimes, a_{\otimes}, c_{\otimes}, u_{\otimes}^{L}, u_{\otimes}^{R}) $ which has products and a comonad  $ (S, \delta, \epsilon) $. (Note that  $ a_{\otimes}, c_{\otimes} $ are associativity and commutativity iso-transformations for the tensor, and  $ u_{\otimes}^{L}, u_{\otimes}^{R} $ are the tensor unit iso-transformations.)
