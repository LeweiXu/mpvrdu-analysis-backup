## Ingredients for 90 lt/24 gal pan

Meat

Vegetables (chopped)

Water

Wine

Sage & rosemary

▶ Oil

Salt

39 kg / 86 lbs

5 kg / 11 lbs  

6,5 l/t / 1,7 gal  

6,5 l/t / 1,7 gal

## Procedure:

Preheat at  $ 200^{\circ} $C /  $ 392^{\circ} $F in braising mode for 5 minutes

▶ Sear the veal for 5 minutes

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_655_947_697_992.jpg" alt="Image" width="2%" /></div>


TIP: to obtain a good searing result we recommend to not exceed the quantity indicated below

▶ Add vegetables, water and wine

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_655_1085_697_1128.jpg" alt="Image" width="2%" /></div>


TIP: for this recipe less water is needed since vegetables are frozen

Cook under pressure ☐ with the probe ☐ till core temperature reaches  $ 80^{\circ} $C /  $ 176^{\circ} $F

TIP: for optimal cooking results of the entire load, it is suggested to use portions of uniform size and weight





<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_1143_768_1181_809.jpg" alt="Image" width="2%" /></div>


<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_1141_911_1181_952.jpg" alt="Image" width="2%" /></div>


TIP: to ensure the best performance wait that sauce boils before starting the pressure phase

Use Bermixer Pro to prepare the sauce (vegetables with sauce)

▶ Chill the meat in the SkyLine Chill $ ^{S} $ Blast Chiller

▶ Cut into serving portions

▶ Regenerate in SkyLine Premium $ ^{S} $

Oven

Note: total cooking time may vary depending on the size and weight of pieces.
