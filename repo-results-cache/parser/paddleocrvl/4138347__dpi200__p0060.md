### 29. 04 Backup First Aid Attendant – Requirement

The Employer shall maintain a trained backup First Aid Attendant for each shift to be available in the event of the absence of the regular First Aid Attendant.

### 29. 05 First Aid – Job Duties

It is understood that any employee receiving the above premium shall carry out the normal responsibilities pertinent to the job classification to which they are assigned.

### 29. 06 First Aid Attendant – Seniority

In the event the First-Aid Attendant is at work out of line of seniority the Employer shall assign the First-Aid Attendant to a job that the First-Aid Attendant is capable of performing but replaces the lowest senior employee called into work.
