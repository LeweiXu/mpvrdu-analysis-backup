# Heritage Canadian International School’s Ninth Graduation

On June, 21 $ ^{st} $, 2021, Heritage Canadian International School celebrated its Ninth Graduation Ceremony. The 48 graduating students were excited as some of them were awarded honors in English, Mathematics, Science, Physical Education, French, ICT, Art, and Social Sciences. Selected graduates were chosen for each of the Business Leadership, Principal’s Leadership, and most importantly, the Governor General’s Academic Medal Award.

The Ceremony commenced with a welcome note from the School’s Director, Engineer Mohamed Awara who was very proud of the students, followed by a speech by Ms. Jasmine Wahhab, Senior Trade Commissioner for Egypt, Embassy of Canada, who also gave the Governor General’s Academic Medal Award to Heritage’s top graduate Noor Moataz Mahmoud Hassan Elkateb, with an average of 92.7%.

Heritage has been recognized by the Chancellery of Honors of the Governor General of Canada to award the Governor General’s Academic Medal to the Heritage graduate with the top course average for all grade 11 and grade 12 courses.

Heritage would like to congratulate students who were accepted at some of the top Universities in Egypt such as the American University in Cairo (AUC), the German University in Cairo (GUC), New Giza University, MSA University, and Nile University.

Some of them also applied and were accepted in Universities out of Egypt such as Carleton University and Simon Fraser University in Canada. University of Carlos III Madrid in Spain, City University of London UK, and University of Groningen in the Netherlands.
