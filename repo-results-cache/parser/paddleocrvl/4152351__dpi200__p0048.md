## Steps into society/world responsibility

## Fritz Perls (Gestalttherapy)

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_168_547_423_846.jpg" alt="Image" width="9%" /></div>


I do what I do;

and you do what you do.

I am not in this world

to live according to your expectations.

And you are not in this world

to live according to mine.

And if by chance we find each other - wonderful.

If not, nothing can be done.

### Ruth C. Cohn

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_1437_531_1647_845.jpg" alt="Image" width="7%" /></div>


I want to do what I do.

I am me.

You want to do what you do.

You are you.

This world is our task.

It does not meet our expectations.

However, if we are committed to it,

This world will be beautiful.

If not, it will be nothing.

Cohn 1974, Die Selbsterfahrungsbewegung, -Autismus, S. 164)
