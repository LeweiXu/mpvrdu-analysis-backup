This steady decrease in rates is consistent with national trends. Research suggests these decreases can be attributed to decreases in numbers of teens having sex and increases in the availability of comprehensive sexual health education and highly effective methods of birth control (Livingston, 2019). Some counties in Washington continue to have adolescent pregnancy rates well above the state average (Appendix E).
