The purchase price of SIBEX’s shares amounted to PLN 1,343 thousand, while as at 31 December 2012 the fair value based on the share price reached the total of PLN 333 thousand. Due to negative consolidated results of SIBEX’s Group for the year 2011, SIBEX’s negative result for the three quarters of 2012, and because of the more than double decline in the share price over the period 2011-2012, as at 31 December 2012 the Management Board decided to make a goodwill impairment loss of interest in SIBEX at a value of PLN 1,011 thousand.
