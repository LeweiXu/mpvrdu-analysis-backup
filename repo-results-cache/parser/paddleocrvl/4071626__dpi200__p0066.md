NINTH RACE

STAKES

The 10th Running of

THE KENTUCKY DOWNS LADIES MARATHON

$550,000 - Listed

(Includes $250,000 from KTDF)

FOR FILLIES AND MARES THREE-YEARS-OLD AND UPWARD. No nomination fee. $1,500 to enter and an additional $1,500 to start. Supplementary nominations may be made at time of entry on Tuesday, September 7, 2021 by payment of $15,000 each (includes all fees). $300,000 Guaranteed plus $250,000 for KTDF eligibles only. After payment of 1% to all owners of horses finishing 6th through last, 62% of the remaining purse shall be paid to the owner of the winner, 20% to second, 10% to third, 5% to fourth and 3% to fifth. KTDF funds correspondingly divided providing a KTDF eligible horse finishes in an award position. Three-Year-Olds, 122 lbs.; Older, 126 lbs. Non-winners of a sweepstakes over a mile on the turf in 2021, allowed 2 lbs.; $30,000 twice over a mile or over on the turf in 2021, 4 lbs. The maximum number of starters will be limited to twelve with four also-eligibles. At the discretion of the Racing Secretary, the also-eligibles may remain on the program and be scratched up to fifteen minutes prior to pari-mutuel windows opening. In the event more than twelve entries pass the entry box, the starters will be determined at that time with preference given to Graded or Group stakes winners in order (I-II-III). Stakes winners, then highest turf earnings in 2021. Any horse excluded from running because of the aforementioned preference shall be refunded the entry fee and supplementary nomination fee if applicable. Starters and riders must be named through the entry box by the usual time of closing.
