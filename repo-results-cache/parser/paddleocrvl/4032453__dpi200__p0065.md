## Firecracker & Beardlip penstemon

Penstemon eatonii, Penstemon barbatus

Height: up to 3 feet

Width: 12–18 inches

Native to Utah and Colorado. P. eatonii (pictured) tends to bloom before P. barbatus. Attractive to hummingbirds. Can reseed.

## ‘May Night’ salvia

Salvia nemorosa

Height: 18–24 inches

Width: 12–18 inches

Longish bloom period. Reseeds. Cut back after blooming for fresh growth and to control reseeding. Visited by many bees.

## Large beardtongue

Penstemon grandiflorus

Height: 2–3 feet

Width: 8–12 inches

Native. Large showy blooms. Reseeds moderately. Short bloom period of approximately 2 weeks. Can be short-lived (1–2 years).

## Desert princesplume

Stanleya pinnata

Height: 3 feet

Width: 18–24 inches

Native. Can be short-lived. Concentrates selenium, can be toxic to livestock if they are without other forage sources. Can reseed.
