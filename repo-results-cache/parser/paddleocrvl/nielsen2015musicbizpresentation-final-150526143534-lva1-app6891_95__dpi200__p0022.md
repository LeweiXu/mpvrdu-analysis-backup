## • HIP-HOP FANS ARE AT THE FOREFRONT OF THE DIGITAL MUSIC MOVEMENT

## WHO ARE THEY?

Over twice as likely to be African- American and 50% more likely to be Hispanic.

## THEY ARE VERY ENGAGED WITH DIGITAL MUSIC

Far more likely to use streaming services for both consumption and discovery.

Strong social element – hip-hop consumers are more than twice as likely to connect with friends through music.

## THEY ARE SPENDING MORE

Hip-hop fans spend 35% more annually on music, including twice as much on club events with live

DJs and 40% more on music festivals.

## WHAT MOBILE SERVICES?

Use Sprint and T-Mobile significantly more than the average consumer.

Also, 50% more likely to have a Samsung mobile phone than average.
