## SCHEDULE B: EXPENDITURES

M.G.L. c. 55 requires committees to list, in alphabetical order, all expenditures over $50 in a reporting period. Committees must keep detailed accounts and records of all expenditures, but need itemize only those over $50. Expenditures of $50 or less may be added together, from committee records, and reported on line 13.

This page may be copied if additional pages are required to report all expenditures. If you do so, include your committee name and a page number on each additional page.
