Vogl ceiling tiles are accessible, perforated ceiling panels with high acoustic performance for installation into T-profile systems.

The black acoustic fleece lining on the back (other colours on request) satisfies the highest demands on sound absorption.

Vogl ceiling tiles come with a factory-applied, pure white finishing coat (similar to RAL 9010).

Minor deviations in the product properties, in particular minor differences in colour and texture as well as insignificant deviations in length, width and thickness of the material delivered, are not considered defects. Mixing panel material from different production batches should be avoided.

Installation system: Excellent (T15) rebated grid

Based on standard: EN 14190 "Gypsum board products from reprocessing"

Material class: A2-s1, d0 (non-combustible) according to EN 13501-1



Long edge: FK T15 (bevelled), type Excellent

Short edge: FK T15 (bevelled), type Excellent
