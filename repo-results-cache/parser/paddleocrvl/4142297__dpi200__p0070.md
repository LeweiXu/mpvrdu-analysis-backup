<div style="text-align: center;">Figure 3.10 Correlated electrical and optical signals for ArcLight-A242 VSFP</div>


The cortical neurons, transduced with rAAV6 psc_hSyn1_ArcLight, were at DPT7/DIV14 stimulated by V-clamp and in parallel, optically recorded. On the upper graph there are traces of the electrical responses of the cell (red) and applied voltage (black) from the patch-clamp pipette, while the lower graph shows the time-correlated processed optical trace (violet).
