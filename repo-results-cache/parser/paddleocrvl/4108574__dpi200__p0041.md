# 7.3 Get Thin Client Hardware and Software Information

The first level of Thin Client information is found in multiple locations on the chassis.

#### 7.3.1 Product Information

The pull-out Product Information tag on the back of the chassis shows the model number and compliance certifications.

#### 7.3.2 MAC Addresses

The small MAC stickers on the side of the Thin Client face show the MAC addresses (physical address of the internal NICs) for the Thin Client. The Thin Client can be uniquely identified and tracked using the MAC addresses.
