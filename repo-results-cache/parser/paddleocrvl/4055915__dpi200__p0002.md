## About the cover … ‘Lil Pumpkins’

This year’s cover was painted in watercolors by Nancy Landis. After a 1-year pandemic-break, the Elizabethtown Fair will be more welcome than ever! We will enjoy the fair anew, just as this little fellow enjoys meeting his first fall pumpkin pile.

Nancy has been truly honored to provide a cover for the Elizabethtown Fair Premium book again this year. The fair is near and dear to our hearts as a solid American tradition that should be apart of our community.



The original artwork will be sold at the Annual Elizabethtown Fair Auction on Friday evening at 9:00 p.m. The proceeds will support the Elizabethtown Fair.
