## Evidence of Success

Evaluate the progress made in increasing enrollment for black, Hispanic, black male, Hispanic male, and ELL students in all advanced courses, and in closing enrollment gaps for black, Hispanic, black male, Hispanic male, and ELL students. Evaluation must include 2016-17 to 2020-21.

The evaluation shows an increase in White, Black and Hispanic student participation when comparing 2016-17 data to 2020-21 data. The number of White students participating in Level 3 courses increased from 42% in 2016-17 to 45.5% in 2020-21. White male participation in Level 3 courses increased in the same period, from 31.85% to 37.2%. Black student participation in all Level 3 courses increased significantly from 20.69% in 2016-17 to 42.9% in 2020-21. Black Males enrolled in level 3 courses had an increase in percentage points from 16.67% in 2016-17 to 27.8% in 2020-21. Hispanic students increased from 41.07% to 50% from 2016-17 to 2020-21. Hispanic Males had a significant increase in percentage points from 20% in 2016-17 to 40% in 2020-21. Comparison data for ELL student participation is not available due to the low number of ELL students (1) in the 9-12 grade span for the 2017-18 to 2020-21 comparison years.
