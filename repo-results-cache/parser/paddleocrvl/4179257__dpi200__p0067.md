## EXT-SP jack

Connects to an external speaker:

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_140_1301_184_1344.jpg" alt="Image" width="2%" /></div>

## SEND jack

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_869_685_929_746.jpg" alt="Image" width="3%" /></div>


The terminal goes low when the transceiver transmits. (RCA Plug)

This terminal is used to control an external non-Icom linear amplifier.

T/R control voltage and current must be less than 16 V DC and 0.5 A.

## PHONES jack

PHONES

Connects to standard stereo headphones:



<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_896_1062_940_1105.jpg" alt="Image" width="2%" /></div>


3.5 mm ( $ \frac{1}{8} $ in) (d)

• Output impedance: 8 ~ 16 Ω

• Output level: More than 5 mW into an 8  $ \Omega $ load.
