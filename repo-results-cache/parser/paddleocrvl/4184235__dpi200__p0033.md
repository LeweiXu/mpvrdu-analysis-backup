We consider here the following Duffing oscillator equations:

 $$ \ddot{x}+0.15\dot{x}-x+x^{3}=0.245\sin(t). $$ 

For this choice of parameters, it is possible to find in the phase space a transient chaos behaviour of the trajectories. Due to the periodic forcing, it is suitable to build a time- $ 2\pi $ map, where the flow is cut every  $ \Delta t = 2\pi $.
