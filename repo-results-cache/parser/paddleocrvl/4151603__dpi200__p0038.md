#### 4.3.4 Recent developments

There have been recent developments in the measurement of compost stability using near Infrared spectroscopy for determining compost stability (Erhart et al., 2017). It is a rapid analysis and requires no chemicals, but it still requires calibration and validation and is therefore not advanced enough to consider as a routine test in Ireland at present.
