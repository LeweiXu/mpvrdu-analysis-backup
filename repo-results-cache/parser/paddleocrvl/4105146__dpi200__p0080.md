Our approach to this Project is to develop a document using the 2015 UWMP information and format for efficiency to provide an updated 2020 UWMP to meet the following objectives:

• Address legislative changes from the 2015 UWMP

• Evaluate existing and future water consumption

• Establish water use baselines and targets

• Describe and evaluate the existing water supplies and system

- Describe the reliability of the City’s water supplies and short and long-term water management strategies

• Discuss the demand management measures for existing and future water conservation

• Provide information regarding the notification, public hearing, and adoption of the UWMP

## Task 1 | Obtain Information and Coordination with City

Kick-Off Meeting. Our team will discuss the roles and responsibilities of the City and Black Water team members, the process for preparing the 2020 UWMP, project schedule, and data requirements.

Project Management and Administration. Internal management of the project including staff scheduling, monthly project updates to City, budget oversight, project correspondence, document production, review of work, and other general overhead items. Includes three (3) project meetings.

Collect and Review Data. Black Water will coordinate with City staff to obtain and review existing data and previous reports to update the existing UWMP including but not limited to the following:

Demographic Data. Land use, population, housing, growth projections, latest US Census data, and 2014-2023 Housing Element.

Historical and Current Water Production Data and Consumption Data by User Class. Black Water will use the historical water use and production data to complete the baseline per person water use calculation considering existing potable and non-potable use, distribution system losses, etc.

Water Conservation Efforts. Previous reports will be reviewed, and City staff will be interviewed to get descriptions of past and current conservation efforts. The water use data by land use will be used to validate current water conservation efforts by the City.

Demand Management Measures (DMM). The City’s current and planned DMMs to achieve water use targets pursuant to the California Water Code will be reviewed and used to update the appropriate narratives.

## Task 2 | Water Conservation Ordinance Revision

Assist with the preparation of revisions to the City’s water conservation ordinance to fit the updated guidance within the UWMP’s Water Shortage Contingency Plan section.
