## 5 Conclusion

In this paper, we highlight the potential of combining retrieving and ranking with multi-modal large language models to revolutionize perception tasks such as fine-grained recognition, zero-shot image recognition, and few-shot object recognition. Our proposed approach can be seamlessly integrated into various MLLMs for real-world applications where the variety and volume of categories continuously expand.
