## <Thread take-up spring height>

Loosen the set screw (1) and turn the tensioner body to adjust the thread take-up spring height.

<div style="text-align: center;"><Thread take-up spring tension></div>


<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_331_1117_1373_1595.jpg" alt="Image" width="62%" /></div>


Adjust by turning the shaft (2) with a screwdriver.

* When the spring height (stroke) is great or the spring tension is insufficient, it may cause the thread end length to vary after thread trimming.
