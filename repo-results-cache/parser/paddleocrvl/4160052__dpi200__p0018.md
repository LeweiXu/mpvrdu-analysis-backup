## For our communities

At Purina, we understand the valuable role that pets can play in society. Promoting loving, responsible pet ownership can help develop thriving communities, improving the life of pets and the people who love them. We want to encourage and preserve the special bond between people and pets.

However, we recognised the challenges facing pet owners and our community partners after the arrival of COVID-19 and we worked together to find new and agile ways to support them.

Our Purina in Society (PinS) goals are designed to help us fulfil these ambitions.
