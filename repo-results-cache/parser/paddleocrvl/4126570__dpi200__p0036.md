## Ingredients for 90 lt/24 gal pan

Meat

▶ Mixed vegetables 2.5 kg / 5.5 lbs

▶ Oil

White wine

Water

18 kg / 40 lbs

1,2 It / 0,3 gal

Spices

Salt

1,2 It / 0,3 gal

7,3 lt / 1,9 gal

## Procedure:

Preheat at  $ 180^{\circ} $C /  $ 356^{\circ} $F in braising mode

▶ Pour oil and sear the meat adding salt, spices, vegetables and wine

▶ Add water

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_654_1024_694_1067.jpg" alt="Image" width="2%" /></div>


TIP: full load for this recipe is the quantity fitting the bottom of the pan. Quantity of water required is the same regardless the load

▶ When water boils, close the lid and cook under pressure 🍜

for 30 minutes

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_1136_823_1181_869.jpg" alt="Image" width="2%" /></div>


TIP: to ensure the best performance wait that sauce boils before starting the pressure phase

Portion and serve
