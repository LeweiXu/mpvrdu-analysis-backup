## Conclusion

The study provides insights on the impact of ICAR-IIRR varieties and quantified the contribution of the IIRR varieties to the total area and production of rice at the national level. It was estimated that, annually, the IIRR varieties contribute to about 8% of the total production of rice in the country. The significant impact of IIRR varieties is the generation of gross returns to the tune of 3,161 Million USD annually and cumulative returns of 15,803 Million USD for the period from 2015-16 to 2019-20.
