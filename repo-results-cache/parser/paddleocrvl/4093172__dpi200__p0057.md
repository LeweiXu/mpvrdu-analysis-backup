The withdrawal of water from Metropolitan’s storage in dry years and the purchase of “transfer” water from willing sellers in these years, have played an integral role in maintaining Metropolitan’s water supply reliability. Under the 2015 IRP Update, the role of storage and transfers will continue to be critically important for balancing water supplies and demands.

In addition, Metropolitan maintains roughly 650,000 acre-feet of emergency storage in all years.
