## Section 18: Acknowledgments

The successful operation of a strandings project over the 10,000 miles comprising Scotland’s coastline is only possible with assistance from a large number of individuals and organisations. These volunteers assist SMASS staff in the identification, recovery, storage and transport of stranded animals. We are immensely grateful to all who helped us out so far in 2018, however particular thanks are due to the staff and students of the Sea Mammal Research Unit, Hessilhead Wildlife Rescue Trust, Karen Hall and the Scottish Natural Heritage team on Shetland, Georg Hantke of the National Museums of Scotland, British Divers Marine Life Rescue medics, the Hebridean Whale and Dolphin Trust and Mark Dagleish at the Moredun Research Institute.

We are also grateful to all our trained stranding volunteers who have ventured out in all weathers to collect photographs, data and samples from some much decomposed animals.
