In addition, a minimum building setback for habitable structures of 10 feet from the development setback (i.e., 60 feet from the top of slope) is also recommended.

As with the setback in natural areas, the development setback should apply to private property and infrastructure. No specific building setback other than those contained in the County Code will be necessary.

## SITE PREPARATION AND GRADING

## General

All site preparation and grading should be performed in accordance with the County of Orange and the City of Newport Beach grading code requirements along with the recommendations presented in this report.

## Clearing

All significant organic material such as weeds, brush, tree branches, roots, construction debris, and any other decomposable materials should be removed from areas to be graded.

## Processing

Once remedial removals are completed where recommended by the project geotechnical consultant, the upper 6 inches of the excavated native soil should be scarified, moisture conditioned, and compacted to at least 90% relative compaction.
