The Sparta Area Chamber of Commerce hereby submits its proposal for the acquisition of the soon to be former Sparta Police Station, located on East Oak Street in Sparta, WI. The proposal has been duly authorized by the Sparta Area Chamber of Commerce Board of Directors as recorded in the minutes of the regular May meeting, held on May 12, 2021.

The following proposal will present a compelling argument for the purchase of the Sparta Police Station from the City of Sparta based on the best and highest use of the property and the benefit to the community and the downtown district of Sparta, Wisconsin.

It may not be known to all who read this that the move of the Chamber to a more central, downtown location dates to the previous Executive Director, an initiative articulated with the offer to purchase the building now owned and occupied by Jackie Jaeger and KEPS. When the offer to purchase was not accepted, the Chamber of Commerce moved to purchase the building now occupied by "A Healthy Touch," located next to the alley adjoining the Beaver Creek Parkway. When the parties were unable to reach agreeable terms on this building, the effort moved to the former LTS Studio located on the corner of North Water Street and West Main Street.

Each location presented its own nuances of upfront cost, renovation cost, location, and overall condition. By the presentation of these previous deliberate efforts, the earnestness of the Chamber to relocate should be clear. What we will illuminate in this proposal is how this opportunity is the best alignment of initial cost, renovation cost, location, and future growth and commerce for the community.

The following proposal will respond to the Request for Proposal (RFP) on the specific requirements of:

- Provide a detailed description of our vision regarding the use of the property and how this vision and proposed use will enhance, blend in, and strengthen the downtown and greater community

Use that is consistent with the current zoning in which the building stands.

- Provide a timeline from the acceptance of the proposal to the occupancy of the building for its intended use detailing key dates and milestones.

Our expectations of the City of Sparta regarding any expectations we have for the transfer of ownership.

We are looking forward to discussing any questions you may have regarding our proposal.
