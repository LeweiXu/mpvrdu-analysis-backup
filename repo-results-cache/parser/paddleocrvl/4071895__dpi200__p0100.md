 $$ \widetilde{\mathcal{C}(X_{\tau})}(x)=\sum_{n\geq1}\frac{x^{n}}{n}\sum_{d|n}\phi(d)p_{d}^{n/d}=\sum_{m\geq1}\frac{\phi(m)}{m}\log\frac{1}{1-p_{m}x^{m}}. $$

c) A colored cycle is called asymmetric if its only automorphism is the identity. Denote by  $ \overline{\mathcal{C}(X_{\tau})} $, the species of colored asymmetric cycles.  $$ \overline{{\mathcal{C}(X_{\tau})}}(x)=\sum_{n\geq1}\frac{x^{n}}{n}\sum_{d|n}\mu(d)p_{d}^{n/d}=\sum_{m\geq1}\frac{\mu(m)}{m}\log\frac{1}{1-p_{m}x^{m}}. $$
