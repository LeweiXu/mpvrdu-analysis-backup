### 6. - Description of a Service

If the "exact change" message is lit, with MDB/ICP coin mechanism it indicates that only exact coinage will be accepted and with EJECUTIVO coin mechanism, that only programmed coins will be accepted, which, in order to guarantee correct change return, should be the same coinage needed for it.

Change coins go to the corresponding tube unless it is full or out of order. In such cases, they will be sent to the bin.

Each coin is analysed according to the models recorded in the selector. If accepted, their amount increases the displayed credit.

Coins that are not for change are sent directly to the bin."
