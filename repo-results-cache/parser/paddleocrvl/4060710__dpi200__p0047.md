#### 4.2.6 Example 1

We present herein an example with the following characteristics:

• A320 BADA3 model

• Madrid to Berlin flight

• 3D equations of motion

• Climbing CAS/Mach phase with Step Climb

• Structured airspace

• Uncertainty on Winds and Temperature (EPS)

• Take-off to last point of the standard arrival procedure

• Non-exposure to convection and no initial time uncertainty.
