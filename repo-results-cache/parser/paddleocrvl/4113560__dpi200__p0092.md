## Treadle

As screws tend to loosen over time, you should check the treadles and pedals after 3 to 5 operational hours for the first time, then every month.

☒ If a treadle loosens tighten it immediately. Remove the black cap from the crankshaft and retighten the screw underneath with a 14 mm socket spanner. The pedal may be retightened to the crankshaft with a 15 mm open-end spanner.
