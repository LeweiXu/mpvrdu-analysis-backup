#### 2.1.3 Multichip module technology

IBM System z and system p machines are the only servers using multichip modules (MCM) and multilayer ceramic (MLC) technology. The advantages over printed circuit board technology are:

• faster chip interconnects

• improved reliability

The compagnion Thermal Conduction Module /TCM) technology ptovides unparalleled power dissipation and cooling characteristics
