## A Process for Inspiring Change

Source: IBM BAO Services methodology.

PADIE (Process-Application-Data-Insight-Embed) technique is a three-step process by which a company can operationalize insights drawn from data: first, document processes and applications; second, use analytic techniques to gain insight from data; and third, select the most appropriate ways to embed insight into operations.
