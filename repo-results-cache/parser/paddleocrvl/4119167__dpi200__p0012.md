## Elder Financial Exploitation

“The fraudulent or otherwise illegal, unauthorized, or improper act of process of an individual, including caregiver or fiduciary, who uses the resources of an older individual for monetary or personal benefit, profit, or gain, or that results in depriving an older individual of rightful access to or use of, benefits resources, belongings or assets.” - 42 U.S.C. 3002
