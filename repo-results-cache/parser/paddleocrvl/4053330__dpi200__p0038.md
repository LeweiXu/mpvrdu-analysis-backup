<div style="text-align: center;">TITLE: Diagnosis Pointer [lines 1–6]</div>


INSTRUCTIONS: In 24E, enter the diagnosis code reference letter (pointer) as shown in Item Number 21 to relate the date of service and the procedures performed to the primary diagnosis. When multiple services are performed, the primary reference letter for each service should be listed first, other applicable services should follow. The reference letter(s) should be A – L or multiple letters as applicable. ICD-10-CM or ICD-9-CM diagnosis codes must be entered in Item Number 21. Do not enter them in 24E.

Enter letters left justified in the field. Do not use commas between the letters.

DESCRIPTION: The “Diagnosis Pointer” is the line letter from Item Number 21 that relates to the reason the service(s) was performed.

FIELD SPECIFICATION: This field allows for the entry of 4 characters in the unshaded area.
