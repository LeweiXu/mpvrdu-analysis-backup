## GLOBAL OS BREAKDOWN

Android dominated the smartphone market with a share of 82.8%.

iOS saw its market share for 2015/Q2 decline by 22.3% QoQ with 47.5 million shipments.

Windows Phone experienced a QoQ decline of 4.2% with a total of 8.8 million units shipped this quarter.

Blackberry OS, which saw a small increase in some regions, continued to decline in growth globally.
