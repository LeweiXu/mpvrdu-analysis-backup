## Slipping hazard

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_83_256_148_315.jpg" alt="Image" width="5%" /></div>


- Fluids can be discharged from the coffee machine if used improperly or if errors occur. These fluids can cause a slipping hazard.

> Regularly check whether the coffee machine is leaking, and make sure no water is coming out.

⚠️ CAUTION

## Misuse

- If the machine is used other than as intended, this could lead to a risk of injury.

> The coffee machine may be used only as intended.

WARNING



The WMF 1500S is designed to dispense beverages made with coffee and/or milk and/or powder (such as choc or topping) into suitable containers. This device is intended for industrial and commercial use and should be operated by experts or trained users in stores, offices, restaurants, hotels, or similar points of use. It can also be used in a domestic environment. The device can be used as a self-service device if it is supervised by trained personnel.
