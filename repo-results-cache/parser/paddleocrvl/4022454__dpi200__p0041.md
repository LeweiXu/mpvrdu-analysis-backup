Kolkata loses 4% of its GDP each year, driven largely by the productivity losses suffered by its large but vulnerable MSME sector. While this economic impact is less severe than that in more polluted Northern cities, it is still ~25% higher than the national figure.

Kolkata lost 6,000 people through air pollution linked diseases in 2019 at a mortality rate 33% higher than even Delhi, leading to a disproportionately large impact from premature mortality, representing 60% of overall impact on GDP vs 46% for the rest of India.
