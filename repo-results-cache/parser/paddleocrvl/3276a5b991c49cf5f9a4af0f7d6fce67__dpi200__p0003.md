## Statutory Basis for Civil Forfeiture

13. Title 18, United States Code, Section 981(a)(1)(A) provides for civil forfeiture of any property, real or personal, involved in a transaction or attempted transaction in violation of 18 U.S.C. §§ 1956, 1957 or 1960, or any property traceable to such property.

14. Title 18, United States Code, Section 981(a)(1)(C) provides for civil forfeiture to the United States of, “[a]ny property, real or personal, which constitutes or is derived from proceeds traceable to a violation of... any offense constituting “specified unlawful activity” (as defined in section 1956(c)(7) of this title), or a conspiracy to commit such offense.”

15. Title 18, United States Code, Section 1956(c)(7) defines “specified unlawful activity” to include any act or activity constituting an offense listed in Title 18, United States Code, Section 1961(1) and other specified offenses. Title 18, United States Code, Sections 1341, 1343, 1956, and 1957 are specified unlawful activities under Title 18, United States Code, Sections 1956(c)(7) and/or 1961(1)(B).
