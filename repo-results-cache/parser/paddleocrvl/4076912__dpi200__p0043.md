######## IV. 2.2.2.1.3. Other efficacy and tolerance testing

This category of regulatory use refers to uses that are neither linked to quality control nor to toxicity testing. These uses are related to, for example, efficacy (immunogenicity) of human and veterinary vaccines and dose ranging studies. They represented little over 124,000 uses in 2018, a decrease

(-41%) despite of the addition of the data from Norway. This could be partly explained by an exceptional increase in 2017 (+29% compared to 2016).
