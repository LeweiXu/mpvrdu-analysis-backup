Definition 1.11. The cycle index series of a species of structures F is the formal power series (in an infinite number of variables  $ x_{1}, x_{2}, x_{3}, \ldots $)

 $$ Z_{F}(x_{1},x_{2},x_{3},\cdots)=\sum_{n\geq0}\frac{1}{n!}\left(\sum_{\sigma\in\mathcal{S}_{n}}\mathrm{f i x}F[\sigma]x_{1}^{\sigma_{1}}x_{2}^{\sigma_{2}}x_{3}^{\sigma_{3}}\cdots\right), $$ 

where  $ S_{n} $ denotes the group of permutations of  $ [n] $ (i.e.,  $ S_{n} = S[n] $) and fix  $ F[\sigma] = (F[\sigma])_{1} = \text{is the number of } F $-structures on  $ [n] $ fixed by  $ F[\sigma] $, i.e., the number of F-structures on  $ [n] $ for which  $ \sigma $ is an automorphism.
