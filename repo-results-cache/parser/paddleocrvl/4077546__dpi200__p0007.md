## Development and Evaluation of Alternatives

• As specified in the NJPDES permits from the New Jersey Department of Environmental Protection, an evaluation of combined sewer overflow (CSO) control technologies at each outfall in the service area is required.

• The goal of identifying, developing and evaluating CSO control technologies is to identify the best strategy to reduce the frequency of overflows to no more than 4 at each outfall in a typical year.

• An evaluation of alternatives for the Adams Street WWTP, River Road WWTP and the CSO outfalls in each service area has been developed.

• The purpose of this evaluation is to submit an approvable report to NJDEP in June that provides the information needed for the Authority to then develop the Long Term Control Plans by next summer.
