## The current account

Compared with the corresponding three months of 2003, the current account deficit more than doubled to Lm100.2 million from Lm42.2 million (see Table 5.1). This widening was essentially due to a larger merchandise trade gap, as imports increased while exports continued to drop. At the same time, the surplus on services contracted, while the deficit on current transfers widened slightly. Conversely, the income account swung from a deficit to a small positive balance. Developments in the fourth quarter of 2004 had a significant impact on the annual outturn (see Chart 5.1).
