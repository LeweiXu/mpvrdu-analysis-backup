### 1.3 The Future of Robotic Arms

Robotic arm that helps in logistic operations such as the separation of items, categorizing, and sorting is highly needed nowadays. This can be seen in large industries where the process of sorting components and distributing them is a critical part of the manufacturing process. As of now, the integration of machine learning with robotic arms is still undergoing many tests before being added into the market.

Knowing that car manufacturers are already using robotic arms to fully assemble a car through predetermined paths, a future where both technologies are used together is not far from reach. These researches are mostly done in universities. Through students, some great projects are created. One of these projects is called Robo-picker built by students from MIT and Princeton University. The robot can pick up an object (a grocery shopping object) it has never seen before and can determine the best way to hold it. After it picks up the object, 3 cameras take multiple images of the objects to compare them with a preset library of grocery shopping items. Once the object is known it moves it to another area. This robot brought much attention, winning in the amazon robotics challenge, where they earned first place in the stowing task in July 2018. This proves that robots with machine learning capabilities will be the norm in factories very soon, it is only a matter of time.
