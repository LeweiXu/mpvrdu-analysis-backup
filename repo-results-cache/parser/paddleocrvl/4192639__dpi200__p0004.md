# ALL MY BONES SHAKE Seeking a Progressive Path to the Prophetic Voice

ROBERT JENSEN

Religious Studies • Trade Paperback • 9781593762346 $15.95 • 256 pages • 5.4" x 8.1" • Ebook available

In a nation where three-fourths of the population identifies as Christian and religion salts the political discourse, unrest has manifested itself as a debate between atheists and believers. In All My Bones Shake, Robert Jensen reveals the multitiered complexity of the conflict and offers a progressive approach. While fundamentalists on both sides have fought to an intellectual standoff and moderates seem content to ignore the battle, Jensen pushes for answers that make sense with our modern scientific world.

More than a study of the religious debate in America, All My Bones Shake marks a new communion: a way to use theology to create a sustainable society and meet the uncertainty of our lives with confidence.

# AN ATHEIST'S HISTORY OF BELIEF Understanding Our Most Extraordinary Invention

MATTHEW KNEALE

Atheism • Trade Paperback • 9781619024694 $16.95 • 288 pages • 6" x 9" • Ebook available

What first prompted prehistoric man, sheltering in the shadows of deep caves, to call upon the realm of the spirits? And why has belief thrived since, shaping thousands of generations of shamans, pharaohs, Aztec priests and Mayan rulers, Jews, Buddhists, Christians, Nazis, and Scientologists?

About the author: Matthew Kneale studied Modern History at Oxford University. He then spent a year in Japan, where he began writing short stories. He is the author of several novels, including English Passengers, which won the Whitbread Award and was short-listed for the Man Booker Prize. He currently lives in Rome.
