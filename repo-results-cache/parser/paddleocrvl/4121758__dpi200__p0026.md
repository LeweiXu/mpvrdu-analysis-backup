# 6.9 LIFE INSURANCE

#### 6.9.1 Overall Performance

The life insurance sector contracted in 2009. Life insurance premiums decreased by 9.9 percent to MK1.8 billion from MK2.0 billion recorded in 2008. Total assets however increased by 14.8 percent (2008: 18.5 percent) to MK58.5 billion (2008: MK50.9 billion).

#### 6.9.2 Premium

NICO Life had the largest market share based on gross Life Insurance premium income written in 2009 with 49.0 percent of the premium written (Chart 12). Vanguard had the least share of the market of the three life insurers that were in existence. Smile Life Insurance Company started operations in April 2009, and managed to get 0.4 percent of the total premiums written for the nine months to December when they were in operation.

The coming into the market of Smile is expected to increase the penetration of insurance in the country. Tables detailing some aspects of the life insurance performance during the year are available in the appendices section of this report.
