####### IV. 2.3.2.1. Animal species reused

In absolute numbers, the main species reused for scientific purposes in 2018 were mice, other fish, sheep, rats, zebra fish, rabbits, horses, donkeys and cross-breeds.

In proportion, large mammals are more often reused such as horses, donkeys and cross-breeds (87%), sheep (70%), cats (47%), dogs (31%) and non-human primates.

Reptiles (59%) and xenopus (28%) amongst amphibians were also often reused (Table 29). The high level of reuse of reptiles may have been a reporting error. However, it was not possible to confirm and correct this in time for the publication of the report.
