### 8. Jhelum District

#### 8.1. Profile of the district

Jehlum District is situated in the north-east of Punjab province. It is bounded in the north by Rawalpindi District, in the south by Mandi Baha-ud-Din, Gujrat and Sargodha, in the east by Azad Jammu and Kashmir and in the west by Chakwal and Khushab, from which it is separated by the Kohistan-e-Namak mountain range.
