Phone signals travel at 3x10^{8} m/s

The waves are transmitted and received.

If the are not ‘in line of sight’

the signal may be lost.

All types of phones receive waves

When you talk into your phone; it converts the sound into a microwave signal and is sent to the nearest phone mast.

Microwave signals are affected by:

-Poor weather conditions

-large surfaces of water
