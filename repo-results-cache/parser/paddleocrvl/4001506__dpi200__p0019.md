## Recruitment and Sustainability

Participants highlighted the problems posed by the lack of SAR volunteers and volunteer burnout in their communities. Many of the community participants wear multiple hats and are involved in multiple groups responsible for SAR – some are GSAR members, Coast Guard Auxiliary, Rangers, and CASARA volunteers. The level of community involvement differs across the Kitikmeot with some reporting a high degree of involvement and others suggesting that it was hard to recruit.
