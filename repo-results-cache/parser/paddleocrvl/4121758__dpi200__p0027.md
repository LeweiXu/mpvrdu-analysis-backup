#### 6.9.3 Underwriting Experience

The life insurance industry underwrote 5,841 new individual life policies during the year under review, (Chart 13) in the process underwriting MK368.9 million in annual premiums (Chart 14).

During the same period, the industry wrote eight new group life insurance policies with an annual premium of MK572.6 million. Following the fact that fewer policies were underwritten in the year, premium income decreased in the current period.
