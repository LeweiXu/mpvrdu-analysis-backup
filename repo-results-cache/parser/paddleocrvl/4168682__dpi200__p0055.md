##### 8.2.2. Financing of Education

As in other districts, in Jhelum the public sector education (up to the secondary level) is mainly financed by the federal and provincial governments through the PFC award. Accordingly, the provincial government distributes funds among the districts falling within its jurisdiction. Jhelum District faces a scarcity of resources. In the budget, from 2007/08 for instance, the district's own revenues amounted to only 26.3 million PKR, 11 percent of the total annual estimated receipts of 2,444.2 million PKR.
