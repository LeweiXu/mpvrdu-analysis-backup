## USCG Arctic Strategy (2013)

CG Vision for Operating In Arctic Region:

■ Ensure safe, secure, and environmentally responsible maritime activity in the Arctic.

■ CG Arctic Strategic Objectives:

■ Improve Awareness

■ Modernize Governance

■ Broaden Partnerships

## ☐ CG Goals:

■ Protect Those On The Sea

■ Protect People From The Sea

■ Protect The Sea Itself
