Such selection will be permitted by Kelowna employees for a period of thirty (30) days from ratification of this agreement, in order of seniority. Kelowna employees will retain their rate of pay for one (1) working month, after which they will revert to the designated pay rate for the job they occupy at the new facility. These Kelowna employees will also retain their industry seniority for all other purposes at their new facility, including for the purposes of posting to any new vacancies in the new facility.

Employees on the Kelowna seniority list who either do not have the required seniority for displacement or do not select another entry level position will be permanently laid off with no right of recall. Employees displaced by a Kelowna employee will have right of recall in accordance with Article 18.01.

This letter shall be utilized as a template should any other company facilities close during the life of this collective agreement.
