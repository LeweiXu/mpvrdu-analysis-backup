#### 2.4.5 Configure Time Zone

Configure the time zone to match the site’s time zone.

➢ To modify the time zone

1. From the Control Panel, select Date and Time.

2. Click Change time zone... and select the time zone that matches the site’s time zone.

3. Click OK to save the changes.

4. Click OK to close the window.
