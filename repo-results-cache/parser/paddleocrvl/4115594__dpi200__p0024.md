The duration is usually two to four years and depends on the type of apprenticeship. Either vocational school is attended all year round on at least one weekday or in blocks (at least eight weeks a year in total) on a seasonal or course-related basis.

The framework curriculum comprises a total of 1260 lessons in 3 school levels, of which at least 360 lessons in each of the first, second and third grades.
