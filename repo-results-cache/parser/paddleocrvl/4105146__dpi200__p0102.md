# PROFESSIONAL SERVICES AGREEMENT BETWEEN (City Attorney Legal Services)

THIS PROFESSIONAL SERVICES AGREEMENT (the “Agreement”) is made by and entered into on May 10, 2021, by and between the CITY OF ATWATER (“CITY), a California municipal corporation and Best Best and Krieger Attorneys at Law (“BBK”). CITY and BBK shall be referred to hereinafter collectively referred to as the “Parties,” and singularly as a “Party.” The Parties hereto agree to as follows:

### SECTION 1. SERVICES OF BBK

1.1 Scope of Services. In compliance with all terms and conditions of this Agreement, on May 15 $ ^{th} $, 2021. BBK shall provide the legal services reasonably required to represent and advise City in connection with City Attorney services. BBK is authorized by this Agreement to perform particular legal services for the CITY, including special counsel services. All work to be performed by BBK is within the scope of this Agreement in accordance with Exhibit “A” (“Scope of Work”).

1.2 Compliance with Law. All services rendered hereunder shall be provided in accordance with all ordinances, resolution, statutes, rules, regulations, and laws of the City of Atwater and any Federal, State, or local governmental agency of competent jurisdiction.

1.3 Business Licenses, Permits, Fees and Assessments. Except as otherwise as specified herein, BBK shall obtain, at its sole cost and expense, such licenses, permits and approvals as may be required by law for the performance of the services required by this Agreement. BBK shall have the sole obligation to pay for any fees, assessments and taxes, plus applicable penalties and interest, which may be imposed by law and arise from or are necessary for the performance of this Agreement.

### SECTION 2. COMPENSATION

2.1 Fees. For the services rendered pursuant to this Agreement, BBK shall be compensated in accordance with Exhibits B-F.

Frank Splendorio will be the Attorney in charge of representing CITY'S interests and shall serve as City Attorney at the CITY'S pleasure. If other attorneys and/or paralegals are assigned to work on CITY matters, the then current hourly rates of those individuals will be utilized. This Agreement retains the legal services of BBK. Hourly rates are subject to reasonable change, usually at the beginning of each year.
