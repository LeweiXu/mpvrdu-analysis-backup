Some prescribing doctors, when meeting such patients, may find signing a prescription the easiest way to bring the consultation to an end.

These trends are no doubt beneficial to the pharmaceutical producers. From a public health perspective the trend can be questioned, since it may allocate the individual health responsibility away from the subject. The health care system must continuously recognize the trend and counteract it whenever necessary. The drug that was never prescribed, the drug that was not necessary to take, is not only part of the solution to sustainable pharmaceutical management. It may also be a benefit for the patient and the environment. As such it is a necessary component in the realisation of the sustainable society.
