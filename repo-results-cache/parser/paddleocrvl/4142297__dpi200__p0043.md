#### 2.2. Molecular cloning – construction of the plasmids

In order to create enhanced DNA constructs of the GEVIs, the VSFPs were cloned behind the strong constitutive human cytomegalovirus (HCMV) or neuron-specific human synapsin-1 (hsyn1) promoters. Consequently, varieties of strategies were used. This chapter describes them briefly.

All DNA constructs were produced in One Shot $ ^{\circledR} $ TOP10F' E.coli strain (Invitrogen $ ^{TM} $), transforming them following the manufacturer's protocol. The positive clones were verified first by test-reaction with restriction enzymes. The cut DNA fragments have been separated by their size by the process of gel electrophoresis. The 1% gels were made of agarose (Sigma-Aldrich $ ^{\circledR} $) dissolved in 1x Tris/Acetate/EDTA (TAE) buffer. Before polymerisation, after the diluted agarose was poured into the chamber, a fluorescent nucleic acid stain (GelRed $ ^{TM} $, Biotium) was added in ratio 1:10000. The electrophoresis was performed at standard voltage conditions (8 V/cm). DNA fragments were visualised with an ultraviolet (UV) transilluminator. In the downstream procedures, the desired DNA fragments at proper position on the gel have been cut, diluted, and purified by GenElute $ ^{TM} $ Gel Extraction Kit (Sigma-Aldrich $ ^{\circledR} $), according to the manufacturer's protocol. The isolated plasmid DNA was resuspended in MilliQ water to a final concentration of 0.5 – 1.4  $ \mu $g/ $ \mu $l and stored at -20°C. Subsequently, they were verified by sequencing (Eurofins MWG Operon, Ebersberg, Germany).
