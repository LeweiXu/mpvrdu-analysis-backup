### 34. 04 Severance Pay

In cases where the Employer considers the employees are not trainable for available positions, those employees with 1680 hours worked may elect to terminate their employment with severance pay of one (1) weeks pay for each year of service up to a maximum of twelve (12) weeks pay.

### 34. 05 New Classifications

In the event changes in methods or equipment relevant to automation or new type equipment become necessary and the classifications contained in this Agreement are not applicable, the Employer and the Union will review and establish such new classifications as may be required.

### 34. 06 Individual Plants

It is agreed that all Clauses in Article 34 on Technological Change shall be on a basis of individual plants.
