### 1.3 National Sustainability Profile

A consultative process with civil society and other key stakeholders was used to complete the 2016 Burma Sustainability Index (SID). Civil society leaders consulted members of their organizations to determine scores for each of the domains and provided their feedback to the PEPFAR team in a half-day workshop. All meetings were held in the local language.
