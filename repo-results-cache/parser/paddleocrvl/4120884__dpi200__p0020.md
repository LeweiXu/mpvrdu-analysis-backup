### 2.1 Sand Casting

Sand casting is a moulding process in which bonded sand is used to form the moulding cavity. A sand mould consists of top and bottom sections called the cope and drag, respectively. The cope and drag contain a portion of the casting cavity and gating system (downsprue, runners and in-gates). Prior to pouring, the cope and drag are assembled in order to obtain the complete mould cavity. For components which require complex internal features, cores of bonded sand or steel are inserted into the sand mould.

One of the most important considerations in mould design is the gating system. The gating system in the sand mould is used to control the flow of molten metal in order to prevent turbulence and subsequent entrainment of inclusions, such as oxides, within the casting $ ^{[4]} $.
