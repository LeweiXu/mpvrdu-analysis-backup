### 7.2 Social Inclusion

It is crucial for a development plan to consider not only the needs of businesses but also those of regular citizens, so that people are not left behind and the benefits of economic growth can be shared by a large share of the population. The relatively high proportion of people receiving welfare payments in Limerick is a notable concern from this point of view. Indecon's analysis also shows that a significant share of the population in Limerick city is at risk of poverty and we estimate the number to be of the order of 10,527.
