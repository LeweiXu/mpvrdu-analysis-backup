Poetry • Trade Paperback • 9781582434070 $14.95 • 192 pages • 5.5" x 8.3"

In simple, striking verse, legendary poet Gary Snyder weaves an epic discourse on the topics of geology, prehistory, and mythology. First published in 1996, this landmark work encompasses Asian artistic traditions, as well as Native American storytelling and Zen Buddhist philosophy, and celebrates the disparate elements of the Earth—sky, rock, water—while exploring the human connection to nature with stunning wisdom. Winner of the Bollingen Prize for Poetry, the Robert Kirsch Lifetime Achievement Award, and the Orion Society's John Hay Award, among other honors, Snyder finds his quiet brilliance celebrated in this new edition of one of his most treasured works.
