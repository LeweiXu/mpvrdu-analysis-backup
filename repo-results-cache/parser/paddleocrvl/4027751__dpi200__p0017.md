#### 2.2.1 Servo Selection

To satisfy results of the previous torque calculations, three DS3225 digital servos (technical specifications can be found in the appendix A) were chosen for the base, shoulder, and elbow joints.

This servo was specifically chosen for these joints since its torque exceeds by a good amount the highest torque of the torque calculations summarized in table 2 above. This means that the servo will perform well under the harshest of scenarios i.e. when the actuators in the arm are subjected to the highest load torque or when the arm is fully stretched.

For the rest of the robotic arm, three MG90s servos were chosen, with one of them being used to open and close the gripper.
