For vector bundles this is equivalent to the concept of a double vector bundle in the sense of Pradines and Mackenzie.

## Theorem (Grabowski-Rotkiewicz)

The concept of a double vector bundle, understood as a particular double graded bundle in the above sense, coincides with that of Pradines.

All this can be extended to n-fold graded bundles in the obvious way:

## Definition

A n-fold graded bundle is a manifold equipped with n homogeneity structures  $ h^{1},\ldots,h^{n} $ which are compatible in the sense that

 $ h_{t}^{i}\circ h_{s}^{j}=h_{s}^{j}\circ h_{t}^{i}\quad for all s,t\inR\quadand\quad i,j=1,\ldots,n. $
