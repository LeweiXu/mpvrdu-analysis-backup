## Subtropical and subpolar gyre variability

The subpolar gyre (SPG) and the subtropical gyre (STG) are the dominant large-scale circulation patterns in the North Atlantic (Figure 4.9). To estimate their variability, we define a gyre-strength index as monthly anomalies of the barotropic stream function at its STG/SPG local extremum (Figure 4.10).
