## Ingredients for 90 lt/24 gal pan

▶ Pork neck (in cubes) 7.5 kg / 16.5 lbs

▶ Pork ribs (in cubes) 6 kg / 13.2 lbs

▶ Bacon (in cubes)

▶ Sausage (in cubes) 4,5 kg / 9,9 lbs

Black beans

3 kg / 6.6

▶ Onion ( julienne)

Garlic

Water

▶ Beans in water

Laurel

3 kg / 6.6 lbs

▶ Oil

300 g / 11 oz

9 kg / 18,3 lbs

12 It / 3 gal

Salt

▶ Pepper

9 It / 2,4 gal

18 leafes

## Procedure:

The day before put beans in cold water

TIP: keep the water of beans and use it to give color to the dish



<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_654_903_697_945.jpg" alt="Image" width="2%" /></div>


Preheat at  $ 220^{\circ} $C /  $ 428^{\circ} $F in braising mode

▶ Sear meat with oil for 6-8 minutes

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_655_1115_696_1158.jpg" alt="Image" width="2%" /></div>


TIP: to obtain a good searing result we recommend to not exceed the quantity indicated below

Add onion, and after 2 minutes add beans and water (of beans)



<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_1140_903_1181_944.jpg" alt="Image" width="2%" /></div>


TIP: to ensure the best performance wait that water boils before starting the pressure phase

▶ Close the lid and cook under pressure 📈 for 30 minutes

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_1140_1086_1179_1127.jpg" alt="Image" width="2%" /></div>


TIP: this dish can be served with rice
