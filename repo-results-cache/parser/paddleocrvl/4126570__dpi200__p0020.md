## Procedure:

Preheat at  $ 180^{\circ} $C /  $ 356^{\circ} $F in braising mode

▶ Add oil and onion

Stir for 1 minute, add octopus (cut in pieces) and stir for 1 minute more

▶ Add wine, capers, tomatoes and water

## Close the lid and cook under pressure ☐ for 15-30 minutes

<div style="text-align: center;"><img src="imgs/img_in_image_box_1140_902_1181_944.jpg" alt="Image" width="2%" /></div>


TIP: to ensure the best performance wait that sauce boils before starting the pressure phase

Note: total cooking time may vary depending on the size and weight of pieces.
