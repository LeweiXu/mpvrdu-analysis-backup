## Satellite Programme

The Indian remote sensing satellite constellation is used for various applications including Agricultural Crops Inventory, Agricultural Drought, Forest Fire, Landslides and Earthquakes monitoring, Groundwater Prospects Mapping, Inventory; Monitoring of Glacial Lakes/Water Bodies, Sericulture Development, and Satellite Aided Search and Rescue. It also supports tele-education and telemedicine, with the tele-education network connecting about 60,000 class rooms and the telemedicine network covering 380 hospitals. The Disaster Management Support (DMS) Programme of ISRO provides space-based information and services to strengthen disaster management activities, including monitoring flood events, supporting the Nepal Earthquake, and monitoring landslide dams in Jammu and Kashmir. The remote sensing data is also used for flood monitoring in 10 states and the generation of LIDAR-based flood depth maps for severe floods in Assam.
